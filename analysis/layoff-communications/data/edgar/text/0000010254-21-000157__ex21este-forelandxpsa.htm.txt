ARTICLE IX TAX MATTERS Section 9.1 Apportionment of Property Taxes . Property Taxes shall be deemed attributable to the period of ownership of the applicable Asset subject to Property Taxes, and liability for such Taxes shall be allocated to Seller for all periods (and portions thereof) ending prior to the Effective Time and to Buyer for all periods (and portions thereof) beginning at or after the Effective Time as described further in this Section 9.1 .
