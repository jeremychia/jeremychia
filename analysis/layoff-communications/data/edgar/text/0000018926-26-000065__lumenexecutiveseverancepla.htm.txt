Lumen Technologies, Inc. (“ Lumen ”) has established the Lumen Executive Severance Plan, effective as of October 22, 2026 (the “ Plan ”), to provide severance benefits for the Company’s senior executives (excluding the Company’s chief executive officer) upon a Non-CIC Termination or a CIC Termination, as each term is defined herein, who otherwise satisfy the terms and conditions of the Plan. The Plan is intended to be an unfunded “top hat” plan under Sections 201(2), 301(a)(3) and Section 401(a)(1) of ERISA. The Plan is administered by the Plan Administrator, with benefits provided in accordance with the provisions of this Plan document. The legal rights and obligations of any individual shall be determined solely by the provisions of the Plan, as interpreted by the Plan Administrator in the exercise of its sole and absolute discretion, including whether an individual shall be an Eligible Employee. Nothing in the Plan shall be construed to give any individual or Employee any right to continue in the employment of the Company or a Subsidiary or Affiliate of the Company. Section 1.02 Application of the Plan . The Plan supersedes any other plan, policy, program, or arrangement, including, but not limited to, benefits payable under the Lumen Severance Plan (known as the Lumen Executive Severance Plan prior to October 22, 2026), and provides | Exhibit 99.1 severance benefits in the event of (i) a Non-CIC Termination of an Eligible Employee and (ii) a CIC Termination of an Eligible Employee that occurs on and after October 22, 2026 (the “ Effective Date ”). Notwithstanding any other provision hereof, there is intended to be no duplication of severance benefits to an Eligible Employee. If an Eligible Employee has any vested rights under another severance or change in control plan, policy, arrangement or contract for severance benefits following termination of employment, the amount of such benefits an Eligible Employee becomes entitled to shall be offset against any amounts owing under the Plan. Section 1.03 Gender and Number. Whenever used herein, the masculine pronoun shall include the feminine and the singular shall encompass the plural, and vice versa. ARTICLE III ELIGIBILITY FOR SEVERANCE BENEFITS Section 3.01 Eligibility . (a) Eligibility for, and receipt of, any Severance Benefits under the Plan are expressly conditioned on the Eligible Employee’s (i) if a Non-CIC Termination, execution of a Separation and Release Agreement without revocation within the time period specified in Section 5.01 and compliance with all the terms and conditions of the Separation and Release; and (ii) compliance with all the terms and conditions of the restrictive covenants set forth in Article VI. (b) If the Plan Administrator determines that the Eligible Employee has not fully complied with any of the terms of the Plan or, if applicable, the Separation and Release Agreement, the Plan Administrator, acting on behalf of the Company, may deny Severance Benefits not yet paid or discontinue the payment of the Eligible Employee’s Severance Benefits and may require the Eligible Employee to repay any portion of any Severance Benefits already received under the Plan, by providing written notice of such repayment obligation to the Eligible Employee. If the Plan Administrator notifies an Eligible Employee that repayment of all or any portion of the Severance Benefits received under the Plan is required, such amounts shall be repaid within 30 calendar days of the date the written notice is sent. Any remedy under this subsection (b) shall be in addition to, and not in place of, any other remedy, including injunctive relief, that the Company may pursue. An Eligible Employee who incurs a Non-CIC Termination shall, subject to the terms and conditions of the Plan and contingent upon the Eligible Employee timely executing a Separation and Release Agreement (without revocation), receive the Non-CIC Cash Severance Benefit paid in accordance with Article V. Section 4.02 CIC Termination Cash Severance Benefit . An Eligible Employee who incurs a CIC Termination shall, subject to the terms and conditions of the Plan, receive the CIC Cash Severance Benefit, paid in accordance with Article V. The foregoing notwithstanding, if the Eligible Employee has a deferral election in effect with respect to any percentage of the annual bonus which would otherwise become payable with respect to the fiscal year in which termination occurs, then the CIC Cash Severance Benefit shall be reduced by an amount equal to the deferred amount which reduction amount shall be deferred in accordance with such election. Section 4.03 Additional Severance Benefits . An Eligible Employee who incurs a Non-CIC Termination or a CIC Termination will receive the following additional Severance Benefits, subject to the terms of the Plan and, if a Non-CIC Termination, contingent upon the Eligible Employee timely executing a Separation and Release (without revocation): (a) Employee Benefits . Medical (including prescription drug), vision and dental benefits for the Eligible Employee and the Eligible Employee’s dependents, if enrolled in the Company’s Health Care Plan at the time of Termination, will continue through the last day of the month in which the Termination occurs. Eligibility for life insurance coverage and short-term disability coverage and benefits end on the Termination Date; provided, however , in accordance with applicable laws and the respective plan documents, notice of any rights to continue, convert, or obtain replacement coverage will be provided to Eligible Employees. (b) COBRA Continuation Coverage . Eligible Employees, and if applicable, their dependents, who participate in the Company’s Health Care Plan may elect under COBRA to continue coverage for a monthly charge beyond the date coverage would otherwise terminate, subject to the provisions of the applicable plans and COBRA (“ COBRA Continuation Coverage ”). Employees who wish to enroll in COBRA Continuation Coverage must return the COBRA enrollment forms within 60 days from receipt of the COBRA notification by the Company. Eligible Employees who elect COBRA Continuation Coverage following a Non-CIC Termination or a CIC Termination will be eligible for subsidized COBRA payments (“ COBRA Subsidy ”) during the COBRA Subsidy Period. The COBRA Subsidy means that the Eligible Employee will pay the same premium contribution rate for medical/prescription, vision and dental coverage during the COBRA Subsidy Period. The COBRA Subsidy will terminate upon the earliest of: (i) the date the Page 12 Exhibit 99.1 Eligible Employee discontinues COBRA Continuation Coverage or otherwise becomes ineligible for COBRA Continuation Coverage, (ii) the date the Eligible Employee is eligible to enroll (regardless of whether the Eligible Employee actually enrolls) in medical coverage sponsored by a subsequent employer of the Eligible Employee, or (iii) the date the COBRA Subsidy Period expires. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if (1) any plan pursuant to which COBRA Continuation Coverage is provided is not, or ceases prior to the expiration of the continuation coverage period to be, exempt from the application of Code Section 409A under Treasury Regulation Section 1.409A-1(a)(5), (2) the Company is otherwise unable to continue to cover the Eligible Employee or the Eligible Employee’s dependents under its group health plans, or (3) the Company cannot provide COBRA Continuation Coverage without violating applicable law (including, without limitation, Section 2716 of the Public Health Service Act), then, in any such case, an amount equal to each remaining COBRA Subsidy shall thereafter be paid to the Eligible Employee in substantially equal monthly installments over the COBRA Subsidy Period (or remaining portion thereof). Following the expiration of the COBRA Subsidy Period, if the Eligible Employee is eligible to continue healthcare coverage in accordance with COBRA, such coverage will be at the Eligible Employee’s expense. (c) Annual Bonus . (i) Payment in Lieu of STI Program Payment for Non-CIC-Termination . If during the year of Termination the Eligible Employee who incurs a Non-CIC Termination has at least three (3) months of consecutive employment, in lieu of the annual incentive bonus that may be payable under the STI Program, the Company will pay the Eligible Employee, a lump sum cash payment (subject to withholding) equal to the Eligible Employee’s last applicable Targeted Bonus Opportunity for the year of Termination prorated for the portion of the year the Eligible Employee is employed from January 1 of the Termination year through the Eligible Employee’s Termination Date, adjusted by the Corporate Performance Factor (as defined in the STI Program), capped at 100%, multiplied by 0.90, to be paid following the Determination and Approval Period as defined in the STI Program. In addition, if the Company has not paid an Eligible Employee (or deferred in accordance with any effective deferral election by the Employee) the annual bonus earned under the STI Program for the completed fiscal year ending immediately prior to the fiscal year of a non-CIC Termination, the Eligible Employee will be paid the determined bonus amount in a lump sum cash payment (subject to withholding) at the same time as the Company pays the STI Program bonuses to active Employees participating in the STI Program in accordance with the terms of the STI Program. (ii) Payment of STI Program Payment for CIC Termination . If the Termination of the Eligible Employee is a CIC Termination, then the Company shall pay to the Eligible Employee in a lump sum cash payment, as promptly as practicable but in no event later than sixty (60) days following the CIC Termination date, a pro rata annual performance bonus for the year of Termination. If the applicable bonus performance period is shorter than a fiscal year, the foregoing provisions shall be applied by substituting the applicable performance period and number of days.
