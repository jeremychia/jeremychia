("ALLETE") Board of Directors (the "Board") elected Charlene A. Thomas to serve on the Board, effective October 4, 2021. Ms. Thomas was appointed to serve on the Audit Committee. Ms. Thomas will receive compensation for her Board service consistent with the compensation received by ALLETE’s other non-employee directors, as disclosed in Exhibit 10(k)(3) to ALLETE's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020. Since January 2021, Ms. Thomas has served as the chief diversity, equity and inclusion officer for United Parcel Service, Inc. (“UPS”) and is a member of UPS’s executive leadership team. Ms. Thomas has progressed through successive leadership roles in U.S. operations since she began her career with UPS in 1989. Prior to assuming her current role, Ms. Thomas was the chief human resources officer for UPS, leading strategic human resources initiatives for a workforce of what is currently more than 540,000 employees. She also previously served as president of UPS’s west region with responsibility for product growth and delivery operations in 25 U.S. central and western states.
