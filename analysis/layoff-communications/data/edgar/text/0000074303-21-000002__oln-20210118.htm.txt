Item 2.05. Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On January 18, 2021, Olin Corporation (“Olin”) approved a plan to cease operations of its Trichloroethylene (“Tric”) and Anhydrous Hydrogen Chloride (“AnHCl”) liquefaction facilities in Freeport, Texas, before the end of 2021. As a result of this action, Olin expects to incur approximately $23 million of pretax restructuring costs, approximately $2 million of which is expected to be incurred in 2021. Olin currently estimates these restructuring costs will consist of $21 million of facility exit costs and $2 million of contract termination costs. Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure. Attached as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference is a copy of Olin’s press release dated January 19, 2021 announcing that it has made the decision to cease operations of its Tric and AnHCl liquefaction facilities in Freeport, Texas before the end of 2021.
