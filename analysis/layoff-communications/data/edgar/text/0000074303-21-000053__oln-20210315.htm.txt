Item 2.05. Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On March 15, 2021, Olin Corporation (“Olin”) approved a plan to permanently cease operations of approximately 50% of its diaphragm-grade chlor alkali capacity (approximately 200,000 tons) at its McIntosh, Alabama facility. The closure is expected to be completed by March 31, 2021. As a result of this action, Olin expects to incur approximately $10 million of pretax restructuring costs, approximately $5 million of which is expected to be incurred in the first quarter of 2021. Olin currently estimates these restructuring costs will consist of $7 million in contract termination costs and $3 million in facility exit costs to be paid in cash through 2031. Item 7.01.
