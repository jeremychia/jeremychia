A copy of the Corporation’s supplementary financial information is included in this Report as Exhibit 99.2 and is furnished herewith. Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 6, 2023, as part of ongoing cost reduction initiatives, the Corporation committed to a workforce reduction expected to reduce its workforce by approximately 4 percent. Affected employees were informed of the workforce reduction beginning on October 6, 2023. The Corporation expects to incur one-time, pre-tax charges and costs associated with these actions of approximately $150 million, primarily related to cash severance, benefits and related termination costs. The Corporation expects that the majority of the charges will be incurred in the fourth quarter of 2023. The Corporation anticipates that implementation of the workforce reduction will be almost entirely complete in the fourth quarter of 2023, and will reduce personnel expenses by approximately $325 million, or 5 percent, annually (estimated based on the annualized pre-reduction personnel expense of affected employees, compared to total personnel expense).
