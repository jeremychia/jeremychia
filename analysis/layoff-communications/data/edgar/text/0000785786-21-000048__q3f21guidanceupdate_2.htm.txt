EX-99.1 2 q3f21guidanceupdate_2.htm EX-99.1 Document Plexus to Provide Financial Update Regarding the Impacts of Malaysia COVID-19 Restrictions at Stifel Investor Conference NEENAH, WI – June 07, 2021 - Plexus (NASDAQ: PLXS) intends to provide an update during a live webcast on Tuesday, June 8, 2021 at Stifel’s 2021 Virtual Cross Sector Insight Conference regarding the impact of the Malaysian government’s COVID-19 workforce curtailment as well as other evolving dynamics on its fiscal third quarter results and future outlook. Plexus’ focus remains on ensuring the health and safety of its team members and on supporting its customers as best as possible amidst a challenging and fluid situation.
