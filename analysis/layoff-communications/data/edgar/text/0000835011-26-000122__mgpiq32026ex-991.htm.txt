(Nasdaq: CASK) on November 2 ATCHISON, Kan., October 28, 2026 - MGP Ingredients, Inc. (Nasdaq: MGPI), a leading provider of branded and distilled spirits and food ingredient solutions, which will change its name to Sempreon, Inc. and its ticker symbol to CASK on November 2, 2026, today reported results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. During the quarter, the company continued to execute against its strategic roadmap, reducing total debt by $28.4 million and delivering adjusted EBITDA and adjusted basic EPS ahead of its expectations. The net debt leverage ratio was approximately 3.6x at September 30, 2026. Branded Spirits Third quarter 2026 versus prior year – Sales of $58.2 million decreased 4% versus $60.7 million. – Gross profit of $29.2 million decreased 9% versus $32.2 million. – Gross margin of 50.2% decreased by 280 basis points versus 53.0%. Key developments versus prior year – Excluding the other products category, which consists primarily of international sales of private label bottled products, sales were in line with the prior year and were negatively impacted by distributor system cutovers. – Premium plus sales decreased by 1%. Penelope Bourbon sales increased 12%. – Combined s ales of the mid- and value-priced portfolios improved slightly , with mid-priced offerings growing 6%, led by Ezra Brooks 90 Proof, Exotico tequila and Juarez tequila. Distilling Solutions Third quarter 2026 versus prior year – Sales of $29.0 million decreased 29% versus $40.9 million. – Gross profit of $9.4 million decreased 34% versus $14.2 million. – Gross margin of 32.4% decreased by 230 basis points versus 34.7%. Key developments versus prior year – As expected, elevated industry-wide barrel inventories resulted in a 47% decline in brown goods sales. – Sales of white goods and other co-products increased 32% primarily due to higher sales volumes. – Warehouse services revenue increased 3%. – Higher white goods production volumes and cost savings initiatives led to lower distillation costs, partially offsetting the impact of lower brown goods sales. Ingredient Solutions Third quarter 2026 versus prior year – Sales of $30.8 million increased 5% versus $29.3 million. – Gross profit of $1.8 million decreased 39% versus $3.0 million. – Gross margin of 6.0% decreased 430 basis points versus 10.3%. Key developments versus prior year – Sales growth was driven by higher sales of specialty wheat proteins, including textured proteins, and biofuel and other byproducts, partially offset by lower specialty wheat starch sales volumes primarily due to longer-than-expected downtime following a scheduled plant shutdown. – Higher waste starch stream costs pressured profitability, partially offset by an initial insurance recovery payment related to a 2025 equipment outage. 2 2026 Financial Outlook MGP reaffirmed its consolidated guidance for fiscal 2026: • Sales projected to be in the range of $480 million to $500 million. • Adjusted EBITDA expected to be between $90 million to $98 million. • Adjusted basic EPS expected to be in the $1.50 to 1.80 range, with weighted average basic shares outstanding of approximately 21.4 million. • An effective tax rate of approximately 23%. • Full-year capital expenditures expected to be approximately $20 million. About MGP Ingredients, Inc. MGP Ingredients Inc. (Nasdaq: MGPI) has been formulating excellence since 1941 by bringing product ideas to life across the alcoholic beverage and specialty ingredient industries through three segments: Branded Spirits, Distilling Solutions and Ingredient Solutions. MGPI is one of the leading spirits distillers with an award-winning portfolio of premium brands including Penelope, Rebel, Remus and Yellowstone bourbons and El Mayor tequila, under the Luxco umbrella. With distilleries in Indiana and Kentucky; a tequila distillery in Arandas, Mexico; and bottling operations in Missouri, Ohio and Northern Ireland, the company creates distilled spirits for customers including many world-renowned spirits brands. In addition, the company’s high-quality specialty fiber, protein and starch ingredients provide functional, nutritional and sensory solutions for a wide range of food products. On November 2, 2026, MGP Ingredients, Inc. will become Sempreon, Inc. To learn more visit MGPIngredients.com .
