Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results; Updates 2026 Guidance Highlights (All comparisons are to the prior year period unless otherwise noted) : • Net sales of $561.7 million for the quarter, up 3.3% • Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) of $1.98, down 20% • Adjusted diluted EPS* of $2.08, down 21% • Generated $64.6 million of cash from operating activities; repurchased $15 million of shares • Updates its full-year guidance for 2026 COLMAR, PA (October 26, 2026) – Dorman Products, Inc. (the “Company” or “Dorman”) (NASDAQ: DORM), a leading supplier in the motor vehicle aftermarket industry, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 26, 2026. Kevin Olsen, Dorman’s Chairman, President, and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “We delivered solid performance in the quarter. Net sales increased 3%, with top-line growth across each of our three business segments driven by new products and business wins. Diluted EPS was $1.98, and adjusted diluted EPS was $2.08, compared to $2.48 and $2.62, respectively, in the prior-year period. The year-over-year earnings comparison reflects the timing of tariff-related pricing that benefitted the third quarter of 2025. We also generated $65 million of operating cash flow and repurchased $15 million of our common stock in the quarter, bringing year-to-date share repurchases to a company record of $112 million. “Given our performance through the first nine months of the year and additional pricing actions we expect to take in the fourth quarter, we are narrowing our full-year 2026 guidance. We now expect full-year net sales growth to be in the range of 2% to 3%, diluted EPS in the range of $7.95 to $8.15, and adjusted diluted EPS in the range of $8.50 to $8.70. “Supported by our strong balance sheet and liquidity position, and the strategic advantages of our innovation engine and diversified supplier network, we remain confident in our long-term growth strategy.” Third Quarter Financial Results The Company reported third quarter 2026 net sales of $561.7 million, up 3.3% from net sales of $543.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit was $221.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 39.4% of net sales, compared to $241.4 million, or 44.4% of net sales, in the same quarter last year. Adjusted gross profit* was $221.2 million, or 39.4% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $241.4 million, or 44.4% of net sales, in the same quarter last year. Selling, general, and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses were $138.7 million, or 24.7% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $135.7 million, or 25.0% of net sales, in the same quarter last year. Adjusted SG&A expenses* were $132.4 million, or 23.6% of net sales, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $129.9 million, or 23.9% of net sales, in the same quarter last year. Diluted EPS was $1.98 in the third quarter of 2026, down 20% from diluted EPS of $2.48 in the same quarter last year. Adjusted diluted EPS* was $2.08 in the third quarter of 2026, down 21% compared to adjusted diluted EPS* of $2.62 in the same quarter last year. About Dorman Products Dorman gives professionals, enthusiasts, and owners greater freedom to fix motor vehicles. For over 100 years, we have been driving new solutions, releasing tens of thousands of aftermarket replacement products engineered to save time and money and increase convenience and reliability. Founded and headquartered in the United States, we are a pioneering global organization offering an always-evolving catalog of products covering cars, trucks, and specialty vehicles, from chassis to body, from underhood to undercarriage, and from hardware to complex electronics. *Non-GAAP Measures In addition to the financial measures prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), this earnings release also contains Non-GAAP financial measures.
