Net income applicable to Morgan Stanley was $4.3 billion, or $2.60 per diluted share, 1 compared with $3.4 billion, or $2.02 per diluted share, 1 for the same period a year ago. Ted Pick, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, said, “The Integrated Firm delivered a very strong quarter with record net revenues of $17.7 billion and EPS of $2.60, and an ROTCE of 23.0%. Institutional Securities strong performance was led by our Markets business with Equity reporting a record $4.1 billion in revenues. Total client assets of $7.7 trillion across Wealth and Investment Management were supported by $94 billion in net new assets.
