Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and convey to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, the Company Securities. 1.2 Estimated Purchase Price . (a) Subject to the adjustment set forth in Section 2.3 , the aggregate consideration to be paid by Buyer to Seller at the Closing for the Company Securities shall be an amount equal to the following: (i) $208,000,000.00 (the “ Base Amount ”); 1 (ii) minus Estimated Closing Date Debt; (iii) plus the Estimated Closing Date Cash; (iv) plus the Estimated Closing Date Net Working Capital Adjustment; (v) minus the Estimated Company Transaction Expenses; (vi) plus the Estimated Rollover PTO Amount . The net amount determined pursuant to clauses (i) – (vi) above, which shall be determined in accordance with Section 2.3(a) , is referred to herein as the “ Estimated Purchase Price. ” The Estimated Purchase Price is subject to adjustment after Closing as set forth in this Agreement. (b) At Closing, Buyer shall pay, or cause to be paid: (i) to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller, the Estimated Purchase Price; (ii) on behalf of the Company, all amounts necessary to discharge fully the outstanding balance of the aggregate amount of Funded Indebtedness as of the Closing, if any; (iii) on behalf of the Company, the Estimated Company Transaction Expenses to the applicable recipients set forth on the Estimated Closing Statement; provided , however , that the Buyer shall cause the Company to promptly pay the amount of any sale, change of control or similar bonuses or other compensatory amounts included in the Estimated Company Transaction Expenses after the Closing to the respective recipients through the Company’s payroll system less applicable withholding Taxes, and Buyer shall cause the Company to timely fund and satisfy all applicable withholding, deposit, remittance, reporting and filing obligations relating thereto; and (iv) pay all Buyer Transaction Expenses. 1.3 Rollover PTO Amount . At Closing, Seller shall, or shall cause its Affiliates to, pay each applicable Continuing Employee his or her respective portion of the Rollover PTO Amount, less applicable withholding Taxes, and Seller shall, or shall cause its Affiliates to, timely pay and satisfy all applicable withholding, deposit, remittance, reporting and filing obligations relating to the foregoing.
