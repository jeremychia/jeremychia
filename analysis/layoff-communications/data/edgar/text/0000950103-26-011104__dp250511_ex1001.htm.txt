WHEREAS , as of the date hereof, the Debtors remain in possession of their property and continue to operate and manage their business as debtors in possession pursuant to Sections 1107(a) and 1108 of the Bankruptcy Code; WHEREAS , the Transactions contemplated by this Agreement are subject to approval by the Bankruptcy Court and will be consummated pursuant to the Bid Procedures Order and the Sale Order to be entered in the Bankruptcy Cases;
