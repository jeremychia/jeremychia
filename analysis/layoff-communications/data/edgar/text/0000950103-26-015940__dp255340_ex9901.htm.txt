(AKZA; AKZOY) publishes results for Q3 2026 Highlights Q3 2026 (compared with Q3 2025) • Organic sales growth of +2% on pricing; revenue flat • Operating income impacted by Russia deconsolidation and Pakistan divestment • Organic adjusted EBITDA growth 1 of €15 million • Adjusted EBITDA margin increased to 15.3% (2025: 15.1%) • Net cash from operating activities positive €187 million (2025: €331 million) • Binding agreements signed to sell Deco Paints South East Asia for $1.35 billion, at 21x FY25 EBITDA AkzoNobel CEO Greg Poux-Guillaume commented: “AkzoNobel delivered another strong quarter, with organic sales and adjusted EBITDA both increasing. Our sixth consecutive quarter of margin expansion confirms our ability to realize pricing in challenging markets and to deliver on our ambitious cost savings plan. “We also announced the divestment of our Decorative Paints business in South East Asia, which successfully concludes the strategic review of our Paints portfolio in Asia. At an enterprise value of 21x FY25 EBITDA – more than twice our current trading multiple – it unlocks significant value, as did the previous sale of our liquid coatings businesses in India for 25x EBITDA.
