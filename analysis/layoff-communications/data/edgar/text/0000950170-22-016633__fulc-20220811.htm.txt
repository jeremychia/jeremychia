Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities On August 11, 2022, Fulcrum also announced the approval by its board of directors of a plan to strategically realign its operations and implement cost reduction measures to prioritize its two clinical programs. Fulcrum plans to streamline its research and development activities and redeploy resources to focus on its two development-stage programs that are expected to drive near-term value. The realignment plan will result in a reduction of 18 positions primarily in the areas of discovery, research and general and administrative. Fulcrum expects to complete the workforce reduction in August 2022. The realignment plan is expected to result in savings in operating expenses of approximately $40-$50 million, which is expected to be fully realized by mid-2024. Fulcrum currently estimates that it will incur cash charges of approximately $0.4-$0.5 million related to the realignment plan consisting of one-time termination benefits. The charges Fulcrum expects to incur in connection with this reduction in force are subject to a number of assumptions, risks and uncertainties, and actual results may materially differ. Fulcrum may also incur other material charges not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, these actions.
