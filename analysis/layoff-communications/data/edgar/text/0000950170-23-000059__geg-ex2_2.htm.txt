(a) Subject to Section 2.5 , in consideration for the sale of the Purchased Interests pursuant to Section 2.1(b) , the Purchaser shall pay an aggregate amount equal to (i) the Base Price, less (ii) the Estimated Closing Date Debt, less (iii) the Estimated Closing Date Transaction Expenses, less (iv) the Estimated Working Capital Deficit, if any, plus (v) the Estimated Closing Date Cash (such calculated amount, the “ Purchase Price ”).
