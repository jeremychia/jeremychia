As previously disclosed in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed by Organovo Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) on August 22, 2023, as part of a reduction in force, on August 16, 2023, Tom Jurgensen, the former General Counsel and Corporate Secretary of the Company, was notified that his employment with the Company would be terminated. Mr. Jurgensen’s last day of employment with the Company was August 25, 2023. On September 7, 2023, in connection with Mr. Jurgensen’s termination, the Company entered into a Separation Agreement and General Release (the “Separation Agreement”) with Mr. Jurgensen, to be effective as of September 15, 2023. Pursuant to the Separation Agreement, Mr. Jurgensen released any claims against the Company and the Company will pay Mr. Jurgensen an aggregate of $345,000 (less applicable federal, state, and local withholdings), in three separate installment payments of $115,000 on or before September 28, 2023, October 16, 2023 and January 5, 2024. The foregoing description of the Separation Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Separation Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
