Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On May 14, 2024, Tenaya announced cost containment measures, including a committed plan to reduce its workforce (the “Workforce Reduction”). The cost containment measures align with Tenaya’s focus on generating data from its clinical-stage gene therapy programs. Employees impacted by the Workforce Reduction were notified on May 13, 2024, and represent approximately 22% of the workforce. In connection with the Workforce Reduction, Tenaya estimates that it will incur approximately $1.3 million to $1.5 million, of aggregate charges, primarily related to employee cash severance and continuing health benefits, which costs are expected to be substantially recognized during the second quarter of 2024. The foregoing estimates that Tenaya expects to incur in connection with the Workforce Reduction are contingent upon various assumptions and actual results may differ. Tenaya may also incur additional costs not currently contemplated due to events related to or resulting from the Workforce Reduction. A copy of the press release announcing the cost containment measures is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.
