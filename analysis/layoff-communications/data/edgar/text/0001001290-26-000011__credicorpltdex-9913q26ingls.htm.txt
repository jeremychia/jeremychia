(“Credicorp” or “the Company”) (NYSE: BAP | BVL: BAP), the leading financial services holding company in Peru with a presence in Chile, Colombia, Bolivia, and Panama today reported its unaudited results for the three- and nine-months ended September 30, 2026. Financial results are expressed in Soles and are presented in accordance with IFRS. 3Q26 OPERATING AND FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS • Net income attributable to Credicorp reached S/1,929.4 million, up 11.0% YoY and down 2.6% QoQ, resulting in ROE of 19.9%, driven by accelerating loan growth, healthy asset quality and diversified revenue growth fueled by strong transactional activity and continued investment in digital capabilities. • Total loans measured in quarter-end balances increased 14.8% YoY (15.2% FX-neutral) and 4.2% QoQ (4.0% FX-neutral). YoY growth was led by BCP’s Retail Banking, particularly Consumer and SME-Pyme, followed by Wholesale Banking, and to a lesser extent Mibanco. QoQ expansion reflected growth in BCP’s same segments and at Mibanco, where disbursements reached a record high in September. • Total Deposits increased 14.3% YoY (14.8% FX-neutral) but declined 0.6% QoQ (0.9% FX-neutral). YoY growth was supported mainly by low-cost deposits, which represented 73.2% of total deposits and 62.0% of the funding base. The sequential decline reflected the normalization of a temporary Wholesale demand deposit recorded at 2Q26-end and partial migration from demand to time deposits. • Net Interest Income (NII) increased 17.7% YoY and 6.0% QoQ, primarily driven by loan growth at BCP and Mibanco, particularly in higher-yielding segments. Net Interest Margin (NIM) expanded 36 bps YoY to 6.9%, as the yield on interest-earning assets rose to 8.8% and growth in low-cost deposits reduced the funding cost to 2.2%. • Asset Qualit y continued to improve, with the NPL ratio declining -91 bps YoY and -15 bps QoQ to 3.9%. Performance registered stronger origination, monitoring and collections capabilities, particularly at BCP Stand-alone, reflecting positive payment behavior and healthy vintages. • Provisions increased 37.4% YoY and 13.3% QoQ, reflecting additional provisioning after the El Niño risk assessment evolved to a “Very Strong” base scenario at quarter-end, with a Cost of Risk standing at 2.0%. YoY, underlying provisions increased alongside loan growth at BCP and Mibanco, reflecting a greater contribution from higher-yielding portfolios. Cost of Risk stood at 2.0%, NPL coverage strengthened to 120.9%, while Risk-Adjusted NIM improved 13 bps to 5.7%. • Other Core Income increased 18.8% YoY and 3.2% QoQ, rising to 29.3% of total risk-adjusted income as Credicorp continues to diversify revenues. Growth was led by increases of 20.4% and 14.3% in fee income and FX gains, respectively. • Insurance Underwriting Result declined 6.7% YoY, reflecting a tough comparison in Life’s D&S line following SISCO reserve releases in 3Q25, partially offset by stronger P&C results. Credicorp has strengthened its leadership in the Andean region through a strategy anchored in digital transformation and innovation, talent development, and the integration of sustainability into the core of its business. The company has transitioned into an integrated financial ecosystem—one where the whole is increasingly greater than the sum of its parts—supported by four differentiated growth levers: 1. Prioritizing Leading Market Positions in Underpenetrated Markets with Clear Growth Avenues Credicorp holds leading positions across mature core businesses in markets that remain structurally underpenetrated. In Peru, low levels of financial inclusion and insurance coverage, together with high informality, continue to create significant room to expand access to financial, payment, insurance, and remittance products and services. This provides a long runway for sustainable growth. 2. Scaling an Integrated Digital Ecosystem Backed by its innovation portfolio, Credicorp has evolved into an integrated financial ecosystem. The combination of complementary capabilities differentiated distribution channels, and a broad client base allow us to strengthen engagement, accelerate product adoption through cross-selling, and deepen integration across businesses. We will continue to broaden access to financial products and services to advance financial inclusion, with the ambition of incorporating 8 million people into the financial system by 2028. 3. Unlocking Synergies by Leveraging Shared Capabilities Across the Ecosystem Shared capabilities in technology, data, analytics, artificial intelligence, and risk management constitute a competitive advantage to accelerate value creation at scale. These capabilities strengthen decision-making, enable more personalized customer experiences, generate productivity gains and accelerate innovation, supporting further monetization across the ecosystem. 4. Delivering Strong, Resilient Returns Across Economic Cycles With decades of disciplined execution, Credicorp has demonstrated its capacity to generate value across economic cycles. The combination of market knowledge and leadership, business diversification, and ecosystem-based competitive advantages strengthen our resilience and supports a sustainable trajectory of profitable growth and long-term value creation.
