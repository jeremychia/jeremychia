IAMGOLD Corporation, as borrower, the lenders parties thereto, as lenders, National Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent, and National Bank of Canada Capital Markets and Royal Bank of Canada as co-lead arrangers and joint bookrunners, are parties to the second amended and restated credit agreement dated as of December 20, 2024 (as amended from time to time prior to the date hereof, the " Existing Credit Agreement "), pursuant to which the Lenders made available to the Borrower a US $650,000,000 revolving credit facility. B. The Lenders, the Agent and the Borrower wish to further amend and restate in its entirety the Existing Credit Agreement in order to, among other things, (i) increase the maximum amount of the revolving credit facility from US $650,000,000 to US $850,000,000, (ii) extend the maturity date of the revolving credit facility for a period of four (4) years following the Effective Date, (iii) amend certain restrictive covenants, and (iv) make certain other modifications to the Existing Credit Agreement, as further set out herein.
