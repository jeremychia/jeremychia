(the "Company") ( OTCID:AITX ), a developer and operator of AI-driven security and productivity solutions for enterprise clients, today announced the first step in its balance sheet stability program. CEO/CTO and founder Steve Reinharz has moved to a full salary deferral and committed to receive no salary or bonus payments through February 28, 2027, the end of the Company's fiscal year. Milestone 1 and the CEO's Salary The Company's first breakeven milestone, monthly operating cash breakeven, is reached when monthly revenue, as recognized, exceeds operating cash expenses paid to operate the Company. It excludes debt service and inventory purchases for device builds. The Company's internal target for Milestone 1 remains January 2027. Salary paid in cash is part of that operating cash expenses. Mr. Reinharz's annual base salary is $420,000. Effective with the Company's October 15, 2026 payroll, he has deferred 100% of it, which keeps approximately $35,000 per month of salary out of operating cash paid. His commitment covers the period in which the Company targets Milestone 1. As of the date of this release, he has deferred approximately $51,000 of his salary this fiscal year. During the same period, he has also personally paid more than $100,000 in Company business expenses that have not been reimbursed. "I'm so focused on hitting these milestones and this helps," said Reinharz. "No salary and no bonus through the end of the fiscal year means more cash stays in the business while we work toward milestone one. This is the first step in a larger plan to stabilize our balance sheet, and we'll share the next steps as they're finalized." Two Levers: Revenue and Cost For its fiscal second quarter ended August 31, 2026, the Company reported revenue of $1,985,604, up 8% sequentially and 5% year over year. Revenue from customers other than its largest grew 61% year over year. Recurring device rental revenue represented 89% of total revenue. On cost, the Company targets cash spend reductions of approximately $345,000 per month by January 2027 and more than $350,000 per month from February 2027, measured against its August 2026 run-rate. The program includes salary deferrals and a reduction in force of approximately 7 positions in August 2026 and 17 positions in October 2026. It also includes reductions in marketing, promotion, cloud and other operating costs. The Company's sales force was not affected. "We have two levers, revenue and cost, and we're pulling both," added Reinharz. "Reaching milestone one would show more self-sustainability than this Company has ever had. It's also the step we hope opens the door to lower-cost, non-dilutive inventory financing." Next Steps The Company expects to announce additional steps in the balance sheet stability program as they are finalized. It intends to report progress against the milestones in future updates. Important Information About the Deferral and the Milestones Deferred salary reduces cash spend, not total expenses. It remains an obligation of the Company and has not been forgiven. Mr. Reinharz's commitment covers salary and bonus payments only. The Company may reimburse some or all of these outstanding expenses before the end of the fiscal year. The Company's projection for Milestone 1 assumes revenue growth of approximately 2.7% per month, the pace implied by its second-quarter sequential growth. The Company described four internal breakeven milestones on October 26, 2026 . Each applies a stricter test than the one before it. All four are internal management measures, not measures under generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP"), and their targets are not projections or guarantees. Important Information Regarding Projections and Management Measures The January 2027 monthly operating cash breakeven projection and the second calendar quarter 2027 all in cash breakeven projection are management estimates based on current assumptions. They are not results already achieved, guarantees of future performance or projections of profitability under generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP"). As used in this release, monthly operating cash breakeven compares projected monthly revenue with projected monthly cash operating expenditures, excluding debt service and device build expenditures. The all in cash breakeven projection additionally includes scheduled debt service and anticipated device build expenditures. These management measures differ from GAAP net income and net cash provided by or used in operating activities and should not be considered substitutes for those measures. Revenue recognized in a month does not necessarily equal cash collected in that month. Collection timing and other working capital requirements may therefore result in a need for financing even when projected revenue exceeds the expenditures included in these comparisons. There can be no assurance that such financing will be available on acceptable terms or at all. The internal targets assume approximately 2.7% monthly revenue growth, implementation and implementation of the cash spend reductions, and the product contributions described in this release. The assumed revenue growth rate exceeds the Company's growth over the preceding twelve months. Recent sequential growth does not establish that this rate will continue. Projected cash spend reductions are measured against the Company's August 2026 monthly expense run rate and include both expense reductions and changes in payment timing. Salary deferrals and deferred compensation remain obligations of the Company and do not constitute permanent expense reductions or forgiveness of amounts owed. Payment of those obligations will require cash or other settlement and may affect the timing or continuation of either projected breakeven milestone. Achievement of either milestone would not, by itself, establish GAAP profitability, eliminate accumulated liabilities or assure that the Company can fund all future requirements without additional financing. The Company continues to depend on outside financing, which may be dilutive.
