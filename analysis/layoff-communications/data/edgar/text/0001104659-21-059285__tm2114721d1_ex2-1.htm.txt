For federal income tax purposes, the Merger is intended to constitute a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368 of the Code and (b) depending on the facts at the time of the Closing, the Merger and the PIPE Investment are intended to be considered part of an overall plan in which the Company Stockholders exchange their shares of Company Common Stock for the Stockholder Merger Consideration in an exchange described in Section 351 of the Code.
