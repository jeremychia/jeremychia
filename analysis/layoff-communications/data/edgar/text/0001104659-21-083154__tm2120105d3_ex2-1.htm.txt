The fees and expenses of the Valuation Firm shall be allocated to and borne by the Buyer, on the one hand, and the Sellers, jointly and severally, on the other hand, in inverse proportion as they may prevail on the objections to the Closing Statement, which proportionate allocations shall be calculated on an aggregate basis based on the relative dollar values of all such objections and shall be determined by the Valuation Firm and included in its written decision. For example, if the objections to the Closing Statement total $1,000 and the Valuation Firm awards $600 in favor of the Buyer’s position and $400 in favor of the Sellers’ position, 60% (i.e., 600 ÷ 1,000) of the fees and expenses of the Valuation Firm would be borne by the Sellers, jointly and severally, and 40% (i.e., 400 ÷ 1,000) would be borne by the Buyer.
