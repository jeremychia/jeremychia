Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. In response to the ongoing adverse effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on Redbox Entertainment Inc.’s (the “Company”) results of operations, the Company entered into a series of actions and initiatives to reduce its cost structure and streamline its operations, which included a reduction in force of 150 employees completed on March 29, 2022. The Company estimates that the workforce reduction will decrease its annual operating costs by approximately $13.1 million, and that it will incur one-time restructuring charges of approximately $3.8 million, the substantial amount of which will be related to severance.
