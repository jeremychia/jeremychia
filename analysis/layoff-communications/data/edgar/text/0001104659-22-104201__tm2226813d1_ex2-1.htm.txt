RECITALS WHEREAS , SPAC is a blank check company incorporated as an exempted company in the Cayman Islands for the purpose of acquiring one or more operating businesses through a Business Combination. WHEREAS , Merger Sub is a newly incorporated, wholly-owned, direct Subsidiary of the Company that was formed for purposes of consummating the transactions contemplated by this Agreement and the other Transaction Agreements (the “ Transactions ”).
