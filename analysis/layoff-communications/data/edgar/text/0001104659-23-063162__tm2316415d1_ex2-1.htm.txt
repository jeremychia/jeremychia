WHEREAS, the Company acknowledges that, following the completion of the Offer and provided that at such time Parent directly or indirectly has acquired or controls at least 90% of the then outstanding Shares (excluding Shares held by the Company or any of its Subsidiaries), Parent intends that, in accordance with the laws of Switzerland and a merger agreement between Ironwood Pharmaceuticals GmbH, a limited liability company organized under the laws of Switzerland and a Subsidiary of Parent (“ Merger Sub ”), and the Company, Merger Sub and the Company will consummate a statutory squeeze-out merger pursuant to which the Company will be merged with Merger Sub, and Merger Sub will continue as the surviving entity of the Merger, and each Share (other than any Excluded Shares) that is not validly tendered and accepted pursuant to the Offer or acquired by Parent after the Acceptance Time will thereupon be cancelled and converted into the right to receive the Offer Price (the “ Merger ”);
