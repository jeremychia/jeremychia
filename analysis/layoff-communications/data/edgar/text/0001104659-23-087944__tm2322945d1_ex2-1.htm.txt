The Sellers and each CorpAcq Party further acknowledges that, if the Transactions, or, in the event of termination of this Agreement, another Business Combination, are not consummated by February 17, 2024 or such later date as approved by the CCVII Stockholders, CCVII will be obligated to return to the CCVII Stockholders the amounts being held in the Trust Account.
