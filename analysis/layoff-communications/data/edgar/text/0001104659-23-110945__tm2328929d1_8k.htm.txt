On October 18, 2023, Brainstorm Cell Therapeutics Inc. (the “Company”) committed to a reduction in force that is expected to result in a reduction in its headcount of approximately 30% (the “RIF”). The Company is taking this step to decrease its costs and strategically realign itself to enable accelerated development of NurOwn® for the treatment of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). In connection with the RIF, the Company currently estimates it will incur a range of costs and expenses of approximately $450,000-$900,000, consisting primarily of cash severance costs and one-time termination benefits. The Company expects to substantially complete the RIF by the end of 2023. The estimates of costs and expenses that the Company expects to incur in connection with the RIF are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ materially. The Company may also incur additional costs not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or that are associated with, the RIF.
