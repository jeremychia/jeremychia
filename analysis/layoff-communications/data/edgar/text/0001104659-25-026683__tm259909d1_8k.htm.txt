COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH EXIT OR DISPOSAL ACTIVITIES. On March 18, 2025, O-I Glass, Inc. (the “Company”) finalized its plans for the closure of a plant in its European segment. This closure is part of the Company’s Fit to Win initiative to reduce redundant capacity and begin to optimize its network. Additional capacity closures and other restructuring actions are expected in 2025. The Company intends to facilitate the closure in a respectful manner for the approximately 170 people impacted. Current customers of the plant impacted by the closure will be served by other European plants in the Company’s network. Subject to finalization of certain estimates, the Company expects to record charges associated with this closure of approximately $50 million in the first quarter of 2025, primarily for one-time employee separation benefits and other costs related to the closing (of which approximately $50 million relate to future cash expenditures). ITEM 8.01. OTHER EVENTS. The Company has also approved a severance program and other restructuring actions that is primarily expected to reduce future selling, general and administrative costs primarily in the Americas segment and in the non-reportable Retained corporate costs and other category. The Company expects to record a charge associated with these actions of approximately $20 million in the first quarter of 2025, all which relates to future cash expenditures.
