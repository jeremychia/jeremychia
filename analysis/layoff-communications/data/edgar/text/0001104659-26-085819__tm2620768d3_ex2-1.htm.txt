Section 2.5 Purchase Price; Purchase Price Adjustments . (a) In consideration for the sale and transfer of the Purchased Assets, and on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, the Buyer shall (i) pay to the Seller an amount in cash equal to (A) the Base Purchase Price, minus (B) the amount, if any, by which the Minimum Cash Amount exceeds the Estimated Closing Cash (the “ Estimated Cash Shortfall ”), minus (C) the Estimated Closing Indebtedness (the “ Closing Cash Payment ”), and (ii) assume the Assumed Liabilities.
