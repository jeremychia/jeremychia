At the Effective Time, (i) Merger Partner shall be merged with and into Merger Sub (Merger Sub as the surviving company following the Merger is sometimes referred to herein as the “ Surviving Company ”) and the separate existence of Merger Partner shall cease and (ii) the articles of organization of Merger Sub as in effect as of immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be amended and restated in its entirety to read as set forth on Exhibit B , and, as so amended and restated, shall be the articles of organization of the Surviving Company until thereafter amended as provided therein and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the WLLCA. The Merger shall have the effects set forth in this Agreement and the applicable provisions of the WLLCA.
