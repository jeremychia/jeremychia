W I T N E S S E T H WHEREAS , the boards of directors of the Parties have determined it to be in the best interests of their respective companies and their respective shareholders to consummate the business combination transaction provided for in this Agreement in which SJCB will, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, merge with and into TCBS (the “ Merger ”), with TCBS as the surviving company in the Merger (sometimes referred to in such capacity as the “ Surviving Entity ”); WHEREAS , as a condition to the willingness of TCBS to enter into this Agreement, each director and executive officer of SJCB who owns shares of SJCB Stock and certain other SJCB shareholders have entered into a voting agreement (each a “ SJCB Voting Agreement ” and collectively, the “ SJCB Voting Agreements ”), substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit B , dated as of the date hereof, with TCBS, pursuant to which each such director and executive officer has agreed, among other things, to vote the SJCB Stock owned by such director and executive officer in favor of the approval of this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, subject to the terms of the SJCB Voting Agreements; WHEREAS , as a condition to the willingness of TCBS to enter into this Agreement, the President, Treasurer and Secretary of SJCB, Ms. Cindy Manthey, has entered into an employment agreement (the “ Manthey Employment Agreement ”), substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit C , dated as of the date hereof and effective upon the Effective Time, with TCBS, pursuant to which (a) TCBS has agreed to employ Ms. Manthey and, in turn (b) Ms. Manthey has agreed, among other things, to remain employed by the Surviving Bank (defined below) and to certain non-competition and non-solicitation obligations following cessation of such employment; WHEREAS , as a condition to the willingness of TCBS to enter into this Agreement, Denny Clark and his affiliates who hold deposits at Target Bank are entering into a Deposit Retention Agreement in the form attached hereto as Exhibit D (the “ Deposit Retention Agreement ”), pursuant to which, among other things, such depositors agree to take all actions necessary to retain existing deposit relationships at Target Bank (defined below) and, following the Merger, at Broadstreet (defined below) for a period of twelve (12) months following the Effective Time; WHEREAS , the Parties desire to make certain representations, warranties and agreements in connection with the Merger and the Bank Merger (as defined below) (collectively, the “ Mergers ”) and also to prescribe certain conditions to the Mergers. ​ 1 NOW, THEREFORE , in consideration of the mutual promises herein contained and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: Article I ​ THE MERGERS Section 1.01 The Merger . Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, in accordance with the Texas Business Organizations Code (the “ TBOC ”), at the Effective Time, SJCB shall merge with and into TCBS pursuant to the terms of this Agreement. TCBS shall be the Surviving Entity in the Merger and shall continue its existence as a corporation under the laws of the State of Texas. As of the Effective Time, the separate corporate existence of SJCB shall cease. Section 1.02 Certificate of Formation and Bylaws . At the Effective Time, the certificate of formation of TCBS in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the certificate of formation of the Surviving Entity until thereafter amended in accordance with applicable Law. The bylaws of TCBS in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be the bylaws of the Surviving Entity until thereafter amended in accordance with applicable Law and the terms of such bylaws. Section 1.03 Directors and Officers . The directors of TCBS in office immediately prior to the Effective Time shall serve as the directors of the Surviving Entity, from and after the Effective Time, in accordance with the bylaws of the Surviving Entity. The officers of TCBS in office immediately prior to the Effective Time shall serve as the officers of the Surviving Entity, from and after the Effective Time, in accordance with the bylaws of the Surviving Entity. Section 1.04 Bank Merger . Except as provided below, immediately following the Effective Time and sequentially but in effect simultaneously on the Closing Date, The Bank of San Jacinto County, Coldspring, Texas, a Texas non-member bank and a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of SJCB (“ Target Bank ”), shall be merged (the “ Bank Merger ”) with and into Broadstreet Bank, SSB, a Texas state savings bank and a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of TCBS (“ Broadstreet ”), in accordance with the provisions of applicable federal and state banking laws and regulations, and Broadstreet shall be the surviving bank (the “ Surviving Bank ”). The Bank Merger shall have the effects as set forth under applicable federal and state banking laws and regulations, and TCBS and SJCB, as the sole shareholders of Broadstreet and the Target Bank, respectively, have approved a separate merger agreement (the “ Bank Plan of Merger ”) in substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit A , and have caused the Bank Plan of Merger to be executed and delivered by Broadstreet and Target Bank simultaneously with the execution and delivery of this Agreement. As provided in the Bank Plan of Merger, the Bank Merger may be abandoned at the election of Broadstreet at any time, whether before or after filings are made for regulatory approval of the Bank Merger, but if the Bank Merger is abandoned for any reason, the Target Bank shall continue to operate under its name; provided , prior to any such election, TCBS shall (a) reasonably consult with SJCB and its regulatory counsel and (b) reasonably determine in good faith that such election will not, and would not reasonably be expected to, prevent, delay or ​ 2 impair any Party’s ability to consummate the Merger or the other transactions contemplated by this Agreement. Section 1.05 Effective Time; Closing . (a) Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Parties will make all such filings as may be required to consummate the Mergers by applicable Laws. The Merger shall become effective as set forth in the certificate of merger (the “ Certificate of Merger ”) related to the Merger, which shall include a statement containing the information set forth in TBOC 10.151(b)(1), that shall be filed with the Secretary of the State of Texas, as provided in TBOC, on the Closing Date. The “ Effective Time ” of the Merger shall be the later of (i) the date and time of filing of the Certificate of Merger, or (ii) the date and time when the Merger becomes effective as set forth in the Certificate of Merger, which shall be within thirty (30) days after all of the conditions to the Closing set forth in Article VI (other than those conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied by actions taken at the Closing, which shall be satisfied or waived at the Closing) have been satisfied or waived (to the extent permitted by applicable law) in accordance with the terms hereof, or at such other time and date as may be mutually agreed in writing by the Parties. (b) The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the “ Closing ”) shall take place on the Business Day prior to the Effective Time (such date, the “ Closing Date ”) by electronic means or such other place as the Parties may mutually agree. At Closing, there shall be delivered to the Parties the Certificate of Merger and such other certificates and other documents required to be delivered under Article VI . Article II ​ MERGER CONSIDERATION; EXCHANGE PROCEDURES Section 2.01 Merger Consideration . (a) At the Effective Time, all of the shares of SJCB Stock issued and outstanding, including all options, warrants and other rights convertible into capital stock, immediately prior to the Effective Time, shall by virtue of the Merger and without any action on the part of the holders thereof, be cancelled and converted into and represent the right to receive in aggregate an amount of cash equal to $9,500,000 (the “ Aggregate Merger Consideration ”), subject to any reduction set forth in Section 5.01(b) . (b) Each share of SJCB Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall, by virtue of the merger and without any action on the part of the holder thereof, be cancelled and converted into and represent the right to receive an amount of cash equal to the quotient of (i) the Aggregate Merger Consideration divided by (ii) the total number of shares of SJCB Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior the Effective Time (the “ Per Share Merger Consideration ”). The Per Share Merger Consideration shall be payable to the holder thereof, without interest thereon and less any withholding Taxes. Section 2.02 Dissenting Shares . Each share of SJCB Stock issued and outstanding immediately before the Effective Time, the holder of which has not voted in favor of nor consented in writing to the approval of the Merger and who has properly perfected his dissenter’s rights of appraisal by following the exact procedure required by Section 10.356 of the TBOC is referred to herein as a “ Dissenting Share .” Notwithstanding any provision of this Agreement to the contrary, each Dissenting Share shall not be converted into or represent the right to receive the Per Share Merger Consideration pursuant to this Article II and shall be entitled only to such rights as are available to such holder pursuant to the applicable provisions of the TBOC. Each holder of Dissenting Shares shall be entitled to receive the value of such Dissenting Shares held by him in accordance with the applicable provisions of the TBOC; provided , such holder shall comply with the procedures contemplated by and set forth in the applicable provisions of the TBOC. If any holder of any Dissenting Shares shall effectively withdraw or lose his dissenter’s rights under the applicable provisions of the TBOC, each such Dissenting Share shall be deemed to have been converted into and to have become exchangeable for, the right to receive the Per Share Merger Consideration without any interest thereon in accordance with the provisions of this Article II . Section 2.03 Procedures . (a) On the day before the Closing Date, TCBS shall deposit with the Paying Agent, for exchange in accordance with this Section 2.03 , cash in an aggregate amount equal to the Aggregate Merger Consideration. At least twenty (20) days before the Closing Date, SJCB shall cause the Paying Agent to mail to each holder of record of a certificate or certificates which represent shares of SJCB Stock (the “ Certificates ”) appropriate transmittal materials and instructions (which shall ​ 4 specify that delivery shall be effected, and risk of loss and title to such Certificates shall pass, only upon proper delivery of such Certificates to the Paying Agent). The Certificates of SJCB Stock so delivered shall be duly endorsed as the Paying Agent may require. In the event of a transfer of ownership of shares of SJCB Stock represented by Certificates that is not registered in the transfer records of SJCB, the consideration provided in Section 2.01 may be issued to a transferee if the Certificates representing such shares are delivered to the Paying Agent, accompanied by all documents required to evidence such transfer and by evidence satisfactory to the Paying Agent that any applicable stock transfer taxes have been paid. If any Certificate shall have been lost, stolen, mislaid or destroyed, upon receipt of (i) an affidavit of that fact from the holder claiming such Certificate to be lost, stolen, mislaid or destroyed, (ii) such bond, security or indemnity as SJCB and the Paying Agent may reasonably require, and (iii) any other documents necessary to evidence and effect the bona fide exchange thereof, the Paying Agent shall issue to such holder the consideration into which the shares represented by such lost, stolen, mislaid or destroyed Certificate shall have been converted. The Paying Agent may establish such other reasonable and customary rules and procedures in connection with its duties as it may deem appropriate. TCBS shall pay all charges and expenses, including those of the Paying Agent, in connection with the distribution of the consideration provided in this Article II . (b) After the Effective Time, each holder of shares of SJCB Stock (other than Dissenting Shares) issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall surrender the Certificate or Certificates representing such shares to the Paying Agent and shall promptly upon surrender thereof receive in exchange therefor the consideration provided in Section 2.01 , together with all undelivered dividends or distributions in respect of such shares (without interest thereon) pursuant to Section 2.04 . TCBS shall not be obligated to deliver the Per Share Merger Consideration to which any former holder of SJCB Stock is entitled as a result of the Merger until such holder surrenders such holder’s Certificate or Certificates (or the documents required by Section 2.03(a) for a lost, stolen, mislaid or destroyed Certificate) for exchange as provided in this Section 2.03(a) . (c) TCBS, the Paying Agent and any other applicable withholding agent shall each be entitled to deduct and withhold from any payment pursuant to this Agreement such amounts, if any, as it is required to deduct and withhold with respect to the making of such payment under the Code, or any provision of applicable Law. To the extent any such amounts are so withheld, the withheld amounts shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the Person in respect of which such deduction and withholding was made. (d) Any other provision of this Agreement notwithstanding, neither TCBS nor the Paying Agent shall be liable to a holder of SJCB Stock for any amounts paid or property delivered in good faith to a public official pursuant to any applicable abandoned property, escheat or similar Law. Adoption of this Agreement by the shareholders of SJCB shall constitute ratification of the appointment of the Paying Agent. Section 2.04 Rights of Former Company Shareholders . At the Effective Time, the stock transfer books of SJCB shall be closed as to holders of SJCB Stock immediately prior to the Effective Time and no transfer of SJCB Stock by any such holder shall thereafter be made or recognized. Until surrendered for exchange in accordance with the provisions of Section 2.03 , each Certificate theretofore representing shares of SJCB Stock (other than Dissenting Shares) shall ​ 5 from and after the Effective Time represent for all purposes only the right to receive the Per Share Merger Consideration provided in Article II in exchange therefor, subject to the Surviving Entity’s obligation to pay any dividends or make any other distributions with a record date before the Effective Time, which have been declared or made by SJCB in respect of such shares of SJCB Stock in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and which remain unpaid at the Effective Time.
