of the Exchange Act. ¨ Introductory Note On November 2, 2026 (the “Closing Date”), Kimberly-Clark Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“K-C” or the “Company”), completed its previously announced acquisition of Kenvue Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Kenvue”), pursuant to the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of November 2, 2025 (the “Merger Agreement”), by and among Kenvue, K-C, Vesta Sub I, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a direct wholly owned subsidiary of K-C (“First Merger Sub”), and Vesta Sub II, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and a direct wholly owned subsidiary of K-C (“Second Merger Sub”). On the Closing Date, pursuant to the Merger Agreement, (i) First Merger Sub merged with and into Kenvue (the “First Merger”), with Kenvue surviving as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of K-C (the “Initial Surviving Company”) (the time the First Merger became effective being the “First Effective Time”), and (ii) immediately following the First Merger, and as part of the same overall transaction as the First Merger, the Initial Surviving Company merged with and into Second Merger Sub (the “Second Merger” and, together with the First Merger, the “Mergers”), with Second Merger Sub surviving the Second Merger as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of K-C and renamed “Kenvue LLC” (the “Final Surviving Company”) (the time the Second Merger became effective being the “Second Effective Time”). The events described in this Current Report on Form 8-K took place in connection with the closing of the Mergers. Item 2.01. Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets. As discussed in the Introductory Note, which is incorporated into this Item 2.01 by reference, on the Closing Date, K-C completed its previously announced acquisition of Kenvue pursuant to the Merger Agreement. At the First Effective Time, pursuant to the terms and subject to the conditions of the Merger Agreement, each share of Kenvue common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Kenvue Common Stock”), issued and outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time (other than shares of Kenvue Common Stock that were owned by K-C or Kenvue or any wholly owned subsidiary of K-C or Kenvue (or were held in treasury by Kenvue)) was converted into the right to receive (A) 0.14625 shares (the “Exchange Ratio”) of K-C common stock, par value $1.25 per share (the “K-C Common Stock” and the shares of K-C Common Stock issued in connection with the First Merger, the “Stock Consideration”), subject to cash in lieu of fractional shares, plus (B) $3.50 in cash (the “Cash Consideration” and, together with the Stock Consideration, the “Merger Consideration”), in each case, without interest. At the Second Effective Time, by virtue of the Second Merger and without any action on the part of any of the parties, the Initial Surviving Company or the equityholders thereof, (i) each share of common stock of the Initial Surviving Company issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Second Effective Time was automatically canceled and ceased to exist without any conversion or payment therefor and (ii) each unit of Second Merger Sub issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Second Effective Time remained issued and outstanding and represented validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable units of the Final Surviving Company, which constituted the only outstanding units of the Final Surviving Company immediately following the Second Effective Time. No fractional shares of K-C Common Stock were issued in the Mergers, and holders of Kenvue Common Stock otherwise entitled to receive a fractional share of K-C Common Stock will receive a cash payment, without interest, in lieu thereof, based on the average of the volume-weighted average price per share of K-C Common Stock for each of the five consecutive trading days ending on (and including) the first trading day immediately prior to the Closing Date, as specified in the Merger Agreement. In accordance with the terms of the Merger Agreement, each equity award of the Kenvue, consisting of stock options, restricted stock units, performance stock units and deferred stock units, that was outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time was converted into an equivalent equity award of K-C, including with respect to vesting conditions (including in connection with a qualifying termination of employment), except that performance stock units were converted into solely time-vesting restricted stock units. The number of shares of K-C Common Stock subject to each converted equity award was equal to the number of shares of Kenvue Common Stock subject to the award, with the number of shares of Kenvue Common Stock subject to any performance stock unit determined based on the greater of target and actual performance measured through the First Effective Time, multiplied by the Equity Award Exchange Ratio (as defined below), and the exercise price of each converted stock option is equal to the exercise price of the stock option of the Company divided by the Equity Award Exchange Ratio. The “Equity Award Exchange Ratio” is a fraction, (i) the numerator of which is the sum of the Cash Consideration and an amount equal to the product of (A) the Exchange Ratio and (B) the closing price of K-C Common Stock on the trading day immediately prior to the date of the First Effective Time (the “K-C Closing Price”) and (ii) the denominator of which is the K-C Closing Price. In respect of Kenvue Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time, K-C delivered or has an obligation to deliver approximately 281,060,840 shares of K-C Common Stock in the aggregate. Item 2.05. Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 30, 2026, in connection with the Mergers, the Board approved the Kenvue Integration Program (the “Program”), which is intended to achieve certain operating profit synergies by 2030. Actions contemplated under the Program commenced on November 2, 2026 and are expected to be completed by December 31, 2029. The Program is expected to result in cumulative pre-tax charges of approximately $3.1 billion ($2.5 billion cash and $0.6 billion non-cash) over this period, with cash charges primarily related to supply chain network optimization (approximately $0.8 billion), enterprise resource planning and technology integration (approximately $0.6 billion), workforce optimization (approximately $0.4 billion), advisory services (approximately $0.4 billion) and program management and other restructuring and integration costs (approximately $0.3 billion). Non-cash charges are primarily related to supply chain network optimization. Additionally, K-C expects to spend approximately $1.4 billion in capital related to the Program through December 31, 2029. The estimate of charges that K-C expects to incur and the timing thereof are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ from current expectations. K-C may also incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, the Program. Item 5.02. Performance Recognition and Incentive Awards On October 30, 2026, the Management Development and Compensation Committee of the Board approved one-time, premium-priced nonqualified stock option awards (the “Performance Recognition and Incentive Awards , ” or the “Awards”) under the Kimberly-Clark Corporation 2021 Equity Participation Plan (as amended, the “Plan”) to the Company’s executive officers, including its named executive officers, and other eligible employees. The grants are effective December 1, 2026 (the “Grant Date”) and were conditioned on the completion of the Company’s acquisition of Kenvue. The grant values of the Awards for the Company’s named executive officers are as follows: Michael Hsu, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, $6,600,000; Nelson Urdaneta, Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, $2,150,000; and Jeffrey Melucci, Chief Strategy, Business Development and Administrative Officer, $1,800,000. The named executive officers’ Awards are intended to recognize their leadership in the successful completion of the Kenvue acquisition, to incentivize their continued execution of the Kenvue integration and synergy realization efforts, to closely align long-term compensation with sustained stockholder value creation, and to promote their continued service with the Company. Awards granted to additional executive officers and eligible employees are intended to achieve similar objectives, and to promote unity across the business for shared strategic objectives. The Awards provide the executive officers and eligible employees with the opportunity to purchase Company stock with an exercise price per share that will be the greater of (i) $120.00 and (ii) 110% of the closing price of a share of the Company’s common stock on the Grant Date. The number of shares of the Company’s common stock subject to each Award will equal the grant value divided by the Black-Scholes value of a single premium priced, nonqualified stock option on the Grant Date, as determined by an independent third-party valuation firm, rounded down to the nearest whole share. The Awards have a ten-year term. The Awards will vest and become exercisable in full on December 1, 2029, subject to the executive’s continued employment through that date. Unvested Awards will generally not vest early on any termination of employment, including retirement, death or disability, and will be forfeited on any such termination. However, upon a qualifying termination of employment following a change of control of the Company that occurs after the Grant Date, the Awards will vest in full under the terms of the Plan. They will be forfeited in their entirety upon a termination of employment for cause. Following any other termination, vested Awards will generally remain exercisable for a limited period that depends on the reason for the termination, but in no event beyond the end of the term. The foregoing description of the Awards does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the form of Performance Recognition and Incentive Awards Agreement, which K-C expects to file with its Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026. Item 5.03. Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year. On October 30, 2026, the Board approved certain amendments to K-C’s By-Laws (as so amended, the “Amended By-Laws”), effective upon the completion of the Mergers on November 2, 2026. The amendments, among other things, revise the advance notice procedures and disclosure requirements applicable to stockholder proposals and director nominations, add requirements addressing the universal proxy rules under Rule 14a-19 under the Exchange Act, revise the procedures for stockholder-requested special meetings, update provisions concerning stockholder meeting notices, meetings held solely by remote communication, meeting conduct, adjournments, the preparation and availability of stockholder lists, eliminate the right of holders of at least ten percent of the voting power represented at a meeting to demand a vote by written ballot, change the quorum requirement for Board meetings, revise the provisions governing Board committees and officers, update the indemnification and expense advancement provisions, and designate the federal district courts of the United States as the exclusive forum for complaints asserting causes of action under the Securities Act, unless K-C selects or consents in writing to an alternative forum. The amendments also remove certain obsolete provisions and make other ministerial, technical, and conforming changes.
