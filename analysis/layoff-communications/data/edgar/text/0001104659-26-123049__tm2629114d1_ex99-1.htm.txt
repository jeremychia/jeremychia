Results of operations During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Group revenue decreased by 13.1% to € 465.0 m compared with € 535.1 m in the corresponding period of the prior year. At constant exchange rates, Group revenue declined by 10.8% to € 477.4 m compared with € 535.1 m in the prior-year period. Base revenue 1 decreased by 12.4% from € 503.7 m in 9M 2025 to € 441.1 m in 9M 2026. Revenue in the Discovery & Preclinical Development (D&PD) segment (including intersegment revenue) declined by 11.2% to € 348.2 m for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with € 392.1 m in the prior-year period. The decrease was primarily driven by lower sales-to-revenue conversion across all D&PD business areas, reflecting ongoing timing effects in commercial activities despite early signs of improvement in the market. Revenue from Just – Evotec Biologics (JEB) decreased by 18.3% to € 117.1 m for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, down from € 143.4 m in the prior-year period. The decline was primarily driven by the USD 25 m Sandoz license sale recognized in Q1 2025 and delayed program activities. Cost of revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 amounted to € 448.4 m compared with € 478.0 m in the corresponding period of 2025, resulting in a gross margin of 3.6% (9M 2025: 10.7%). Within D&PD, cost of revenue totalled € 320.4 m for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with € 338.9 m in the prior-year period. Gross margin decreased to 8.0% from 13.6% in 9M 2025, primarily driven by lower top-line performance and continued underutilization, which is being addressed through Project Horizon. Within JEB, cost of revenue decreased to € 128.3 m in the first nine months of 2026, compared with € 139.4 m in the corresponding period of 2025. Gross margin declined to (9.5)% compared with 2.7% in the first nine months of 2025, reflecting delayed program activities, temporarily higher material and project costs due to production phasing, as well as the Sandoz license income in Q1 2025. R&D expenses decreased by 29.8% to € 29.7 m, compared with € 42.3 m in the nine months ended September 30, 2025, driven by more focused capital allocation to selected R&D projects. Research and development expenses were incurred predominantly within the D&PD segment. SG&A expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 amounted to € 121.9 m and were thus € 16.5 m or 11.9% lower compared to last year (9M 2025: € 138.4 m). This change was primarily attributable to lower SG&A expenses within the D&PD segment, which declined to € 97.2 m from € 115.8 m in the first nine months of 2025, mainly due to lower personnel costs following the implementation of the Horizon program and reduced consultancy expenses. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, other operating income amounted to € 31.2 m, compared with € 41.4 m in the prior year period. The decrease was primarily attributable to the D&PD segment and mainly driven by lower R&D tax credits from slower activity and the non-recurrence of a € 7.5 m one-off insurance reimbursement related to the cyber-attack that was received in 2025, partially offset by other operating income from transition service agreements. Other operating expenses increased from € 10.9 m in the first nine months of 2025 to € 59.1 m in the first nine months of 2026. The increase was primarily attributable to an impairment loss of € 42.3 m in the D&PD segment relating to a laboratory building in Hamburg, recognized during 2026, as well as € 3.2 m of expenses related to transition service agreements. 1 Base revenue does not include revenue from licenses, milestone fees and royalties. 5 INTERIM STATEMENT 9M 2026 For the nine months ended September 30, 2026 Reorganization costs amounted to € 91.9 m (9M 2025: income of € 0.7 m), reflecting expenses for provisions and asset impairments related to the previously announced Project Horizon. The adjusted EBITDA of the D&PD segment was a loss of € 25.5 m (9M 2025: loss of € 18.8 m), due to reduced revenue, which could only partially be offset by a lower cost base within Cost of revenue, R&D and SG&A. Project Horizon On March 10, 2026, Evotec announced ‘Horizon’, the next phase in its multi-stage transformation initiative. Horizon establishes a new operating model built across the three pillars of operations, science, and commercial execution. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Evotec recorded reorganization costs totaling € 91.9 m, which were fully allocated to the D&PD segment. These costs are directly attributable to measures linked to the restructuring and are not related to operating activities. These reorganization costs include € 43.3 m attributable to personnel measures (including severance payments) as well as € 43.1 m of impairment losses on property, plant and equipment at affected sites. The measurement of restructuring provisions is based on estimates and assumptions regarding the amount of severance payments, the timeline for the implementation of the measures and the progress of discussions in accordance with local labor laws and regulations. The measurement assumptions are regularly reviewed as the restructuring program progresses. 5.
