On May 24, 2022, the Board of Directors of Eargo, Inc. (the “Company”) approved a plan to reduce the Company’s employee workforce by approximately 17% as part of the Company’s cost-cutting measures to reduce operating expenses and preserve capital. The Company expects to substantially complete the employee workforce reduction by the end of its quarter ending June 30, 2022 and estimates that the costs associated with all activities under the employee workforce reduction plan will be approximately $0.4 million to $0.6 million, which includes severance and related benefits.
