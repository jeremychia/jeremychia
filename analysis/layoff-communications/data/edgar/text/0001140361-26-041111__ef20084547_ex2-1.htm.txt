(c) Conversion of Company Common Stock. Each issued and outstanding share of Company Common Stock (other than Excluded Shares and Appraisal Shares) (i) with respect to which an election to receive cash (a “ Cash Election ”) has been properly made and not revoked or deemed revoked pursuant to Section 1.08 shall be converted automatically into the right to receive $65.00 in cash, without interest (the “ Cash Consideration ”), (ii) with respect to which an election to receive Parent Class A Common Stock (a “ Stock Election ”) has been properly made and not revoked or deemed revoked pursuant to Section 1.08 (a “ Stock Election Share ”) shall be converted automatically into the right to receive 0.3372 (the “ Stock Exchange Ratio ”) validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of Parent Class A Common Stock, subject to Section 1.07(d) with respect to Parent’s election to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration and Section 1.09(j) with respect to fractional shares, without interest (the “ Stock Consideration ”), (iii) with respect to which both a Cash Election and a Stock Election (a “ Mixed Election ”) have been properly made and not revoked or deemed revoked pursuant to Section 1.08 (a “ Mixed Election Share ”) shall be converted automatically into the right to receive (x) $48.75 in cash, without interest, plus (y) 0.08430 (the “ Mixed Exchange Ratio ”) validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of Parent Class A Common Stock, subject to Section 1.07(d) with respect to Parent’s election to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration and subject to Section 1.09(j) with respect to fractional shares, without interest (the “ Mixed Consideration ” and, together with the Cash Consideration and the Stock Consideration, the “ Merger Consideration ”) or (iv) with respect to which neither a Cash Election nor a Stock Election has been properly made (a “ Non-Election Share ”) shall be converted automatically into the right to receive the Cash Consideration. As of the Effective Time, all such shares of Company Common Stock shall no longer be outstanding and shall automatically be canceled and retired and shall cease to exist, and each holder of any such shares of Company Common Stock shall cease to have any rights with respect thereto, except the right to receive the Merger Consideration in accordance with Section 1.09 and the right, if any, to receive any distribution or dividend pursuant to Section 1.09(k) . 5 (d) Alternative Stock Consideration . Notwithstanding anything in this Agreement to the contrary, prior to the earlier of (x) March 31, 2027 and (y) three months prior to the Closing (the “ Issuance Election Deadline ”), Parent may elect to issue validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of Parent Class C Common Stock in lieu of the shares of Parent Class A Common Stock to be issued as Stock Consideration and the stock portion of the Mixed Consideration (the “ Alternative Stock Consideration ”); provided , that (a) the conditions set forth on Exhibit C are satisfied prior to the applicable time set forth therein and (b) Parent has provided prior, irrevocable written notice to the Company of any such election. SECTION 1.08. Proration; Election Mechanics. (a) Notwithstanding any other provision contained in this Agreement, the total number of shares of Company Common Stock to be converted at the Effective Time into the right to receive the Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(c) (or the Alternative Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(d) ) shall not exceed the Maximum Stock Share Number. Subject to Section 1.08(b) , once the Maximum Stock Share Number is met, all other shares of Company Common Stock with respect to which Stock Elections have been properly made and not revoked or deemed revoked pursuant to this Section 1.08 shall be converted at the Effective Time into the right to receive the Cash Consideration. 6 (b) Promptly (and in any event no later than five (5) business days) after the Effective Time, Parent shall cause the Exchange Agent to effect the allocation of rights to receive the Stock Consideration among holders of shares of Company Common Stock as follows: (i) if the aggregate number of shares of Company Common Stock with respect to which Stock Elections shall have been properly made and not revoked or deemed revoked pursuant to this Section 1.08 (the “ Stock Election Number ”) exceeds the Maximum Stock Share Number, then Stock Election Shares of each holder will be converted automatically into the right to receive the Stock Consideration in respect of that number of Stock Election Shares equal to the product of (A) the number of Stock Election Shares held by such holder and (B) a fraction, the numerator of which is the Maximum Stock Share Number and the denominator of which is the Stock Election Number (with the Exchange Agent to determine, consistent with Section 1.09(j) , whether fractions of Stock Election Shares shall be rounded up or down), with the remaining number of such holder’s Stock Election Shares being converted automatically into the right to receive the Cash Consideration; and (ii) if the Stock Election Number is less than or equal to the Maximum Stock Share Number, then all Stock Election Shares shall be converted into the right to receive the Stock Consideration. (c) Each holder of record of shares of Company Common Stock to be converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration in accordance with, and subject to, Section 1.07(c) and this Section 1.08 (an “ Electing Holder ”) shall have the right, subject to the limitations set forth in this Section 1.08 , to submit an election in accordance with the following procedures: (i) Each Electing Holder may specify in a request made in accordance with the provisions of this Section 1.08 (herein called an “ Election ”) (A) the number of shares of Company Common Stock owned by such Electing Holder with respect to which such Electing Holder desires to make a Stock Election, (B) the number of shares of Company Common Stock owned by such Electing Holder with respect to which such Electing Holder desires to make a Cash Election and (C) the number of shares of Company Common Stock owned by such Electing Holder with respect to which such Electing Holder desires to make a Mixed Election; 7 (ii) Parent shall prepare a form of election reasonably acceptable to the Company (including appropriate and customary transmittal materials in such form as prepared by Parent and reasonably acceptable to the Company) (the “ Form of Election ”), so as to permit Electing Holders to exercise their right to make an Election. Any Electing Holder that holds any shares of Company Common Stock as nominee, as trustee or in other representative capacity may, through proper instructions and documentation, submit a separate Form of Election prior to the Election Deadline with respect to each beneficial owner for whom such nominee, trustee or representative holds such shares of Company Common Stock. (iii) The Company and Parent (A) shall initially make available and mail the Form of Election not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the anticipated Election Deadline to Electing Holders of record as of the fifth (5th) business day prior to such mailing date, and (B) following such mailing date, shall use reasonable best efforts to make available as promptly as practicable a Form of Election to any holder of shares of Company Common Stock who requests such Form of Election prior to the Election Deadline. The time period between such mailing date and the Election Deadline is referred to herein as the “ Election Period ”. (iv) Any Election shall have been made properly only if the Exchange Agent shall have received, during the Election Period, a Form of Election properly completed and executed (including duly executed transmittal materials included in the Form of Election). As used herein, unless otherwise agreed in advance by Parent and the Company, “ Election Deadline ” means 5:00 p.m. local time (in the city in which the principal office of the Exchange Agent is located) on the date that is three (3) business days prior to the parties’ good faith estimate of the Closing Date, or such other date as may be mutually agreed to by the Company and Parent. Parent and the Company shall cooperate to issue a press release reasonably satisfactory to each of them announcing the date of the Election Deadline at least one (1) business day prior to the Election Deadline. If the Closing Date is delayed to a subsequent date, the Election Deadline shall be similarly delayed to a subsequent date, and Parent and the Company shall promptly announce any such delay and, when determined, the rescheduled Election Deadline. The Company may waive the Election Deadline only if (A) the new Election Deadline is publicly disclosed by the Company to all Company stockholders on a date agreed to by Parent, and (B) Parent has given its prior written consent to such waiver (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed). 8 (v) Any Electing Holder may, at any time during the Election Period, change their Election by written notice received by the Exchange Agent prior to the Election Deadline accompanied by a properly completed and executed revised Form of Election. If any Election is not properly made with respect to any shares of Company Common Stock (none of Parent, Merger Sub, the Company or the Exchange Agent being under any duty to notify any Electing Holder of any such defect), such Election shall be deemed to be not in effect, and the shares of Company Common Stock covered by such Election shall, for purposes hereof, be deemed to be Non-Election Shares, unless a properly completed and executed Election is thereafter received by the Exchange Agent prior to the Election Deadline. Subject to the terms of this Agreement and of the Form of Election, the Exchange Agent will have reasonable discretion to determine whether any election, revocation or change has been properly or timely made and to disregard immaterial defects in the Forms of Election, and any good faith decisions of the Exchange Agent regarding such matters will be binding and conclusive. (vi) Any Electing Holder may, at any time during the Election Period, revoke their Election by written notice received by the Exchange Agent prior to the Election Deadline. SECTION 1.09. Exchange Procedures. (a) Exchange Agent. Prior to the Effective Time, Parent shall select a bank or trust company reasonably acceptable to the Company to act as exchange agent (the “ Exchange Agent ”) for the payment of the Merger Consideration to each Person who was, as of immediately prior to the Effective Time, a holder of any shares of Company Common Stock (other than Excluded Shares and Appraisal Shares) and, in connection therewith, prior to the Effective Time, shall enter into an agreement with the Exchange Agent in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company. At or immediately after the Effective Time, Parent shall provide or cause to be provided to the Exchange Agent (i) an amount of cash necessary to pay for the shares of Company Common Stock converted into the right to receive the Cash Consideration and the Mixed Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(c) , (ii) evidence of shares of Parent Class A Common Stock (or Parent Class C Common Stock if Parent elects to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(d) ) in book-entry form necessary to pay for the shares of Company Common Stock converted into the right to receive the Stock Consideration and the Mixed Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(c) , (iii) any dividends or distributions payable on such shares of Parent Class A Common Stock (or Parent Class C Common Stock if Parent elects to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(d) ) pursuant to Section 1.09(k) with respect to shares of Company Common Stock which had not theretofore been surrendered for exchange pursuant to Section 1.09(b) and (iv) an amount of cash necessary to pay cash in lieu of fractional shares in accordance with Section 1.09(j) (such amount of cash, Parent Class A Common Stock, Parent Class C Common Stock and dividends or other distributions with respect thereto being hereinafter referred to as the “ Exchange Fund ”). The Exchange Fund shall not be used for any purpose that is not expressly provided for in this Agreement. (b) Treatment of Company Common Stock. (i) At the Effective Time, holders of shares of Company Common Stock held in direct registry form shall not be required to take any action with respect to the exchange of such shares for the Merger Consideration and any dividends or other distributions in respect of Parent Class A Common Stock (or Parent Class C Common Stock if Parent elects to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(d) ) to be issued or paid pursuant to Section 1.09(k) . Any holder of shares of Company Common Stock held in direct registry form shall, subject to compliance with customary procedures of the Exchange Agent and the Company’s transfer agent, automatically upon the Effective Time be entitled to receive, and Parent shall cause the Exchange Agent to issue and pay, as soon as reasonably practicable after the Effective Time (but in no event later than five (5) business days after the Effective Time), the Merger Consideration to which such holder is entitled pursuant to Section 1.07(c) . 9 (ii) With respect to shares of Company Common Stock held, directly or indirectly, through the Depository Trust Company (“ DTC ”), the Company shall cooperate to establish procedures with the Exchange Agent, DTC, DTC’s nominees and such other necessary third-party intermediaries to ensure that the Exchange Agent will transmit to DTC or its nominees as soon as reasonably practicable after the Effective Time (but in no event later than five (5) business days after the Effective Time) the aggregate Merger Consideration (together with any dividends or other distributions in respect of Parent Class A Common Stock (or Parent Class C Common Stock if Parent elects to issue the Alternative Stock Consideration pursuant to Section 1.07(d) )) to which the beneficial owners of such shares of Company Common Stock held, directly or indirectly, through DTC are entitled pursuant to Section 1.07(c) . (c) No Further Ownership Rights in Company Common Stock. The Merger Consideration issued or paid in accordance with the terms of this Article I as a result of the conversion of any shares of Company Common Stock shall be deemed to have been issued or paid in full satisfaction of all rights pertaining to such shares of Company Common Stock, subject, however, to (i) the right to receive dividends or other distributions, if any, in accordance with Section 1.09(k) and (ii) the Surviving Corporation’s obligation to pay any dividends or make any other distributions with a record date prior to the Effective Time that may have been declared or made by the Company on such shares of Company Common Stock not in violation of the terms of this Agreement or prior to the date of this Agreement and which remain unpaid at the Effective Time. After the Effective Time there shall be no further registration of transfers on the stock transfer books of the Surviving Corporation of shares of Company Common Stock that were outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time.
