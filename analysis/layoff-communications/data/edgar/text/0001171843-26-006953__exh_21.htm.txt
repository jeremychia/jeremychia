Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, on the Closing Date and in accordance with the General and Business Corporation Law of Missouri, as amended (the “ GBCLM ”) and the Missouri Limited Liability Company Act (the “ MLLCA ”), the Company shall be merged with and into Merger Sub, whereupon the separate corporate existence of the Company shall cease, and Merger Sub shall continue as the company surviving the Merger (the “ Surviving Company ”). Immediately following the Merger, the Surviving Company shall be dissolved or merged with and into Parent. Parent will cause the merger of the Bank with and into the Parent Bank at a time to be determined by Parent following the Effective Time (the “ Bank Merger ”). At the effective time of the Bank Merger, the separate existence of the Bank will terminate. Parent Bank will be the surviving bank and will continue its existence under Applicable Law. The Bank Merger shall be accomplished pursuant to the bank merger agreement in form attached hereto as Exhibit B . 1.2 Effective Time . As of the Closing, the Parties will cause the articles of merger (the “ Articles of Merger ”) to be executed and filed with the Missouri Secretary of State (the “ MSOS ”) as provided in the GBCLM and the MLLCA. The Merger shall become effective on the date and time (referred to as the “ Effective Time ”) at which the Articles of Merger are filed with the MSOS, or at such other date and time as is agreed among the Parties and specified in the Articles of Merger. 1.3 Effects of the Merger . At and as of the Effective Time: (a) as a result of the Merger, the certificate of formation and operating agreement of Merger Sub shall be the certificate of formation and operating agreement of the Surviving Company; (b) the officers of the Surviving Company shall be the officers of Merger Sub serving immediately prior to the Effective Time, who shall continue in office for the terms provided in the operating agreement of the Surviving Company and until their successors are duly elected or appointed and qualified; and (c) the Merger shall have the effects set forth in the applicable provisions of the GBCLM and MLLCA and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, at the Effective Time, all of the property, rights, privileges, powers and franchises of the Company shall be vested in the Surviving Company, and all debts, liabilities and duties of the Company shall become the debts, liabilities and duties of Merger Sub. 1.4 Merger Consideration; Conversion of Shares . (a) At the Effective Time, by virtue of the Merger and without any action on the part of the holder thereof, each share of common stock of the Company, par value $0.01 per share, issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (“ Company Common Stock ”), other than Excluded Shares and Dissenting Shares, shall be converted into and become the right to receive the following consideration (and thereupon shall no longer be outstanding and shall automatically be cancelled and retired and shall cease to exist and shall thereafter represent only the right to receive, upon surrender of such certificate in accordance with Section 1.6(b) , the following consideration) (the consideration described in clauses (i) and (ii) below, subject to adjustment in accordance with Section 1.4(d) , the “ Merger Consideration ”) : (i) $5.20 per share in cash without interest (such per share amount is hereinafter referred to as the “ Cash Consideration ”); and (ii) 0.4081 (the “ Exchange Ratio ”) validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of common stock, par value $4.00 per share, of Parent (the “ Parent Common Stock ”) (such per share amount, together with any cash in lieu of fractional shares of Parent Common Stock to be paid pursuant to Section 1.7 , is hereinafter referred to as the “ Parent Stock Consideration ”). (b) The holders of any certificates of Company Common Stock (“ Company Stock Certificates ”) previously evidencing such shares of Company Common Stock outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall cease to have any rights with respect thereto except as otherwise provided in this Agreement or by law. (c) If, between the date of this Agreement and the Effective Time, shares of Parent Common Stock shall be changed into a different number of shares or a different class of shares by reason of any reclassification, recapitalization, split-up, combination, exchange of shares or readjustment, or if a dividend in the form of Parent Common Stock shall be declared with a record date within such period, then the Exchange Ratio will be appropriately and proportionally adjusted so as to provide the holders of Company Common Stock with the same economic effect as contemplated by this Agreement prior to such event and as so adjusted shall, from and after the date of such event, be the Exchange Ratio. If, between the date of this Agreement and the Effective Time, shares of Company Common Stock shall be changed into a different number of shares or a different class of shares by reason of any reclassification, recapitalization, split-up, combination, exchange of shares or readjustment, or if a dividend in the form of Company Common Stock shall be declared with a record date within such period, then the Cash Consideration and Exchange Ratio will be appropriately and proportionally adjusted so as to provide the holders of Company Common Stock with the same economic effect as contemplated by this Agreement prior to such event and as so adjusted shall, from and after the date of such event, be the Cash Consideration and Exchange Ratio, respectively; provided, however, that nothing contained in this sentence shall be construed to permit the Company to take any action with respect to its securities or otherwise that is prohibited by the terms of this Agreement. 2 (d) In the event the Closing Balance Sheet reflects Consolidated Shareholders’ Equity is less than $69,333,034.00 (the “ Minimum Adjusted Net Worth ”), the Merger Consideration shall be reduced dollar-for-dollar by an amount equal to the amount of such shortfall (any such shortfall, the “ Net Worth Adjustment Amount ”), as follows: (i) First, the Cash Consideration shall be reduced by an amount, rounded up to the nearest whole cent, equal to the quotient obtained by dividing (A) the Net Worth Adjustment Amount by (B) the number of shares of Company Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than Excluded Shares and Dissenting Shares) (such reduced amount, the " Adjusted Cash Consideration "), and the Adjusted Cash Consideration shall be the Cash Consideration for all purposes of this Agreement; provided, however, that in no event shall the Cash Consideration be reduced below zero; and (ii) If the Net Worth Adjustment Amount exceeds the aggregate Cash Consideration that would otherwise have been payable absent this Section 1.4(d) (such excess, the " Excess Shortfall Amount "), then, in addition to the reduction of the Cash Consideration to zero pursuant to Section 1.4(d)(i) , the Exchange Ratio shall be adjusted as follows: (A) first, the " Adjusted Base Amount " shall be calculated by subtracting (1) the Excess Shortfall Amount from (2) the product of (i) the number of shares of Company Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than Excluded Shares and Dissenting Shares), multiplied by (ii) the Exchange Ratio, multiplied by (iii) the Closing Parent Common Stock Price (such product, the " Base Amount "); (B) second, the Adjusted Base Amount shall be divided by the Base Amount (such quotient, the " Adjustment Percentage "); and (C) third, the Exchange Ratio shall be multiplied by the Adjustment Percentage (such ratio, the “ Adjusted Exchange Ratio ”), and the Adjusted Exchange Ratio shall be the Exchange Ratio for purposes of Section 1.4(a) . The Adjusted Exchange Ratio shall be calculated and applied after any adjustment to the Exchange Ratio pursuant to Section 8.1(c)(iii) . (e) If the Closing Balance Sheet reflects Consolidated Shareholders’ Equity equal to or greater than the Minimum Adjusted Net Worth, then there will be no adjustment to the Merger Consideration.
