China Southern Airlines’ flight safety levels maintained the Company’s leading position in China’s civil aviation industry. Our senior managers strengthen staff protection, implementing fixed scheduling, centralized management and isolated high-risk flight crews when needed. For the first time, China Southern Airlines introduced a policy of free transportation for all pandemic prevention materials, with a total of 1,017 tons of freely moved within China and the international community. China Southern Air Cargo led the way with the resumption of flight operation of our 747 cargo aircraft which led to the restoration of cargo production and the stability of the aviation industrial chain supply chain. The China Southern’s dual brand management and service improvement committee(s) were established to promote the large-scale service of China’s largest airline. China Southern’s detailed process tracking of luggage obtained IATA full network compliance certification.
