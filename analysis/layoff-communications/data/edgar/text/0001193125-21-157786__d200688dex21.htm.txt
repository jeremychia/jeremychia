WHEREAS, Buyer wishes to purchase from Interest Sellers, and Interest Sellers wish to sell to Buyer, the Securities, upon and subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement; WHEREAS, Asset Seller owns certain assets set forth on Schedule 1.1 hereto related to the operation of the Business (collectively, the “ Purchased Assets ”);
