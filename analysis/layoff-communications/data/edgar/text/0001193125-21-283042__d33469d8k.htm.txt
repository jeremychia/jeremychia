On September 22, 2021, Amarin Corporation plc (“Amarin”) announced that it was implementing a new go-to-market strategy for VASCEPA ® (icosapent ethyl), resulting in a reduction of Amarin’s field force to approximately 300 sales representatives. Amarin estimates that it will incur approximately $15 million in charges related to the reduction in force, substantially all of which are cash expenditures for one-time termination benefits and associated costs. Amarin expects to record the charges in the third quarter of 2021 and to make substantially all of the related payments by the end of the first quarter of 2022.
