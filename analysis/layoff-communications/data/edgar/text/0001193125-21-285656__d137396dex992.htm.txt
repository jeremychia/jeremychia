MKS and Atotech provide different customer solutions in key advanced electronics markets, with MKS’ expertise in drilling and Atotech’s in electroplating. PCBs are becoming increasingly complex as miniaturization is creating new challenges where reliability, productivity and peak performance are critical. The roadmap for next-generation PCB interconnects continues to accelerate the need for more integrated solutions that enable yield and throughput gains. The combination of Atotech’s expertise in electroplating and chemistry and MKS’ strengths in lasers, laser systems, optics and motion will enable innovative solutions for customers in the areas of materials processing and complex applications.
