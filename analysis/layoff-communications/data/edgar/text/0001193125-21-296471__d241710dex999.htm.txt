EX-99.9 11 d241710dex999.htm EX-99.9 EX-99.9 Exhibit 99.9 Antonio Employee Video Transcript Hi everyone, today is an exciting day in our 40-year history, and the beginning of a new phase for the company. We all know that the world is changing faster than ever, and sustainability is an imperative. Over the years, we have taken significant steps to accelerate the digital transformation of the industries we serve by optimizing their assets to run safer, greener, longer and faster. Today, I’m excited to announce a transformative, strategic transaction and enhanced partnership with Emerson that will advance our global leadership in industrial software. This strategic investment from Emerson will add complimentary Emerson software businesses to the AspenTech portfolio. Emerson is an ideal strategic partner aligned with AspenTech on driving profitable growth. As you may know, Emerson is a global technology and engineering company serving the industrial, commercial and residential end-markets. We have been commercial partners with Emerson for several years and know their team well. Emerson’s leading software assets, including the world-class OSI business focused on power transmission and distribution management and its exploration and production software unit, are highly complementary to AspenTech’s existing portfolio and will open up new high-growth opportunities and expand our offerings in existing end-markets. This combination will enhance our ability to support the world’s carbon reduction ambitions by enabling companies to provide newer and cleaner forms of renewable energy such as solar and wind power and more efficiently manage these resources. From an ownership perspective, AspenTech shareholders will receive 6 billion dollars in cash in return for 45% ownership of pro forma AspenTech. Emerson shareholders will receive 55% investment in pro forma AspenTech. As part of this combination, AspenTech expects to conclude an expanded commercial agreement with Emerson that will accelerate and enable the distribution and availability of AspenTech’s innovative asset of communication solutions into many industries. For you, a lot will look familiar and little will change. AspenTech will continue to be headquartered in Bedford, Massachusetts. We will also remain an independent, publicly traded software company under the existing AspenTech NASDAQ ticker. And I look forward to continuing to lead the new AspenTech with our executive management team. As we take this step, however, we expect you to see more opportunities to expand your career and skills, more resources to fuel your innovation and even more focus on our ability to advance digitalization to help our customers. And the world become more sustainable for generations to come. Our success is a direct result of your passion and dedication to our customers and colleagues around the world. I thank you for that, and I look forward to that remaining core to our success going forward. Today’s announcement is just the first step in a month’s long process to close the transaction. But for now, I want to express my gratitude for the support and tireless hard work displayed by each and every one of you. The future is incredibly bright for AspenTech as we move towards our next chapter as the new AspenTech. I’m excited for what’s ahead.
