(a) At the Effective Time, upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the DGCL, First Merger Sub and the Company will consummate the First Merger, pursuant to which First Merger Sub will be merged with and into the Company, following which the separate corporate existence of First Merger Sub will cease, and the Company will continue as the Surviving Corporation after the First Merger and as a direct, wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent ( provided , that references to the Company for periods after the Effective Time until the Second Effective Time will include the Surviving Corporation).
