Subject to Purchaser’s right to dispute the existence of a Title Benefit and the Title Benefit Amount asserted with respect thereto, the amount of any decrease to the Unadjusted Purchase Price under Section 3.2(g) with respect to a given Subject Company Group shall be offset by the sum of the aggregate Title Benefit Amounts attributable to all such Title Benefits with respect to such Subject Company Group; provided that for the avoidance of doubt, that a Title Benefit may only offset a Defect, and may not result in any standalone increase to the Unadjusted Purchase Price. For avoidance of doubt, the aggregate Title Benefit Amounts attributable to the effects of all Title Benefits of an affected Well or DSU shall not exceed the Allocated Value of the affected Well or DSU.
