RECITALS WHEREAS , CGAC is a blank check company and was formed for the purpose of effecting a merger, share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses; WHEREAS , Merger Sub is a newly formed Cayman Islands exempted company limited by shares, wholly owned by CGAC, and was formed for the purpose of effectuating the Merger; WHEREAS , the parties hereto desire and intend to effect a business combination transaction whereby Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company (the “ Merger ”), with the Company being the surviving entity and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of CGAC (the Company is hereinafter referred to for the periods from and after the Effective Time as the “ Surviving Entity ”), the Merger to occur upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Cayman Companies Act (As Revised) (the “ Cayman Act ”); WHEREAS , following the date of this Agreement, but prior to the Effective Time, CGAC will use its reasonable best efforts to enter into certain private sales of Equity Securities issued by CGAC in accordance with the terms of Section 8.3 of this Agreement, in an effort to achieve the Gross Proceeds Target; WHEREAS , for U.S. federal income tax purposes, (a) it is intended that the Merger will qualify as a “reorganization” under Section 368(a)(1)(A) and Section 368(a)(2)(E) of the Code and (b) this Agreement is intended to constitute and hereby is adopted as a “plan of reorganization” with respect to the Merger within the meaning of Treasury Regulations Sections 1.368-2(g) and 1.368-3(a) for purposes of Sections 354, 361 and 368 of the Code and the Treasury Regulations thereunder (clauses (a) and (b) , the “ Intended Tax Treatment ”); WHEREAS , the Company is currently applying to continue its legal personality from Cyprus to the Cayman Islands pursuant to sections 354I-354IH of the Cyprus Companies Law (Cap.113) and section 201 of the Cayman Act (the “ Domestication ”). The Company will adopt a new memorandum and articles of association, governed by the laws of the Cayman Islands, which will fully replace its existing Cypriot-law governed memorandum and articles of association (the “ Cayman Articles ”), at 00:01 (Cyprus time) on the day immediately following the day on which a copy of the official certificate from the Cayman Islands Companies Registry certifying the continuation of the Company to the Cayman Islands is filed with the Cypriot Companies Registrar (the date when the Cayman Articles are adopted being, for the purposes of this Agreement, the “ Domestication Effective Date ”).
