Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 17, 2024, Flagstar Bank, National Association, a wholly-owned subsidiary of New York Community Bancorp, Inc. (the “Company”), announced a reduction in force involving approximately 8% of our employees. This decision was based on cost-reduction initiatives intended to reduce operating expenses. The Company currently estimates that it will incur one-time cash charges of approximately $20 million in connection with the reduction in force, primarily consisting of notice period and severance payments, and related costs. The Company expects that the majority of these charges will be incurred in the fourth quarter of 2024, and that the reduction in force will be substantially complete by the end of 2024, subject to local law and consultation requirements, which may extend the process beyond the end of 2024 in certain jurisdictions. The charges the Company expects to incur are subject to assumptions, including local law requirements, and actual charges may differ from the estimate above. Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure. On October 17, 2024, the Company issued a press release announcing the reduction in force. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is furnished herewith.
