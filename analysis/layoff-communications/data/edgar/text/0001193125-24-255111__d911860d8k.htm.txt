On November 8, 2024, the Company implemented a strategic reorganization to sharpen the Company’s focus on growth. This reorganization resulted in a workforce reduction of approximately 10% and will focus the Company’s commercial strategy on the highest growth drivers. The reorganization will also improve operational efficiency, with anticipated post-reorganization annualized cash savings of more than $3.5 million. The Company expects to recognize approximately $0.4 million in total expenses for severance and related benefits for employees impacted by the reduction in force, consisting primarily of severance payments and related benefits.
