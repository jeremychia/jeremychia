Item 8.01. Other Events. Transfer of Publicly Traded Securities to the OTCID Basic Market As previously disclosed in the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed on December 22, 2025, the Company’s Class A common stock (the “Common Stock”) was delisted from the Nasdaq Capital Market effective December 22, 2025. On that date, the Common Stock and the Company’s Public Warrants began trading on the OTCQB Venture Market under the symbols “MSPR” and “MSPRZ,” respectively, and the Company’s New Warrants began trading on the OTCID Basic Market under the symbol “MSPRW.” On January 5, 2026, the Company was notified by OTC Markets staff that the trading of its Public Warrants will be transferred to the OTCID Basic Market effective January 6, 2026, unless and until the Company satisfies the listing requirements of the OTCQB Venture Market for the Public Warrants. The Company intends to continue pursuing qualification for trading of the Public Warrants on the OTCQB Venture Market; however, there can be no assurance that the Company will satisfy the applicable requirements or that its application will be approved. Trading on the OTCID may result in limited market quotations, reduced liquidity, and lower trading prices for the Company’s securities, and may adversely affect the Company’s ability to raise additional capital. There can be no assurance that an active trading market for the Company’s securities will develop or be sustained. Employee Furlough On December 22, 2025, MSP Recovery, Inc. (the “Company”) furloughed seven employees, representing approximately 33% of its workforce, in connection with its liquidity management and restructuring efforts. During the furlough, affected employees will not perform services for the Company and will not receive salary or wages. The Company cannot predict the duration of the furlough and may recall employees based on operational needs and capital availability. Although the Company’s offices were closed for the holiday period, the furlough is expected to materially impact the Company’s core operations beginning January 5, 2026, and may have a material adverse effect on the Company’s revenues and operating results.
