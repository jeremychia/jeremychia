THOSE STOCKHOLDERS EXECUTING THIS AGREEMENT And CHAD HUGHES dated as of October 13, 2026 AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER This Agreement and Plan of Merger (this “ Agreement ”), dated as of October 13, 2026, is entered into among Cardinal Infrastructure Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“ Parent ”), CHBC Intermediate Holdco, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“ Merger Sub 1 ”), CHBC Holding Company, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“ Merger Sub 2 ” and together with Merger Sub 1, “ Merger Subs ”), HBC Holdco, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“ Target ”), Hughes Brothers Construction Inc., a Florida corporation (“ Company ”), those stockholders of the Company executing the signature page hereto (“ Stockholders ”), and CHAD HUGHES, a resident of Florida, solely in his capacity as Stockholder Representative (“ Stockholder Representative ”). RECITALS A. The Stockholders are the owners, beneficially and of record, of (i) all the issued and outstanding capital stock (the “ Pre-Contribution Shares ”) of the Company and (ii) all the issued and outstanding membership interests (the “ USC LLC Interests ”) in Utility Services Construction, LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“ USC ”). B. Prior to the date of this Agreement, Stockholders formed the Target. C. Prior to the Closing Date, the Target, Stockholders and the Company shall consummate the following transactions: (i) at least two (2) days prior to the Closing Date, Stockholders contribute all of the outstanding Pre-Contribution Shares of the Company to the Target in exchange for 100% of the equity interests of the Target, resulting in the Target becoming the sole owner of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company (the “ Contribution ”); (ii) on the date of the Contribution, and immediately following the Contribution, Stockholders cause the Target to elect to treat the Company as a “qualified subchapter S subsidiary” within the meaning of Section 1361(b)(3)(B) of the Code by executing and filing (via certified mail) a valid IRS Form 8869, Qualified Subchapter S Subsidiary Election, effective as of the date of the Contribution, indicating on Line 14 of such Form 8869 that the election is being made in combination with a reorganization under Section 368(a)(1)(F) of the Code described in IRS Revenue Ruling 2008-18 (the “ Q-Sub Election ”); and (iii) at least one (1) day following the mailing of the Q-Sub Election and at least one (1) day prior to the Closing Date, the Company converts from a Florida corporation to Hughes Brothers Construction, LLC, a Florida limited liability company (after such conversion referred to herein as “ Company ” and such conversion, the “ Conversion ”) and as a result, the Company will be treated as an entity disregarded as separate from the Target, in each case, for U.S. federal (and applicable state and local) income tax purposes (clauses (i) through (iii) collectively, the “ Restructuring ”). D. Prior to the Contribution, the Stockholders will contribute the USC LLC Interests to the Company, resulting in USC becoming the sole wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company (after giving effect to the Subsidiary Distribution) (the “ USC Contribution ”). E. As a result of the Restructuring, immediately prior to the Closing Date, all of the issued and outstanding stock of Target will be held beneficially and of record by the Stockholders, and all of the equity interests of the Company (the “ Equity Interests ”) will be held beneficially and of record by Target. F. The parties intend for Parent to acquire the Group Companies on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement. G. In furtherance of such acquisition of the Company by Parent, and on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law (the “ DGCL ”), Merger Sub 1 shall be merged with and into the Target (the “ First Merger ”), with the Target surviving the First Merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, and each outstanding share of the Target Common Stock will be converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration. H. Immediately following the First Merger, and on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with the DGCL and the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (the “ DLLCA ”), the Target shall be merged with and into Merger Sub 2 (the “ Second Merger ” and, together with the First Merger, the “ Mergers ”), with Merger Sub 2 surviving the Second Merger as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. I. The board of directors of the Target (the “ Target Board ”) and the managers of the Company (the “ Company Managers ”) have unanimously (a) determined that this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, including the Mergers, are in the best interests of the Target and its stockholders and the Company and its members, respectively, (b) approved and declared fair and advisable this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, including the Mergers, and (c) resolved to recommend adoption of this Agreement by the stockholders of the Target and members of the Company, respectively, in accordance with the DGCL and the DLLCA. J. The board of directors of Parent and the governing bodies of Merger Subs have (a) determined that this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, including the Mergers, are in the best interests of Parent, Merger Subs and their respective stockholders, and (b) approved, authorized and declared advisable this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby, including the Mergers. K. For U.S. federal income Tax purposes, the parties intend that the First Merger and the Second Merger, taken together, qualify as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code, and that this Agreement be, and is hereby, adopted as a plan of reorganization within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code. L. A portion of the cash otherwise payable by Parent to the Stockholders in connection with the Mergers shall be placed in escrow by Parent, the release of which shall be contingent upon certain events and conditions, all as set forth in this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. M. As a condition and material inducement to the Parent’s execution and delivery of this Agreement, the Stockholders are executing and delivering to Parent, simultaneously with the execution of this Agreement, the Employment Agreements, the Restrictive Covenant Agreements and the Lock-Up Agreements (each as defined in this Agreement), each of which shall become effective upon the Closing and subject to the terms contained herein and therein.
