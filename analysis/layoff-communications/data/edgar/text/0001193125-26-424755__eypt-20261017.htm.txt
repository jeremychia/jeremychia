Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 17, 2026, following review of topline results from LUCIA, the second pivotal Phase 3 clinical trial evaluating DURAVYU™ for the treatment of wet age-related macular degeneration (wet AMD), the Board of Directors of the Company approved a strategic prioritization plan (the “Prioritization”) designed to reduce operating expenses and focus resources on the Company’s late-stage program for DURAVYU™ in diabetic macular edema (DME). The Prioritization includes a plan to conclude the pivotal Phase 3 wet AMD clinical trials early and reduce the Company’s workforce by approximately 53%, with the substantial majority of the workforce reduction expected to occur during the fourth quarter of 2026 and to be completed during the first half of 2027. As a result of the Prioritization, the Company estimates it will incur aggregate charges of approximately $9 million related to employee separation benefits, including employee severance and other related expenses, a majority of which are expected to be recognized during the fourth quarter of 2026 with the remaining charges recognized through the third quarter of 2027. The Company expects that substantially all of these charges will result in cash expenditures over the same time period. The estimate of charges and expenditures that the Company expects to incur and the timing thereof are subject to a number of assumptions, and actual results may differ. As the Prioritization is implemented, the Company’s management will reevaluate the estimated charges and expenses set forth above and may revise them as appropriate, consistent with generally accepted accounting principles. The Company may also incur other non-cash charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, the Prioritization.
