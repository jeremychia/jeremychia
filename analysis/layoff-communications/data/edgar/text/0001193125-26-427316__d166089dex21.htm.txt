WHEREAS , the Boards of Directors or other governing bodies, as applicable, of each of Parent, Merger Sub, Seller, and the Company have unanimously approved and declared advisable this Agreement, the merger of Merger Sub with and into the Company, with the Company surviving (the “ First Step Merger ”), and, immediately thereafter and as part of an integrated plan, the merger of the Company with and into Parent, with Parent surviving (the “ Second Step Merger ” and, together with the First Step Merger, the “ Mergers ”), and the other Transactions, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with applicable Law; WHEREAS , Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding units of membership interest in the Company (the “ Company Units ”); and WHEREAS , the Parties intend that, as consideration for the Merger, each Company Unit issued and outstanding immediately prior to the First Effective Time shall be converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration; and WHEREAS , for U.S. federal income tax purposes, it is intended that (a) the Mergers, taken together, constitute an integrated plan and qualify as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code, and (b) this Agreement constitute and be adopted as a “plan of reorganization” within the meaning of Sections 1.368-2(g) and 1.368-3(a) of the Treasury Regulations; and WHEREAS , on October 20, 2026, (a) Savarms Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and wholly owned Subsidiary of Seller (“ Savarms Holdings ”), merged with and into Seller pursuant to certificates of merger filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Texas and the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, respectively (the “ Savarms Merger ”), and (b) as a result of the Savarms Merger, the separate existence of Savarms Holdings ceased and Seller continued as the surviving limited partnership; and WHEREAS , prior to the execution of this Agreement, Seller and the Company Group completed certain internal transactions pursuant to which (a) Ludus Indemnity, Inc. and its business were separated from, and ceased to be a Subsidiary of, the Company, such that neither Ludus Indemnity, Inc. nor its business, assets or Liabilities form part of the Company Group or the Transactions, as more fully described in that certain Assignment and Assumption Agreement, dated as of October 20, 2026, by and among Seller and the Company, and (b) the Company Group assigned and transferred to Seller or one or more of its Affiliates (other than the Company Group) certain antique firearms (“ Antique Firearms ”), as more fully described in that certain Assignment and Assumption Agreement, dated as of October 20, 2026, by and among Savage Arms, Inc. and Hilcorp Investments, L.P. (together, the “ Pre-Closing Restructuring ”). THE MERGER; CLOSING Section 1.01. The Merger . Upon the terms set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with the DLLC Act and the Laws of the State of Nevada, as applicable, (a) at the First Effective Time, Merger Sub shall be merged with and into the Company in the First Step Merger. As a result of the First Step Merger, the separate existence of Merger Sub shall cease and the Company shall continue as the surviving limited liability company (the “ Intermediate Surviving Company ”) and shall continue its existence under the DLLC Act as a wholly owned Subsidiary of Parent. Immediately after the First Step Merger, and as part of an integrated plan, the Intermediate Surviving Company shall be merged with and into Parent in the Second Step Merger. As a result of the Second Step Merger, the separate existence of the Intermediate Surviving Company shall cease and Parent shall continue as the surviving corporation (the “ Surviving Company ”) and shall continue its existence under the Laws of the State of Nevada. The First Step Merger and the Second Step Merger shall have the effects set forth herein and in the applicable provisions of the DLLC Act and the Laws of the State of Nevada, as applicable. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, and subject thereto, at the First Effective Time all the property, rights, privileges, powers and franchises of the Company and Merger Sub shall vest in the Intermediate Surviving Company, and all Liabilities and duties of the Company and Merger Sub shall become the Liabilities and duties of the Intermediate Surviving Company; and at the Second Effective Time all the property, rights, privileges, powers and franchises of the Intermediate Surviving Company shall vest in the Surviving Company, and all Liabilities and duties of the Intermediate Surviving Company shall become the Liabilities and duties of the Surviving Company. Section 1.02. Closing . The closing of the Merger (the “ Closing ”) shall take place remotely by exchange of documents and signatures (or their electronic counterparts) at 10:00 a.m. prevailing Central time on the date hereof (the “ Closing Date ”), or such other time as agreed by the Parties. Section 1.03. Effective Time . Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, as promptly as practicable on the Closing Date, (a) the Company shall file a certificate of merger (the “ First Certificate of Merger ”) with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware executed in accordance with, and in such form as is required by, the relevant provisions of the DLLC Act, and shall make all other filings or recordings required under the DLLC Act in connection with the First Step Merger. The First Step Merger shall become effective at the time the First Certificate of Merger is duly filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, or at such later time as the Company and Parent shall agree and specify in the First Certificate of Merger in accordance with the DLLC Act (the “ First Effective Time ”), and (b) immediately thereafter, Parent shall file the certificates or articles of merger required to effect the Second Step Merger (the “ Second Certificates of Merger ”) with the Secretary of State of the State of Nevada and the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware and shall make all other filings or recordings required under applicable Law in connection with the Second Step Merger. The Second Step Merger shall become effective at the later of the time the applicable certificates or articles of merger are duly filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Nevada and the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, or at such later time as Parent and the Company shall agree and specify in such certificates or articles of merger in accordance with applicable Law (the “ Second Effective Time ”). References to the “ Effective Time ” in this Agreement without further qualification shall refer to the Second Effective Time. Section 1.04. Organizational Documents of the Surviving Entities . (a) At the First Effective Time, the certificate of formation of the Company, as in effect immediately prior to the First Effective Time, shall, by virtue of the First Step Merger, be amended and restated in its entirety, in the form set forth in Exhibit A , and as so amended and restated shall be the certificate of formation of the Intermediate Surviving Company until the Second Effective Time. At the Second Effective Time, the articles of incorporation of Parent, as in effect immediately prior to the Second Effective Time, shall be the articles of incorporation of the Surviving Company until thereafter changed or amended as provided therein or by applicable Law. Each of the Company, Merger Sub and Parent shall take all action necessary to carry out the actions contemplated by this Section 1.04(a) . 2 (b) The limited liability company agreement of the Company, as in effect immediately prior to the First Effective Time, shall, by virtue of the First Step Merger, be amended and restated in its entirety, in the form set forth in Exhibit B (the “ Intermediate Surviving Company LLC Agreement ”), and as so amended and restated shall be the limited liability company agreement of the Intermediate Surviving Company, until the Second Effective Time. The bylaws of Parent, as in effect immediately prior to the Second Effective Time, shall be the bylaws of the Surviving Company until thereafter changed or amended as provided therein or by applicable Law. Each of the Company, Merger Sub and Parent shall take all action necessary to carry out the actions contemplated by this Section 1.04(b) . Section 1.05. Managers, Directors and Officers of the Surviving Company . Subject to Sections 1.1 and 1.2 of the Investor Rights Agreement, the directors or managers, as the case may be, of Parent immediately prior to the Effective Time shall continue to be the directors or managers, as the case may be, of the Surviving Company, in each case until the earlier of their resignation or removal or until their respective successors are duly elected and qualified, as the case may be. The officers of Parent immediately prior to the Effective Time shall continue to be the officers of the Surviving Company, in each case until the earlier of their resignation or removal or until their respective successors are duly elected or appointed and qualified, as the case may be. If, during the Negotiation Period, Seller and Parent are able to resolve any Disputed Amounts, then such agreed upon amounts will become final, binding, non-appealable, and conclusive on the Parties. (iv) If a final resolution is not obtained within the Negotiation Period, Parent and Seller will retain, within fifteen (15) days following the expiration of the Negotiation Period, for the benefit of all the Parties a nationally recognized independent public accounting or advisory firm that has proven experience in resolving the matters related to the Disputed Amounts and that is mutually agreed to by Parent and Seller (such mutually agreed to public accounting firm, the “ Independent Accountant ”) to resolve any remaining Disputed Amounts. If the Independent Accountant is retained, then (A) Parent and Seller will each submit to the Independent Accountant in writing, not later than fifteen (15) days after the Independent Accountant is retained, their respective positions with respect to the Disputed Amounts, together with such supporting documentation as they deem necessary or as the Independent Accountant requests, and (B) the Independent Accountant will, within thirty (30) days after receiving the positions of Parent and Seller and all supplementary supporting documentation requested by the Independent Accountant (or such longer period as may be agreed to by Parent and Seller, if requested by the Independent Accountant), render its decision as to the Disputed Amounts in a written report, which decision will be final, binding, non-appealable, and conclusive on the Parties absent manifest error. Neither Parent nor Seller will have or conduct any communication, either written or oral, with the Independent Accountant without such other Party being present, and such other Party shall receive a concurrent copy of any written communication of the Independent 5 Accountant with the other Party. Parent and Seller, and their respective Representatives, will cooperate reasonably with the Independent Accountant during its engagement and respond on a timely basis to all requests for information or access to documents or personnel made by the Independent Accountant, all with the intent to fairly, reasonably and in good faith resolve all Disputed Amounts as promptly as reasonably practicable.
