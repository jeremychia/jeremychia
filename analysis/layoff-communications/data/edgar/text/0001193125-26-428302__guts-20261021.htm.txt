On October 21, 2026, Fractyl Health, Inc. (the “ Company ”) adopted a plan to sharpen its strategic focus on its Revita ® program in connection with a strategic reprioritization. As part of the plan, the Company will reduce its workforce by approximately 45% of the Company’s current headcount. The reduction in force is expected to be completed during the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company estimates that it will incur charges of approximately $2 million for severance and other employee termination-related costs, with the majority of such costs to be incurred in the fourth quarter of 2026. The estimated costs that the Company expects to incur, and the timing thereof, are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ. The Company may also incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, the workforce reduction, including potential impairment charges, if any. If required, the Company will amend this Current Report on Form 8-K at such time as its management is able in good faith to estimate the amount, or range of amounts, of these additional charges. Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. Retirement of Executive Officer In connection with the workforce reduction, on October 21, 2026, Jay Caplan announced his retirement. effective October 30, 2026 (the “ Separation Date ”), from his executive roles as the Company’s President and Chief Product Officer. Mr. Caplan will be entitled to severance benefits as set forth in his employment agreement. Mr. Caplan’s Employment Letter Agreement, dated January 26, 2024, by and between the Company and Mr. Caplan (the “ Caplan Agreement ”), is filed as Exhibit 10.7 to the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-1/A (File No. 333-276046), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “ SEC ”) on January 29, 2024. Mr. Caplan is expected to continue to support the Company as a senior advisor to the leadership team following the Separation Date. In connection with Mr. Caplan’s retirement, the Company intends to enter into separation and consulting agreements with Mr. Caplan that will provide for the terms of Mr. Caplan’s cessation of employment. Such separation and consulting agreements have not yet been finalized. The Company will provide a description of the material terms of the separation and consulting agreements by filing a Current Report on Form 8-K after the information is determined or becomes available. Appointment Of President In connection with Mr. Caplan’s departure, on October 21, 2026, the Board of Directors of the Company appointed Harith Rajagopalan, M.D., Ph.D., the Company’s Co-Founder, Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board, to serve as President of the Company, effective as of the Separation Date, in addition to his continuing roles as Chief Executive Officer and as a director of the Company. Dr. Rajagopalan will continue to serve as the Company’s principal executive officer. Item 8.01 Other Events.
