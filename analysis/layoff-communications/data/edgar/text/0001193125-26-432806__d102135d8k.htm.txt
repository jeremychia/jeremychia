Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 22, 2026, the board of directors of the Company approved the discontinuation of the Company’s development of AlloNK ® (AB-101), its non-genetically modified, allogeneic natural killer (NK) cell therapy candidate, as the Company conducts a process to explore strategic alternatives to maximize stockholder value, which includes a plan to implement a reduction of the Company’s workforce by approximately 94% of the Company’s current employee base (the “Wind Down”). At this time, the Company cannot reasonably estimate the total costs and charges in connection with the Wind Down. These costs and charges will relate primarily to contract termination and other costs related to the discontinuation of its clinical trials, costs related to leases, other existing liabilities and operational closing activities, and costs related to the workforce reduction. The Company expects to complete the workforce reduction in the first quarter of 2027 and to incur a significant majority of the costs related to the Wind Down in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company may also incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, the Wind Down. The estimated timing of the Wind Down as well as the estimated timing of the related costs, are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ.
