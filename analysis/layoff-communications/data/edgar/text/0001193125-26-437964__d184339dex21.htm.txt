At the Effective Time, by virtue of the Merger and without any action on the part of the Company, Parent, Merger Sub or the holders of any shares of Company Common Stock or any shares of capital stock of Merger Sub: (a) Capital Shares of Merger Sub. All shares of Company Common Stock that are owned by the Company as treasury shares immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be canceled and shall cease to exist and no consideration shall be delivered in exchange therefor. SECTION 2.03. Treatment of Equity-Based Awards . Prior to the Effective Time, the Board of Directors of the Company (or, if appropriate, any duly authorized committee thereof administering the Equity Plans) shall adopt such resolutions and take such other actions as may be required to provide for the following: (a) Each vested Company Stock Option outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall, as of the Effective Time, be canceled and the holder thereof shall then become entitled to receive solely, in full satisfaction of the rights of such holder with respect thereto, a lump-sum cash payment, without interest, equal to the product of (i) the number of 7 shares of Company Common Stock subject to such vested Company Stock Option immediately prior to the Effective Time and (ii) the excess, if any, of (A) the Merger Consideration over (B) the per share exercise price of such vested Company Stock Option. Any Company Stock Option with a per share exercise price that is equal to or greater than the Merger Consideration shall be canceled for no consideration. (b) Each unvested Company Stock Option outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall, as of the Effective Time, be canceled and converted into the contingent right to receive an amount in cash, without interest (a “Contingent Cash Award”), equal to the product of (i) the number of shares of Company Common Stock subject to such unvested Company Stock Option immediately prior to the Effective Time and (ii) the excess, if any, of (A) the Merger Consideration over (B) the per share exercise price of such unvested Company Stock Option. Each such Contingent Cash Award shall have the same terms and conditions (including service-based vesting conditions) that applied to the corresponding unvested Company Stock Option immediately prior to the Effective Time, except for terms rendered inoperative by reason of the Transactions or administrative or ministerial changes. Any unvested Company Stock Option with a per share exercise price that is equal to or greater than the Merger Consideration shall be canceled for no consideration. (c) Notwithstanding anything in this Agreement to the contrary, each Company RSU outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall, as of the Effective Time, be cancelled and converted into a Contingent Cash Award with respect to an amount equal to the product of (i) the Merger Consideration, multiplied by (ii) the number of shares of Company Common Stock subject to such Company RSU immediately prior to the Effective Time (assuming solely for purposes of computing the target amount of cash pursuant to performance-based vesting conditions, the attainment of all applicable performance goals at target), with the same terms and conditions (including service- and/or performance-based vesting conditions) that applied to the corresponding Company RSU immediately prior to the Effective Time, except for terms rendered inoperative by reason of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement or administrative or ministerial changes.
