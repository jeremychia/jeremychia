RECITALS Background and Restructuring WHEREAS, Company is in the business of laboratory and interior construction, including the provision and servicing of laboratory furniture, interior woodwork, lab equipment, educational furniture, interior specialties, seating, environmental rooms, and related parts and supplies for the foregoing (the “ Business ”); WHEREAS, prior to giving effect to the Restructuring (defined below), the Equityholders directly hold 100% of the issued and outstanding equity interests of Company (the “ Securities ”); WHEREAS, prior to the Contribution (defined below), Company shall dissolve Black Heath Group, LLC (“ Black Heath Group ”), a Delaware limited liability company and wholly owned Subsidiary of Company (the “ Dissolution ”); WHEREAS, in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby, following the Signing Date but at least three (3) Business Days prior to the Closing Date: (a) the Equityholders will form, or cause to be formed, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware (“ Seller ”); (b) a protective S corporation election on IRS Form 2553 will be made for Seller to be taxed as an “S corporation” under the Code; (c) the Equityholders will contribute 100% of the issued and outstanding Securities to Seller, in exchange for 100% of the issued and outstanding equity interests of Seller in a transaction intended to be a tax-free reorganization within the meaning of Section 368(a)(1)(F) of the Code and pursuant to Revenue Ruling 2008-18 (the “ Contribution ”); and (d) Seller will file an IRS Form 8869 electing to treat Company as a “qualified subchapter S subsidiary,” within the meaning of Section 1361(b)(3)(B) of the Code, effective as of the date of the Contribution (the “ QSub Election ”) and mail such QSub Election to the IRS; WHEREAS, at least two (2) Business Days after the date of the Contribution, Seller will cause Company to convert from a Virginia corporation to “Nycom, LLC”, a Delaware limited liability company (the “ Conversion ”; the Conversion, collectively with the Dissolution, Contribution and the QSub Election, the “ Restructuring ”); and WHEREAS, the Equityholders will cause Seller to execute and deliver to Purchaser a joinder to this Agreement as a Seller Party, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Purchaser (the “ Joinder Agreement ”), promptly following the incorporation of Seller. Sale Transaction WHEREAS, following the Restructuring, Purchaser desires to acquire the Business from Seller, and Seller Parties desire to cause Seller to sell the Business to Purchaser; WHEREAS, following the Restructuring and immediately prior to the Closing, Seller will directly hold 100% of the issued and outstanding Securities; and WHEREAS, to effect the acquisition and sale of the Business, pursuant to this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell to Purchaser and Purchaser will purchase 100% of the issued and outstanding Securities.
