Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities As previously disclosed in the Original Report, on May 1, 2026, management of the Company elected to wind down and discontinue its butter product offerings to focus on its core egg product categories, with such discontinuation expected to be substantially completed by the end of fiscal 2026. On October 30, 2026, management of the Company determined in good faith that the Company expects to incur costs and charges associated with the wind-down that are now reasonably estimable, as follows: Description Estimated Range Cash / Non-Cash Expected Timing Inventory Obsolescence $8.0 million to $13.0 million Non-cash charges By the end of fiscal 2026 TOTAL $8.0 million to $13.0 million Inventory obsolescence includes write-downs of finished goods butter, bulk butter and packaging. The range noted above includes the $7.8 million of inventory obsolescence the Company recognized in the 13-week period ended June 28, 2026. The Company does not expect to incur material costs other than non-cash charges related to inventory obsolescence. The Company does not expect these charges to result in future cash expenditures. The Company may also incur additional costs not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or that are associated with, the wind-down of its butter business.
