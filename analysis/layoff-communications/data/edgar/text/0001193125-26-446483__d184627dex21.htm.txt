WHEREAS, the Boards of Directors of Sierra, Alpha and Alpha Merger Sub have each approved and declared advisable this Agreement and the Merger and the other transactions contemplated hereby (the Merger and the other transactions contemplated hereby, collectively, the “ Transactions ”) and determined that it is advisable and in the best interests of their respective companies and stockholders to consummate the Merger and the other Transactions on the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement; WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of Alpha (the “ Alpha Board ”) has, subject to Section 6.4 , resolved to recommend the approval of the Alpha Common Stock Issuance by the Alpha stockholders; WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of Alpha Merger Sub has resolved to recommend the adoption of this Agreement by its sole stockholder and, following the execution and delivery of this Agreement, Alpha shall adopt and approve this Agreement and the Transactions as the sole stockholder of Alpha Merger Sub; WHEREAS, the Board of Directors of Sierra (the “ Sierra Board ”) has, subject to Section 6.4 , resolved to recommend the adoption of this Agreement by the Sierra stockholders; WHEREAS, for U.S. federal income Tax purposes, the parties intend that the Merger qualify as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the “ Code ”) (the “ Intended Tax Treatment ”), and this Agreement constitute, and be adopted as, a “plan of reorganization” within the meaning of Treasury Regulations §§ 1.368-2(g) and 1.368-3(a); and WHEREAS, contemporaneously with the execution and delivery of this Agreement, and as a condition and inducement to Sierra’s willingness to enter into this Agreement, Alpha, BCP Helios Aggregator L.P., VP Helios Investments LLC and BEFIP III – ESC Helios Holdco L.P. are entering into that certain letter agreement, pursuant to which, among other things, Alpha has agreed to amend the Certificate of Designations of Series A Perpetual Preferred Stock of Alpha on the Closing Date to provide for, among other things, a commitment to redeem (i) $150,000,000 of shares of Alpha Preferred Stock at a redemption price of 93% of the Base Amount (as defined in the Amended and Restated Certificate of Designations of Series A Perpetual Preferred Stock of Alpha) within ninety (90) days of Closing and (ii) $150,000,000 of shares of Alpha Preferred Stock at a redemption price of 96% of the Base Amount within three (3) years of the initial redemption deadline. AGREEMENT NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, premises, representations and warranties and agreements contained in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged, and intending to be legally bound, the parties to this Agreement agree as follows: ARTICLE 1 THE MERGER 1.1 The Merger . (a) At the Effective Time, Alpha Merger Sub shall be merged with and into Sierra in accordance with the DGCL, and upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement, whereupon the separate existence of Alpha Merger Sub shall cease and Sierra shall continue as the surviving corporation (the “ Surviving Corporation ”). As a result of the Merger, the Surviving Corporation shall become a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Alpha. References herein to “Sierra” with respect to the period from and after the Effective Time shall be deemed to be references to the “Surviving Corporation”. (b) The Merger shall have the effects specified in the DGCL. From and after the Effective Time, the Surviving Corporation shall possess all the property, rights, powers, privileges, immunities and franchises and be subject to all of the obligations, liabilities and duties of Sierra and Alpha Merger Sub, all as provided under the DGCL. 1.2 Closing . The closing of the Merger (the “ Closing ”) shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of executed documents, commencing at 9:00 a.m., New York City time, as soon as practicable (and, in any event, within three (3) Business Days) after satisfaction or, to the extent permitted hereunder, waiver of all applicable conditions set forth in Article 7 (except for any conditions that by their nature can only be satisfied at the Closing, but subject to the satisfaction of such conditions or, to the extent permitted hereunder, waiver by the party entitled to waive such conditions at the Closing) or at such other time and place as Alpha and Sierra shall agree in writing. The date and time at which the Closing occurs is referred to herein as the “ Closing Date ”. 9 1.3 Effective Time . On the Closing Date, Sierra and Alpha Merger Sub shall cause a certificate of merger in a form and substance reasonably acceptable to Alpha and Sierra (the “ Certificate of Merger ”) to be filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware in accordance with the relevant provisions of the DGCL and shall make all other filings or recordings required under the DGCL. The Merger shall become effective upon the due filing of the Certificate of Merger with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, or such later time as Alpha and Sierra shall agree and specify in the Certificate of Merger (such time as the Merger becomes effective hereinafter referred to as the “ Effective Time ”). 1.4 Charter and Bylaws of the Surviving Corporation . At the Effective Time, by virtue of the Merger and without any action by Sierra, Alpha, Alpha Merger Sub or any other Person, the certificate of incorporation of Sierra in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be amended and restated in its entirety as set forth as Exhibit A , and as so amended and restated shall be the certificate of incorporation of the Surviving Corporation until thereafter changed or amended as provided therein or by applicable Law. In addition, the parties shall take such actions necessary so that, at the Effective Time, the bylaws of Sierra in effect immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be amended and restated in their entirety as set forth as Exhibit B , and as so amended and restated shall be the bylaws of the Surviving Corporation until thereafter changed or amended as provided therein or by applicable Law. (c) Alpha shall take all actions necessary to cause (i) all of the Alpha Designees and Sierra Designees and the CEO Designee and the Chairman Designee to be appointed, elected and approved as directors of the Alpha Board effective as of the Effective Time and (ii) all Alpha Board members prior to the Closing not designated as Alpha Designees or as Chairman Designee to resign from the Alpha Board effective as of the Effective Time. 2.2 Chief Executive Officer and Board Chairman . (a) Subject to Section 2.2(b) , Alpha shall take all actions necessary to cause, immediately prior to the Effective Time: (i) Brandon Moss, the Chief Executive Officer of Sierra as of the date of this Agreement (the “ CEO Designee ”), to be appointed as the Chief Executive Officer of Alpha effective as of the Effective Time ( provided , that he remains Chief Executive Officer of Sierra as of immediately prior to the Effective Time); and (ii) Brad Forth, the Chairman of the Sierra Board and the Chairman of the Alpha Board as of the date of this Agreement (the “ Chairman Designee ”), to be appointed to serve as the Chairman of the Alpha Board ( provided , that he remains the Chairman of the Alpha Board and the Sierra Board as of immediately prior to the Effective Time). (b) Notwithstanding Section 2.2(a) , in the event that, prior to the Effective Time: (i) the CEO Designee is not then serving as the Chief Executive Officer of Sierra or is unwilling or unable to serve as the Chief Executive Officer of Alpha at the Effective Time as a result of death, removal, resignation or any other reason, then, Sierra (acting through the Sierra Board) and Alpha (acting through the Alpha Board) shall work together in good faith to designate and determine the individual who will serve as the Chief Executive Officer of Alpha at the Effective Time; and (ii) the Chairman Designee is not then serving as the Chairman of the Sierra Board and the Alpha Board or is unwilling or unable to serve as the Chairman of the Alpha Board at the Effective Time, as a result of death, removal, resignation or any other reason, then, Sierra (acting through the Sierra Board) and Alpha (acting through the Alpha Board) shall work together in good faith to designate and determine the individual who will serve as the Chairman of the Alpha Board at the Effective Time. 2.3 Other Executives . At the Effective Time, the parties shall take all necessary action such that (a) the directors of Alpha Merger Sub as of immediately prior to the Effective Time shall become the only directors of the Surviving Corporation and (b) the officers of Sierra as of immediately prior to the Effective Time shall become the initial officers of the Surviving Corporation and shall hold office in accordance with the certificate of incorporation and bylaws of the Surviving Corporation until their respective successors shall have been duly elected, designated or qualified, or until their earlier death, resignation or removal in accordance with the certificate of incorporation and bylaws of the Surviving Corporation. ARTICLE 3 CONVERSION OF SECURITIES IN THE MERGER 3.1 Conversion of Securities . At the Effective Time, by virtue of the Merger and without any action on the part of Alpha, Sierra, Alpha Merger Sub or any holder of any of the following securities: (a) Each share of Sierra Common Stock held by Sierra as treasury stock or held directly by Alpha or Alpha Merger Sub (or any direct or indirect wholly owned Subsidiaries of Sierra, Alpha or Alpha Merger Sub), in each case, immediately prior to the Effective Time, shall automatically be cancelled and shall cease to exist, and no consideration or payment shall be delivered in exchange therefor or in respect thereof. (b) Subject to Section 3.1(a) , Section 3.2 , Section 3.3 and Section 3.4 , each share of Sierra Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be converted into and thereafter represent the right to receive that number of validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable shares of common stock, $0.001 par value per share, of Alpha (“ Alpha Common Stock ”), equal to the Exchange Ratio (the “ Common Stock Merger Consideration ”) and the Fractional Shares Cash Amount to be paid in lieu of fractional shares pursuant to Section 3.3 , if any. The Common Stock Merger Consideration to be issued pursuant to this Section 3.1 and the Fractional Shares Cash Amount to be paid in lieu of fractional shares 12 pursuant to Section 3.3 , if any, are referred to, as applicable, as the “ Merger Consideration ”. For purposes of this Agreement, the “ Exchange Ratio ” means 1.6931. Without limiting the foregoing, each of Alpha and Sierra shall take all actions necessary prior to the Effective Time so that any risk of forfeiture or right of repurchase in favor of Sierra that is applicable to any Sierra Common Stock as of immediately prior to the Effective Time shall apply to the related Alpha Common Stock received as Merger Consideration on the same terms and conditions except that the vesting schedule and repurchase price shall be adjusted to reflect the Exchange Ratio and references to services to or performance of Sierra and its Subsidiaries shall be deemed to refer to Alpha and its Subsidiaries. (c) All of the outstanding shares of Sierra Common Stock shall be converted into the right to receive the Merger Consideration pursuant to this Section 3.1 and all such shares of Sierra Common Stock shall cease to be outstanding and shall cease to exist and, as of the Effective Time, each holder of a certificate representing any such shares of Sierra Common Stock (a “ Sierra Certificate ”) or shares of Sierra Common Stock held in book-entry form (“ Sierra Book-Entry Shares ”) shall cease to have any rights with respect thereto, except the right to receive, in accordance with Section 3.1(b) , the Merger Consideration in accordance with Section 3.4 . (d) Each share of common stock, par value $0.00001, of Alpha Merger Sub (the “ Alpha Merger Sub Common Stock ”) issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be converted into and become one fully paid and non-assessable share of common stock, par value $0.00001, of the Surviving Corporation. 3.2 Certain Adjustments . Notwithstanding anything in this Agreement to the contrary, if, from the date of this Agreement until the Effective Time, the outstanding shares of Alpha Common Stock or Sierra Common Stock shall have been changed into a different number of shares or a different class by reason of any reclassification, stock split (including a reverse stock split), recapitalization, split-up, combination, exchange of shares, readjustment or other similar transaction, or a stock dividend or stock distribution thereon shall be declared with a record date within said period, the applicable Merger Consideration and the Exchange Ratio and any other similarly dependent items, as the case may be, shall be equitably adjusted to provide the holders of Sierra Common Stock the same economic effect as contemplated by this Agreement prior to such event. 3.3 Fractional Shares . All fractional shares that a single record holder of Sierra Common Stock would otherwise be entitled to receive shall be aggregated. No fractional shares of Alpha Common Stock shall be issued in the Merger, but in lieu thereof each holder of Sierra Common Stock otherwise entitled to a fractional share of Alpha Common Stock will be entitled to receive, from the Exchange Agent in accordance with the provisions of this Section 3.3 , a cash payment (rounded to the nearest cent) in lieu of such fractional share of Alpha Common Stock determined by multiplying (a) the volume-weighted average closing sales prices of the Alpha Common Stock on the Nasdaq as reported by The Wall Street Journal for the consecutive five (5) full trading days ending on the day preceding the Closing Date and (b) the fraction of a share (after aggregating all shares of Sierra Common Stock held by such holder immediately prior to the Effective Time and rounded to the nearest one-thousandth when expressed in decimal form) of Alpha Common Stock which such holder would otherwise be entitled to receive pursuant to Section 3.1(b) (the “ Fractional Shares Cash Amount ”). Sierra and Alpha acknowledge that 13 payment of the cash consideration in lieu of issuing fractional shares of Alpha Common Stock was not separately bargained-for consideration but merely represents a mechanical rounding off for purposes of avoiding the expense and inconvenience to Alpha that would otherwise be caused by the issuance of fractional shares of Alpha Common Stock. 3.4 Exchange of Certificates and Book-Entry Shares . (a) Prior to the Effective Time, Alpha shall appoint a nationally recognized financial institution or trust company to act as exchange agent (the “ Exchange Agent ”) for the payment and delivery of the Merger Consideration, pursuant to an agreement in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Sierra and Alpha and in accordance with this Article 3 . At or prior to the Effective Time, Alpha shall deposit, in trust for the benefit of the holders of Sierra Common Stock, with the Exchange Agent for exchange in accordance with this Article 3 , (i) a number of shares of Alpha Common Stock equal to the total shares of Alpha Common Stock issuable pursuant to Section 3.1 and (ii) an amount in cash equal to the Fractional Shares Cash Amount payable in accordance with Section 3.3 (such number of shares of Alpha Common Stock, together with the aggregate Fractional Shares Cash Amount, the “ Exchange Fund ”). The Exchange Agent shall, pursuant to irrevocable instructions, deliver the whole shares of Alpha Common Stock contemplated to be issued pursuant to Section 3.1 and the Fractional Shares Cash Amount out of the Exchange Fund. The Exchange Fund shall not be used for any other purpose. (b) Exchange Procedures . (i) Certificates . As promptly as practicable after the Effective Time (but in no event later than three (3) Business Days after the Effective Time), Alpha shall cause the Exchange Agent to mail to each holder of record of a Sierra Certificate: (A) a letter of transmittal, which shall specify that delivery shall be effected, and risk of loss and title to the Sierra Certificates shall pass, only upon delivery of the Sierra Certificates to the Exchange Agent, and shall otherwise be in such form and have such other provisions as Alpha, Sierra and the Exchange Agent may reasonably agree prior to the Effective Time and (B) instructions for effecting the surrender of the Sierra Certificates (or affidavits of loss in lieu of the Sierra Certificates as provided in Section 3.4(e) ) in exchange for the applicable Merger Consideration and the method of payment of such holder’s Fractional Shares Cash Amount. Upon surrender of a Sierra Certificate (or affidavit of loss in lieu of the Sierra Certificate as provided in Section 3.4(e) ) to the Exchange Agent or to such other agent or agents as may be appointed by Alpha and Sierra, and upon delivery of a letter of transmittal, duly executed and in proper form, with all required enclosures and attachments, with respect to such Sierra Certificates, the holder of such Sierra Certificates shall be entitled to receive in exchange therefor the whole shares of Alpha Common Stock that such holder is entitled to receive pursuant to Section 3.1 and, if applicable, the Fractional Shares Cash Amount for each share of Sierra Common Stock formerly represented by such Sierra Certificates, and any Sierra Certificate so surrendered shall forthwith be cancelled. If payment of the applicable Merger Consideration is to be made to a Person other than the Person in whose name any surrendered Sierra Certificate is registered, it shall be a condition precedent of payment that the Sierra Certificate so surrendered shall be properly endorsed or shall be otherwise in proper form for transfer, and the Person requesting such payment shall have paid any transfer and other similar Taxes required by reason of the delivery of the applicable Merger Consideration to a Person other than the registered holder of the Sierra Certificate so surrendered and shall have established to the 14 satisfaction of Alpha or the Exchange Agent, as applicable, that such Taxes either have been paid or are not required to be paid. No interest will be paid or accrued on any amount payable upon due surrender of Sierra Certificates. Until surrendered as contemplated hereby, each Sierra Certificate shall be deemed at any time after the Effective Time to represent only the right to receive the applicable Merger Consideration provided in this Agreement. (ii) Book-Entry Shares . Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in this Agreement, no holder of Sierra Book-Entry Shares shall be required to deliver a Sierra Certificate or, in the case of holders of Sierra Book-Entry Shares held through The Depository Trust Company, an executed letter of transmittal to the Exchange Agent, to receive the Merger Consideration that such holder is entitled to receive pursuant to Section 3.1 . In lieu thereof, each holder of record of one or more Sierra Book-Entry Shares held through The Depository Trust Company whose shares of Sierra Common Stock were converted into the right to receive the applicable Merger Consideration shall automatically upon the Effective Time be entitled to receive, and Alpha shall cause the Exchange Agent to pay and deliver to The Depository Trust Company or its nominee as promptly as practicable after the Effective Time, the whole shares of Alpha Common Stock that such holder is entitled to receive pursuant to Section 3.1 and, if applicable, the Fractional Shares Cash Amount for each share of Sierra Common Stock formerly represented by such Sierra Book-Entry Shares, and such Sierra Book-Entry Shares of such holder shall be cancelled. As promptly as practicable after the Effective Time (but in no event later than three (3) Business Days after the Effective Time), Alpha shall cause the Exchange Agent to mail to each holder of record of Sierra Book-Entry Shares not held through The Depository Trust Company: (A) a letter of transmittal, which shall be in such form and have such provisions as Alpha, Sierra and the Exchange Agent shall reasonably agree prior to the Effective Time; and (B) instructions for returning such letter of transmittal in exchange for the applicable Merger Consideration and the method of payment of such holder’s Fractional Shares Cash Amount. Upon delivery of such letter of transmittal, in accordance with the terms of such letter of transmittal, duly executed and in proper form with all required enclosures and attachments, the holder of such Sierra Book-Entry Shares shall be entitled to receive in exchange therefor the whole shares of Alpha Common Stock that such holder is entitled to receive pursuant to Section 3.1 and, if applicable, the Fractional Shares Cash Amount for each share of Sierra Common Stock formerly represented by such Sierra Book-Entry Shares, and such Sierra Book-Entry Shares so surrendered shall at the Effective Time be cancelled. Payment of the applicable Merger Consideration with respect to Sierra Book-Entry Shares shall only be made to the Person in whose name such Sierra Book-Entry Shares are registered. No interest will be paid or accrued on any amount payable upon due surrender of Sierra Book-Entry Shares.
