On October 27, 2026, Artiva Biotherapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) filed a Current Report on Form 8-K (the “Original 8-K”) with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) disclosing that the Company’s board of directors approved the discontinuation of the Company’s development of AlloNK ® (AB-101), its non-genetically modified, allogeneic natural killer (NK) cell therapy candidate, as the Company conducts a process to explore strategic alternatives to maximize stockholder value, which includes a plan to implement a reduction of the Company’s workforce by approximately 94% of the Company’s current employee base (the “Wind Down”). At the time of filing the Original 8-K, the Company was unable to make a good faith estimate or range of estimates of the costs and charges it would incur in connection with the Wind Down. This Amendment to Current Report on Form 8-K/A amends the Original 8-K to include estimates for the major types of costs associated with the Wind Down. Except as described below, all other information in the Original 8-K remains unchanged and this Form 8-K/A should be read in conjunction with the Original 8-K. The Company now estimates it will incur the following charges in connection with the Wind Down: • $10 million to $15 million in contract termination and other costs related to the discontinuation and orderly close-out of its AlloNK clinical trials; and • $17 million to $20 million in costs related to the workforce reduction, including severance, benefits, payroll taxes and other termination costs. The Company expects to complete the workforce reduction in the first quarter of 2027 and to incur a significant majority of the costs related to the Wind Down in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company may also incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or associated with, the Wind Down. The estimated costs of the Wind Down as well as the estimated timing of the related costs are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ.
