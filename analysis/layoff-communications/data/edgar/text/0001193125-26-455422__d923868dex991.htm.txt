Continued customer momentum Sunrise recorded net additions of +19,000 mobile postpaid 1 RGUs (revenue-generating units) in Q3 2026. Continued momentum in Flanker Brands and the business-customer segment was a key driver of this ongoing positive performance. The broadband Internet 2 customer base remained stable supported by lower churn compared to the prior-year quarter. Seasonally lower market activity in Q3 2026 and the reaction to the price increases were in line with expectations. The proportion of broadband customers also using a Sunrise mobile offering also continued to rise in Q3 2026. This fixed-mobile convergence rate (FMC 9 ) increased by +2.5 percentage points to 61.5% as of 30 September 2026. As of 30 September 2026, Sunrise had approximately 3.19 million mobile, 1.29 million broadband Internet 2 and 0.96 million enhanced TV RGUs. Customer loyalty, product innovation and service quality drive positive trend Sunrise Rewards continued to perform positively in Q3 2026, supporting customer loyalty for the main brand. The launch of the iPhone 18 Pro and the iPhone 18 Pro Max also further boosted sales of «Device as a Service» offerings, increasing uptake of the Smart Upgrade and Flex Upgrade options. The proportion of iPhones sold with Device as a Service offers consequently rose from 69% in the prior-year period to over 75% this year. The newly positioned premium product Iconic Bundle also proved popular, accounting for a double-digit % of iPhone sales. Sunrise also achieved strong results with its main brand in the connect industry benchmarks for customer service, further supporting its position in the premium segment. In the business-customer segment, Sunrise began operational implementation of its partnership with PHOENIQS and launched the Sunrise Business AI Assistant , an exclusive, fully sovereign Swiss AI product for companies. Together with the Sunrise Business AI Assistant, and as the ideal basis for it, the new bundle offer SME SecureConnect was launched; it combines Internet, cloud telephony and integrated cybersecurity within a new plug & work solution. With five , Sunrise also prepared the launch of an AI-native secondary brand at the upper end of the «Smart Shopper» segment (B segment) in Q3 2026. five offers a hyper-personalised digital customer experience. Customers create their own mobile subscription through an intuitive AI chat dialogue, with the price adjusting each month to their individual usage without exceeding the flat rate of CHF 25/month for communication needs within Switzerland. The powerful, fully sovereign Swiss AI assistant (provided by PHOENIQS) is always included. 2/7 Transformation of the operating model initiated Sunrise has begun the comprehensive realignment of its operating model announced in September. Through this multi-year transformation, Sunrise aims to make the company more focused, more efficient and more technology-driven. As part of the transformation programme, internal and external cost structures are being reviewed comprehensively, processes and responsibilities simplified and resources and investments targeted more closely towards customer experience and loyalty, the high-performance network and the reliability and quality of operational delivery. This is also intended to create greater scope for targeted reinvestment and strengthen the foundations for future growth. The reorganisations in Q1 and Q4 2026 make an initial contribution towards the substantial cost savings targeted over the medium term. Sunrise expects these to deliver savings in operating and capital expenditure of around CHF 70 million on an annualised basis (run-rate savings) before social-plan costs. The savings will be used in part to offset existing headwinds and cost increases, and to enable targeted reinvestments. The full effect of the reorganisations on Adjusted Free Cash Flow is expected from 2028. Q3 2026 financial results in line with expectations Revenue decreased moderately by -1.2% YoY to CHF 732.0 million in Q3 2026. The revenue decline was therefore smaller than in the preceding quarter. The price increase implemented in August, higher device sales and growth in business and wholesale customers partially offset revenue pressure in the fixed-line business. Continued improvements in operating costs and lease expenses supported operating performance. Adjusted EBITDAaL 3 decreased slightly by -2.0% YoY to CHF 264.8 million in Q3 2026. P&E Additions (CAPEX 4 ) were reduced by -17.0% YoY to CHF 88.1 million in Q3 2026, representing 12.0% of revenue. Adjusted EBITDAaL 3 less P&E Additions (CAPEX 4 ) consequently increased by +7.7% YoY to CHF 176.7 million. Adjusted Free Cash Flow (FCF 3,5 ) amounted to CHF 32.5 million in Q3 2026 and was therefore broadly in line with the prior-year quarter. Earlier interest payments in 2025 and temporary working-capital outflows outweighed lower investment and tax expenses. In the first nine months, adjusted FCF 3,5 increased by +71.3% YoY to CHF 125.9 million. Sunrise achieved a net profit of CHF 0.8 million in Q3 2026, compared with a net loss of CHF 4.4 million in the prior-year quarter. Net cash provided by operating activities amounted to CHF 234.5 million (-19.7% YoY), while capital expenditure as reported in the cash flow statement 6 decreased by -59.1% YoY to CHF 38.9 million. Sunrise believes that Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDAaL are meaningful measures because they provide a transparent view of Sunrise’s recurring operating performance, which is unaffected by its capital structure, and allow management to (a) readily identify operating trends, (b) perform analytical comparisons and benchmarking between segments, and (c) identify strategies to improve operating performance. Sunrise also believes that Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDAaL are useful to investors because they provide a basis for comparing Sunrise’s performance with that of other companies in the same or similar industries.
