Who We Are American Barrick is a premier pure-play gold producer and the largest gold producer with a portfolio of assets located entirely in North America. We operate six large, high-quality assets in two jurisdictions, Nevada and the Dominican Republic, including every Tier One gold asset that exists in the United States and four of the world’s 16 largest gold assets by 2025 production. In 2025, we produced 2.0 million ounces (“Moz”) of gold on an attributable basis. Our portfolio is underpinned by 40 Moz of attributable mineral reserves, representing a 20-year reserve life, and 38 Moz of attributable mineral resources. Our portfolio is anchored by our 61.5% joint venture interest in Nevada Gold Mines in the United States, the largest gold mining complex in the world and the centerpiece of the premier gold mining jurisdiction globally. Within Nevada Gold Mines, we are advancing the Fourmile project, which we believe is one of the most significant gold discoveries of the last 25 years. Fourmile represents a compelling source of future growth, integrated into the Cortez Complex and supported by Nevada Gold Mines’ existing infrastructure. Assuming Fourmile timely reaches its expected annual production run-rate, by 2034 we would own and operate five of the seven North American mines producing over 500 thousand ounces (“Koz”) per year. Nevada Gold Mines is located in Nevada and accounted for 81% of our attributable gold production in 2025. Nevada is the world’s premier gold mining jurisdiction, ranked the #1 mining jurisdiction globally for investment attractiveness on the Fraser Institute’s 2025 survey and a top-three jurisdiction for nine consecutive years. The State of Nevada has contributed 72% of overall U.S. gold production since 2020 and is also the #2 gold mineral resource endowment on Earth, making it the most important and prospective geological land package in the Western Hemisphere. Gold plays a fundamental role globally as an investment asset and a store of wealth. For today’s investors, exposure to gold delivers compelling benefits: long-term capital appreciation, portfolio diversification, and inflation protection. Global market interest in gold is surging. The gold price averaged $4,545 per ounce over the nine months ended September 30, 2026, reflecting sustained central bank buying, geopolitical uncertainty, and record global demand, with annual consumption now exceeding 160 million troy ounces, valued at over $700 billion (based on a $4,545 per ounce gold price).
