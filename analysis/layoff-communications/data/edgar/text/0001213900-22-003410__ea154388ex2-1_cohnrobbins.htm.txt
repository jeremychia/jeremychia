and (d) following the actions undertaken in clause (c) , the Exchange Agent will transfer the Merger Consideration received in clause (c)(i) to the Acquiror Shareholders, and the Swiss NewCo Warrants to holders of Acquiror Warrants pursuant to the Warrant Conversion.
