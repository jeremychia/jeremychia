WHEREAS , upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement and in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law (the “ DGCL ”) and the Florida Business Corporation Act (the “ FBCA ”), the SPAC and the Company will enter into a business combination transaction pursuant to which, on the Closing Date, Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company (the “ Merger ”), with the Company surviving the Merger as a wholly owned Subsidiary of the SPAC (the Company, in its capacity as the surviving corporation of the Merger, is sometimes referred to herein as the “ Surviving Subsidiary Corporation ”); WHEREAS , for U.S. federal income tax purposes, (a) it is intended that the Merger qualify as a “reorganization” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code to which the SPAC, Merger Sub and the Company are parties within the meaning of Section 368(b) of the Code;
