Option Shares . On the basis of the representations and warranties herein contained, but subject to the terms and conditions herein set forth, the Company hereby agrees to issue to the Representative (and/or each of its designees) on the applicable Closing Date (as defined herein) and Option Closing Date (as defined herein, and if applicable), warrants, substantially in the form of Exhibit A attached hereto, to purchase such number of Ordinary Shares equal to five percent (5.0%) of the Offered Securities sold by the Company (the “ Representative’s Warrants ”), including any Ordinary Shares issued pursuant to the exercise of Over-Allotment Option. The Representative’s Warrants shall be exercisable at any time, and from time to time, in whole or in part, commencing six months from the First Closing Date and expiring on the four-and-a-half-year anniversary of the First Closing Date at an initial exercise price of $8.40 per Ordinary Share, which is equal to one hundred and twenty percent (120%) of the public offering price. The Representative’s Warrants may be exercised on a cashless basis so long as they are held by the initial purchasers and will terminate on the fifth anniversary of the First Closing Date. The Representative’s Warrants are not redeemable by the Representative.
