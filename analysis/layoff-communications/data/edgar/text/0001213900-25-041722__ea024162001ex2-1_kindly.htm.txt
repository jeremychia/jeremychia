Subject to Section 2.2 , each share of Merger Partner Class A Common Stock (other than shares to be cancelled in accordance with Section 2.1(b) and any Dissenting Shares) issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time shall be automatically converted into the right to receive from Public Company the number of validly issued, fully paid and nonassessable shares of Public Company Common Stock equal to the Class A Exchange Ratio.
