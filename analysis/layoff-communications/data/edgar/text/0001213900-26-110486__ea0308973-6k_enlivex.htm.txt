Form 20-F ☒ Form 40-F ☐ On October 19, 2026, Enlivex Ltd., a company organized under the laws of the State of Israel (the “ Company ”), announced a cost reduction plan, effective immediately, designed to substantially reduce the Company’s cash burn and focus its resources on the execution of its Phase 2b clinical trial of Allocetra™ for the treatment of patients with moderate-to-severe age-related symptomatic primary knee osteoarthritis (OA). The Company expects to report topline six-month data from the trial by mid-2027. The Company’s plan includes the following measures: ● Wind-down of non-clinical R&D: The Company is winding down, effective immediately, all research and development activities not related to the Phase 2b clinical trial. ● Focus on clinical execution: The Company’s resources will be dedicated to the execution of the Phase 2b trial and related activities. ● Workforce reduction: The Company is reducing its workforce by approximately 60%. ● Management compensation reduction: Salaries for the Company’s management team will be reduced by 20%, and the Chief Executive Officer’s will be reduced by 40%.
