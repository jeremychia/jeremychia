EX-99.1 2 ea030897301ex99-1.htm PRESS RELEASE Exhibit 99.1 Enlivex Announces Cost Reduction Plan to Focus Resources on Phase 2b Trial of Allocetra™ in Age-Related Primary Moderate-to-Severe Knee Osteoarthritis ● Non-clinical R&D activities to be wound down; resources to be focused on executing the Phase 2b trial ● Workforce reduced by approximately 60%; management and CEO salaries reduced ● Plan designed to substantially reduce cash burn ● Topline Phase 2b six-month endpoint data expected by mid-2027 Nes-Ziona, Israel, Oct. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- “Enlivex” or the “Company”), a quality longevity company powered by a prediction markets treasury, today announced a cost reduction plan, effective immediately, designed to substantially reduce the Company’s cash burn and focus its resources on the execution of its Phase 2b clinical trial of Allocetra™ for the treatment of patients with moderate-to-severe age-related symptomatic primary knee osteoarthritis (OA). The Company expects to report topline six-month data from the trial by mid-2027. The Company’s plan includes the following measures: ● Wind-down of non-clinical R&D: The Company is winding down, effective immediately, all research and development activities not related to the Phase 2b clinical trial. ● Focus on clinical execution: The Company’s resources will be dedicated to the execution of the Phase 2b trial and related activities. ● Workforce reduction: The Company is reducing its workforce by approximately 60%. ● Management compensation reduction: Salaries for the Company’s management team will be reduced by 20%, and the Chief Executive Officer’s will be reduced by 40%. Dr. Oren Hershkovitz, Chief Executive Officer of Enlivex, stated ” Our priorities are clear. The positive results from our Phase 1/2a trial of Allocetra™ and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration’s (FDA) Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation give us strong conviction in Allocetra’s™ potential in treating knee osteoarthritis. We believe that the most important milestone ahead of us is the six-month topline data from our Phase 2b trial. By winding down non-clinical R&D, reducing our workforce, and having management, including myself, take substantial salary reductions, we are focusing our resources to reach that readout as efficiently as possible, which we expect by mid-2027.” Phase 2b Trial, Phase 1/2a Results and RMAT Designation The global, multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2b trial is evaluating intra-articular injections of Allocetra™ in patients aged 64 and older with moderate-to-severe age-related symptomatic primary knee OA, the population for which the FDA granted RMAT designation in July 2026. The trial builds on the completed Phase 1/2a trial (NCT06233474), in which Allocetra™ demonstrated in the age-related Primary OA responder population, statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvements in knee pain and function compared with placebo at three and six months, with a favorable safety profile. About Enlivex (Nasdaq: ENLV) Enlivex is a quality longevity company powered by a prediction markets treasury. The Company is advancing Allocetra™, an advanced clinical-stage immunotherapy targeting inflammatory conditions associated with aging, with a primary focus on age-related osteoarthritis. In addition to its clinical programs, Enlivex operates a prediction markets treasury strategy built around the Rain protocol, the leading decentralized prediction markets infrastructure on Arbitrum. This dual strategy combines the development of quality longevity therapeutics with exposure to the emerging prediction markets ecosystem. About Knee Osteoarthritis Osteoarthritis (OA) is one of the most prevalent and disabling diseases worldwide, affecting more than 32 million Americans today and projected to impact 78 million Americans by 2040. There are currently no approved disease-modifying therapies; treatment is largely limited to pain relief, intra-articular steroids, or surgery, and the burden increases sharply with age. By age 60, Knee OA affects roughly 30% of the population, and about half of knee OA patients are 60 years and older. This demographic is expanding with global aging trends, underscoring the need for new, durable therapies.
