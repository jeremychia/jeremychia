AS OF JUNE 30, 2026 AND FOR THE SIX MONTHS ENDED JUNE 30, 2026 AND THE YEAR ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2025 Exhibit 99.7 UNAUDITED PRO FORMA FINANCIAL INFORMATION On August 13, 2026 (the “Closing Date”), Proficient Auto Logistics, Inc. (“PAL” or the “Company”), through its wholly owned subsidiaries Proficient Services, Inc. (“US Buyer”) and H&A CN Acquisition, Ltd. (“Canada Buyer” and, together with US Buyer, the “Buyers”), completed the acquisition (the “Acquisition”) of all of the issued and outstanding equity interests of Hansen & Adkins Auto Transport, LLC (“H&A Auto Transport”), Hansen & Adkins Auto Logistics, LLC (“H&A Auto Logistics”), Royal Truck Leasing, LLC (“Royal”) and Hansen & Adkins Canada Ltd. (“H&A Canada”) pursuant to the Equity Purchase Agreement, dated as of August 10, 2026 (the “Purchase Agreement”), by and among the Buyers, Steven Hansen, Louie Adkins, Hansen & Adkins, Inc., Royal Holdco, LLC, The Steven Hansen Separate Property Trust and The Louie Adkins 2024 Trust (collectively, the “Sellers”), and Steven Hansen, in his capacity as seller representative (the “Seller Representative”). H&A Auto Transport, H&A Auto Logistics and Royal are referred to collectively as “H&A.” Upon completion of the Acquisition, MCL McGill Carriers Ltd. (“MCL”) became a subsidiary of H&A Canada. For purposes of the historical financial information and the unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information, MCL’s historical financial information is presented under the caption “H&A Canada.” H&A and H&A Canada are referred to collectively as the “acquired businesses.” The acquired businesses provide new and used vehicle transportation and logistics services in the United States and Canada. The Acquisition expanded PAL’s scale, service capabilities and geographic presence, including its entry into Canada. The Purchase Agreement provided for a base purchase price of $130.0 million, subject to customary adjustments for closing indebtedness, cash, transaction expenses and net working capital. For purposes of the preliminary purchase price allocation accounting, preliminary purchase consideration was approximately $62.8 million, consisting of approximately $60.5 million of cash consideration and approximately $2.2 million representing the acquisition-date fair value of 421,354 shares of PAL common stock issued as consideration. Contemporaneous with the closing of the Acquisition, PAL also repaid outstanding indebtedness and paid certain seller transaction expenses. The Sellers are eligible to receive up to $22.1 million of additional contingent consideration (the “Earnout”) based on the acquired businesses’ 2026 earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“EBITDA”), and the purchase price remains subject to customary post-closing adjustments. The preliminary purchase price allocation includes fair value adjustments to acquired property and equipment, approximately $18.2 million of identifiable intangible assets, principally customer relationships and trade names, and goodwill attributable in part to the assembled workforce and expected operating synergies. The allocation, including the estimated fair values and useful lives of the acquired assets, remains preliminary and may be revised as the valuation process is completed. See Notes 1 and 3 for additional information regarding the Acquisition, consideration transferred and preliminary purchase price allocation. PAL financed the Acquisition with cash on hand. PAL financed the debt repayment with the net proceeds from the issuance of $75.0 million aggregate principal amount of 5.500% Convertible Notes due 2033 (the “Convertible Notes”) and $82.0 million of borrowings under the M&T equipment financing agreement (the “M&T Credit Agreement”). In connection with the issuance of the Convertible Notes, PAL also entered into capped call transactions (the “Capped Calls” and, together with the issuance of the Convertible Notes and the M&T Credit Agreement borrowings, the “Financing Transactions”). The premium in respect of the Capped Calls was paid with the net proceeds from the issuance of the Convertible Notes. The unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information reflects the Financing Transactions, the repayment of certain historical indebtedness and the related adjustments to interest expense and financing costs. See Notes 1, 4 and 5 for additional information regarding the Financing Transactions and related pro forma adjustments. The following unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information has been prepared in accordance with Article 11 of Regulation S-X. The unaudited pro forma condensed combined balance sheet as of June 30, 2026 gives effect to the Acquisition and related Financing Transactions as if they had occurred on June 30, 2026. The unaudited pro forma condensed combined statements of operations for the year ended December 31, 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026 give effect to the Acquisition and related Financing Transactions as if they had occurred on January 1, 2025. The transaction accounting adjustments reflect the application of the acquisition method of accounting under Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) Topic 805, Business Combinations , with PAL identified as the accounting acquirer. The unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information was derived from, and should be read together with, PAL’s audited consolidated financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2025 and unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements as of and for the six months ended June 30, 2026, included in PAL’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025 and Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, respectively. It should also be read together with the historical financial statements of H&A and H&A Canada included as Exhibits 99.3 through 99.6 to this Current Report on Form 8-K/A. The historical financial statements of H&A and H&A Canada were prepared in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“U.S. GAAP”) and are presented in U.S. dollars. The unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information is presented for informational purposes only and is not necessarily indicative of the financial position or results of operations that would have been achieved had the Acquisition and related Financing Transactions occurred on the dates indicated. It also is not indicative of the future financial position or results of operations of the combined company. The pro forma adjustments are based on currently available information and assumptions that management believes are reasonable. The preliminary purchase price allocation was prepared solely for purposes of this unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information and may differ materially from the final allocation upon completion of the valuation of the assets acquired and liabilities assumed. (“PAL” or the “Company”), through its wholly owned subsidiaries Proficient Services, Inc. (“US Buyer”) and H&A CN Acquisition, Ltd. (“Canada Buyer” and, together with US Buyer, the “Buyers”), completed the acquisition (the “Acquisition”) of all of the issued and outstanding equity interests of Hansen & Adkins Auto Transport, LLC (“H&A Auto Transport”), Hansen & Adkins Auto Logistics, LLC (“H&A Auto Logistics”), Royal Truck Leasing, LLC (“Royal”) and Hansen & Adkins Canada Ltd. (“H&A Canada”) pursuant to the Equity Purchase Agreement, dated as of August 10, 2026 (the “Purchase Agreement”), by and among the Buyers, Steven Hansen, Louie Adkins, Hansen & Adkins, Inc., Royal Holdco, LLC, The Steven Hansen Separate Property Trust and The Louie Adkins 2024 Trust (collectively, the “Sellers”), and Steven Hansen, in his capacity as seller representative (the “Seller Representative”). H&A Auto Transport, H&A Auto Logistics and Royal are referred to collectively as “H&A.” Upon completion of the Acquisition, MCL McGill Carriers Ltd. (“MCL”) became a subsidiary of H&A Canada. For purposes of the unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information, MCL’s historical financial information is presented under the caption “H&A Canada.” H&A and H&A Canada are referred to collectively as the “acquired businesses.” US Buyer acquired the H&A interests—H&A Auto Transport and H&A Auto Logistics having converted from California corporations to California limited liability companies before the Acquisition and Canada Buyer acquired the shares of H&A Canada. For U.S. federal income tax purposes, the purchase of the H&A interests is treated as a purchase of assets, with the acquired tax bases stepped up to allocated purchase price, while the purchase of H&A Canada is treated as a purchase of shares, with MCL’s historical tax bases carrying over. The $130.0 million base purchase price under the Purchase Agreement was adjusted for a net working capital adjustment of $0.1 million, increased by estimated closing cash of $6.1 million and reduced by estimated closing indebtedness of $72.8 million and estimated transaction expenses of $1.9 million, resulting in an estimated equity purchase price of $62.8 million. For purchase-accounting purposes, preliminary consideration transferred was $62.8 million, consisting of $60.5 million of cash consideration and $2.2 million representing the fair value of 421,354 shares of PAL common stock measured at the acquisition date (which is the Closing Date). Because the Acquisition closed earlier on the Closing Date, the closing price of PAL common stock the day prior to the Closing Date was used. The difference between the estimated equity purchase price and preliminary consideration transferred primarily relates to the transaction expenses and the Seller-directed payoff paid by PAL on behalf of the Sellers, which reduced the estimated equity purchase price but are included in consideration transferred, and to the acquisition-date fair value measurement of the common stock issued as consideration. The components of consideration transferred and the preliminary purchase price allocation are presented in Note 3. The estimated closing purchase price is subject to a customary post-closing true-up under the Purchase Agreement, based on a final closing statement (the “Final Closing Statement”) to be delivered within 90 days after the Closing Date, with any amount owed to the Buyers recoverable first from the $2.0 million adjustment escrow. In addition, the Sellers are entitled to additional contingent consideration (the “Earnout”) of up to $22.1 million if the acquired businesses’ 2026 earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“EBITDA”) exceeds specified thresholds, of which up to $2.0 million is payable in shares of PAL common stock and the remainder in cash. No amount has been assigned to the Earnout in preliminary consideration transferred. Contemporaneous with the closing of the Acquisition, PAL completed the issuance of $75.0 million aggregate principal amount of 5.500% Convertible Notes due 2033 (the “Convertible Notes”) and $82.0 million of borrowings under the M&T equipment financing agreement (the “M&T Credit Agreement”). In connection with the issuance of the Convertible Notes, PAL also entered into capped call transactions (the “Capped Calls” and, together with the issuance of the Convertible Notes and the M&T Credit Agreement borrowings, the “Financing Transactions”). The Convertible Notes bear interest at 5.500% per annum, payable semi-annually, and are convertible at an initial conversion rate of 153.7870 shares of PAL common stock per $1,000 principal amount (an initial conversion price of approximately $6.50 per share), with PAL electing to settle conversions in cash, shares or a combination. Concurrently with the issuance of the Convertible Notes, PAL paid $9.2 million for the Capped Calls intended to reduce potential dilution upon conversion of the Convertible Notes. The pro forma effects of the Financing Transactions on interest expense are described in Note 5. The Acquisition has been accounted for as a business combination under the acquisition method of accounting in Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) Topic 805, Business Combinations, with PAL as the accounting acquirer. Autonomous Entity Adjustments under Rule 11-02(a)(6)(hh) are not applicable because the Acquisition does not affect PAL’s status as an autonomous entity. Management’s Adjustments under Rule 11-02(a)(7) have not been presented; accordingly, the unaudited pro forma condensed combined financial information reflects only the Transaction Accounting Adjustments necessary to present the pro forma effect of the Acquisition. The Acquisition was consummated on the Closing Date. The pro forma condensed combined balance sheet is presented as of June 30, 2026 and gives effect to the Acquisition and the Financing Transactions as if they had been consummated on that date. The pro forma condensed combined statements of operations for the year ended December 31, 2025 and the six months ended June 30, 2026 give effect to the Acquisition and the Financing Transactions as if they had been consummated on January 1, 2025. Transaction Accounting Adjustments that are nonrecurring are accordingly reflected only in the year ended December 31, 2025 and are not repeated in the interim period presented; see Note 6. The preliminary allocation of consideration transferred in Note 3 is based on the consideration transferred and the estimated fair values of the assets acquired and liabilities assumed, based on balances as of June 30, 2026 that have not yet been updated to the Closing Date. The pro forma condensed combined balance sheet uses H&A’s and MCL’s historical balance sheets as of June 30, 2026 as the starting point. Consequently, goodwill in the pro forma condensed combined balance sheet equals the amount in the preliminary purchase price allocation in Note 3. Goodwill as finally determined is expected to differ by the change in H&A’s and MCL’s net assets between June 30, 2026 and the Closing Date, comprising their results of operations for that period, distributions to the Sellers (other than the pre-closing distribution of excluded assets reflected in adjustment (e)) and the difference between the acquired businesses’ indebtedness at June 30, 2026 and at the Closing Date. PAL will update the allocation in Note 3 for Closing Date balances, and the goodwill that PAL recognizes in its consolidated financial statements may differ materially from the amount presented in Note 3. The historical balance sheets of H&A as of June 30, 2026 include certain assets that were distributed to the Sellers or otherwise excluded from the Acquisition before the Closing Date. The Transaction Accounting Adjustments eliminate those assets from the pro forma condensed combined balance sheet, with the corresponding effect reflected in accumulated deficit as a pre-closing distribution to the Sellers, with no effect on goodwill, because the assets were not included in the net assets acquired.
