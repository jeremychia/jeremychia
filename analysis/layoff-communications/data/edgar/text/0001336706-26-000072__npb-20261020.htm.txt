Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition. On October 20, 2026, Northpointe Bancshares, Inc. (the “Company”), the holding company for Northpointe Bank (“the Bank”) issued a press release announcing its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. A copy of the press release is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On October 18, 2026, the Boards of Directors of the Company and the Bank approved a plan to exit the Bank's distributed retail and consumer direct mortgage origination channels (the "Exit Plan"). As part of the Exit Plan, the Company and CMG Financial and its affiliates ("CMG") have entered into a non-binding letter of intent regarding the potential sale of those channels to CMG (the "Proposed Transaction"). The Exit Plan reflects the Company’s decision, given the current economic and operating environment and the continued evolution of mortgage distribution channels, to refocus its strategic priorities on the businesses in which it has the strongest competitive advantages, including its Mortgage Purchase Program, Specialty Servicing, and Digital Banking businesses. The Company expects to continue to close loans currently in process and to provide support to affected team members. Subject to the satisfactory completion of due diligence, the execution of a definitive agreement, and the satisfaction of customary closing conditions, the Company currently anticipates that the Proposed Transaction will close by the end of 2026. The Company currently estimates restructuring charges of between $6 million and $7 million, comprised primarily of cash expenditures related to severance and employee transition related expenses, along with an impairment charge of approximately $800,000 on the remaining intangible asset associated with a previous mortgage team acquisition. The Company expects to realize the majority of these charges during the fourth quarter of 2026. This estimate of restructuring costs is based on a number of assumptions. Actual results may differ materially, and additional charges not currently expected may be incurred in connection with, or as a result of, the workforce reduction. As permitted by Item 2.05 of Form 8-K, the Comply will file an amendment to this report if charges and future cash payments differ materially from current estimates.
