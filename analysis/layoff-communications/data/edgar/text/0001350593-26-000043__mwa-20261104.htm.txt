Item 2.05. Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On November 4, 2026, the Company announced that it has entered into agreements with ZENNER USA, part of the Minol-ZENNER Group (“ZENNER”), to transition the Company’s metering business to ZENNER. The decision to pursue the transition of the metering business follows a comprehensive review by the Company’s Board of Directors and management of the strategic fit and performance of the metering business on the Company’s portfolio. Under its agreements with ZENNER, the Company’s metering software customers are expected to transition to ZENNER, and the Company will retain its existing hardware contracts and warranty obligations, which it expects to satisfy using its own inventory or products purchased from ZENNER. The Company expects the transition of its metering business to be substantially completed in fiscal 2027, though some activity and associated costs could extend beyond that year. In connection with the transition of the Company’s metering business, the Company expects to record total one-time pre-tax charges of approximately $26 million, substantially all of which are expected to be recognized in fiscal 2027, with the majority expected to be recognized in the first quarter of fiscal 2027. At this time, the Company is unable in good faith to make a determination of an estimate or range of estimates required by paragraphs (b) and (d) of Item 2.05 of Form 8-K, and the Company will file an amendment to this Current Report on Form 8-K after it makes a determination of such estimate or range of estimates.
