At the Effective Time, and subject to and upon the terms and conditions of this Agreement, and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the DGCL, Merger Sub and the Company shall consummate the Merger, pursuant to which (i) Merger Sub shall merge with and into the Company, (ii) the separate corporate existence of Merger Sub shall cease and (iii) the Company shall continue its corporate existence under the DGCL as the surviving corporation.
