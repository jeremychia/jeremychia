Cayman Merger . (a) On the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, and in accordance with Part 16 of the Companies Act (As Revised) of the Cayman Islands (the “ CICA ”), at the Cayman Effective Time, Cayman Merger Sub shall be merged with and into Cayman Target Company.
