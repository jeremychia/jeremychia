On October 6, 2026, the Board of Directors of Mesa Laboratories, Inc. (“Mesa,” the “Company,” “we,” “us” or “our”) approved a restructuring program (the “Restructuring Program”) designed to focus our Clinical Genomics division on pharmacogenomics, oncology and high-utilization customer accounts and to reduce our operating costs. We began notifying affected employees on October 12, 2026. The Restructuring Program includes the elimination of approximately 40 positions, primarily in our Clinical Genomics division, with additional reductions in our corporate functions, and the exit of our Hamburg, Germany facility when its lease expires in February 2027. We expect the Restructuring Program to be substantially complete by the end of fiscal year 2027, except for certain international employee exits that are subject to protected notice periods, which we expect to complete in the first half of fiscal year 2028. We estimate that we will incur total pre-tax charges of approximately $2.6 million to $3.4 million, primarily during the second half of fiscal year 2027. These charges are expected to consist primarily of employee-related costs of approximately $2.3 million to $3.0 million, net of an expected non-cash reversal of approximately $0.3 million of previously recognized stock-based compensation expense, and other exit costs of approximately $0.3 million to $0.4 million. We expect related cash expenditures of approximately $2.9 million to $3.7 million, substantially all of which will occur over the next 12 months. Estimates of the headcount reductions, timing and charges related to certain international operations remain subject to the completion of applicable local employee consultation processes. These amounts are preliminary estimates based on information currently available to management. Actual charges and cash expenditures associated with the Restructuring Program may differ from these estimates.
