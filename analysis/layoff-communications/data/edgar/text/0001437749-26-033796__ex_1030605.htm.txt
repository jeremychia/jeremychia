Third Quarter Financials GAAP net sales, including Superform, were $91.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $95.6 million in the prior-year period, a decrease of 4.1%. The decline primarily reflected lower sales within the Elektron segment, while Gas Cylinders’ sales remained relatively flat year-over-year. Excluding Superform, adjusted net sales decreased 3.9% to $89.3 million, compared to $92.9 million in the prior-year period. GAAP gross profit decreased to $22.4 million, compared to $22.8 million in the prior-year period, while gross margin expanded approximately 60 basis points to 24.4%. Adjusted gross profit, excluding Superform, increased to $21.8 million from $21.7 million, with adjusted gross margin expanding approximately 100 basis points to 24.4% too, despite lower year-over-year sales. Adjusted EBITDA decreased 10.3% to $12.2 million, compared to $13.6 million in the prior-year period, with adjusted EBITDA margin declining approximately 100 basis points to 13.7%. The reduction in profitability primarily reflected lower year-over-year sales within the Elektron defense sector, partially offset by improved performance in Gas Cylinders. GAAP net profit was $0.9 million, or $0.03 per diluted share primarily reflecting transaction expense, compared to net income of $2.7 million, or $0.10 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. Adjusted net income decreased to $7.0 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, compared to $8.1 million, or $0.30 per diluted share, in the prior-year period. Transaction Update As previously announced, on July 27, 2026, Luxfer Holdings PLC entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by affiliates of Wynnchurch Capital, L.P. in an all-cash transaction valued at $17.37 per ordinary share. The transaction remains subject to shareholder approval and the satisfaction of other customary closing conditions. A shareholder vote is scheduled for November 4, 2026, with the transaction expected to close later in November 2026. Due to the pending transaction, Luxfer will not host a third quarter 2026 earnings conference call. About Luxfer Luxfer is a global industrial company innovating niche applications in materials engineering. Using its broad array of proprietary technologies, Luxfer focuses on value creation, customer satisfaction, and demanding applications where technical know-how and manufacturing expertise combine to deliver a superior product. Luxfer’s high-performance materials, components, and high-pressure gas containment devices are used in defense and emergency response, clean energy, healthcare, transportation, and general industrial applications. For more information, please visit www.luxfer.com . Luxfer is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and its ordinary shares trade under the symbol LXFR.
