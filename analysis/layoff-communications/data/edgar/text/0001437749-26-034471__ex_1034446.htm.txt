The Contingent Purchase Price shall be payable as the sum of the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment plus the Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment, determined as follows: (a) First Contingent Purchase Price Payment . For the First Contingent Purchase Price Period, Shareholders can earn a Contingent Purchase Price Payment (the “ First Contingent Purchase Price Payment ”) up to the Maximum Contingent Purchase Price Amount based on the following calculation: (i) If Gross Profit is less than or equal to $4,145,000, the Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be $0. (ii) If Gross Profit is greater than $4,145,000 but not more than $5,221,000, the Contingent Purchase Price Payment will equal (A) $5,000,000, multiplied by (B)(I) Gross Profit, less $4,145,000, divided by (II) $1,076,000. (iii) If Gross Profit is greater than $5,221,000 but not more than $6,021,000, the Contingent Purchase Price Payment will equal (A) $5,000,000, plus (B)(I) $2,000,000, multiplied by (II)(x) Gross Profit, less $5,221,000, divided by (y) $800,000. (iv) If Gross Profit is greater than $6,021,000, the Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be the Maximum Contingent Purchase Price Amount. The foregoing calculation applies to both the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment and the Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment, in each case based on Gross Profit for the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Period. By way of example only, if, in the First Contingent Purchase Price Period, Company generates $4,600,000 in Gross Profit, Buyer shall pay, or cause to be paid, $2,114,312 to the Shareholder Account. (b) Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment . Assuming Shareholders have not already received the Maximum Contingent Purchase Price Amount per the terms of the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment in Section 2(a) of this Exhibit C , for the Second Contingent Purchase Price Period, Shareholders can earn a Contingent Purchase Price Payment (the “ Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment ”) up to an amount equal to the Maximum Contingent Purchase Price Amount calculated using the Section 2(a) calculation schedule based on Gross Profit for the Second Contingent Purchase Price Period, plus (i) any interest payable on any unpaid amount of Contingent Purchase Price minus (ii) the amount of the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment and minus (iii) the Margin Amount, provided that in no event shall the sum of the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment and the Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment exceed the Maximum Contingent Purchase Price Amount. By way of example only, assuming Company generates Gross Profit of $4,400,000 in the First Contingent Purchase Price Period, Company generates Gross Profit of $4,800,000 in the Second Contingent Purchase Price Period, the Margin Amount is equal to $100,000, and the interest payable on any unpaid amount of Contingent Purchase Price is equal to $50,000, the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be equal to $1,184,944, and the Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be equal to $1,808,736 ($3,043,680 less the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment of $1,184,944, less the Margin Amount of $100,000 plus the interest payable on any unpaid amount of Contingent Purchase Price of $50,000). By way of example only, assuming Company generates Gross Profit of $4,400,000 in the First Contingent Purchase Price Period, Company generates Gross Profit of $5,800,000 in the Second Contingent Purchase Price Period and the Margin Amount is equal to $100,000, the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be equal to $1,184,944, and the Second Contingent Purchase Price Payment will be equal to $5,162,556 ($6,447,500 less the First Contingent Purchase Price Payment of $1,184,944 and less the Margin Amount of $100,000). 3. Calculation and Payment of the Contingent Purchase Price Payments . (a) Within ninety (90) days after the end of the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Period, Buyer shall prepare and deliver to Shareholder Representative its good faith calculation of (i) Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold, and Gross Profit, and (ii) the earned portion of the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Payment for such period (a “ Contingent Purchase Price Statement ”). Buyer shall deliver, together with each Contingent Purchase Price Statement, reasonable supporting documentation for the calculations set forth therein, including, without limitation, (A) the Company’s general ledger detail for the applicable period, (B) a line-item build-up of Net Sales and Cost of Goods Sold, (C) a schedule of any allocation, adjustment, reclassification, or elimination applied in the calculation, together with an explanation of the basis therefor, and (D) a reconciliation of Gross Profit to the Company’s financial statements for the applicable period (collectively, the “ Supporting Documentation ”). (b) After receipt of a Contingent Purchase Price Statement and the Supporting Documentation, Shareholder Representative shall have forty-five (45) days to review the Contingent Purchase Price Statement. Shareholder Representative and Shareholder Representative’s authorized representatives shall have reasonable access during normal business hours to all relevant books and records of Company to the extent required to complete their review of the Contingent Purchase Price Statement. Unless Shareholder Representative delivers written notice to Buyer on or prior to the forty-fifth (45th) day after Shareholder Representative’s receipt of the Contingent Purchase Price Statement and the Supporting Documentation of Shareholder Representative’s objection to the calculation of the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Payment, Shareholder Representative shall be deemed to have accepted and agreed to the calculation of the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Payment. Shareholder Representative’s objections shall be limited to: (i) Buyer’s determination of Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold, or Gross Profit, (ii) whether the applicable amounts set forth on the Contingent Purchase Price Statement were prepared in a manner consistent with the provisions of the Agreement, including this Exhibit C and the Earnout Accounting Methodology, or (iii) whether there were mathematical errors in the computation of any amount set forth in the Contingent Purchase Price Statement, as applicable. (c) If Shareholder Representative timely notifies Buyer of its objection to the calculation of the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Payment, Buyer and Shareholder Representative shall, within thirty (30) days (or such longer period as the parties may agree in writing) following such notice, attempt to resolve their differences and any resolution by them as to any disputed amounts shall be final, binding and conclusive. If Buyer and Shareholder Representative cannot reasonably resolve all objections by Shareholder Representative, then the disputed items shall be submitted to the Neutral Auditors for resolution consistent with the principles set forth in Section 2.6(e) of the Agreement, mutatis mutandis . (d) The payment of any Contingent Purchase Price Payment shall be due not later than five (5) Business Days following final determination of Gross Profit and the corresponding Contingent Purchase Price Payment for the applicable Contingent Purchase Price Period. The Contingent Purchase Price Payments shall be funded first from the Earnout Escrow Funds and then by Buyer, in accordance with Section 4. (e) Buyer may set off against any Contingent Purchase Price Payment on account of a claim that has been finally determined by a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction, resolved by a final and binding arbitral award, or resolved by written agreement of Buyer and Shareholder Representative. 4.
