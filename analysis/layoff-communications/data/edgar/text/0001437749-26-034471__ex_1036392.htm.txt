(“MCM”) for a base purchase price of $18 million, in addition to up to a $7 million performance-based earnout, subject to achieving financial milestones within 14 months of the transaction date. MCM is an Alabama-based precision manufacturer with a 30-year track record of designing, engineering, and fabricating metal components for the electric utility and critical infrastructure markets. From its 37,000 square foot facility in Northport, MCM supplies mission-critical hardware to some of the nation's leading utilities and their suppliers, with products engineered to meet stringent standards for corrosion resistance, conductivity, and performance under extreme weather conditions in high-power transmission line applications. Through a vertically integrated offering that spans machining, fabrication, assembly, kitting, and delivery, MCM has established itself as a leading component provider to the domestic electrical Transmission and Distribution ("T&D") sector. The company holds sole-source or preferred supplier status across many of its key customer relationships, with average tenure exceeding 20 years among its top ten customers. MCM is 100% U.S.-based and sources all raw materials exclusively through an established domestic procurement network, creating surety of supply for customers on an on-demand basis. MCM’s approximately 50 employees, including its senior leadership team, joined Broadwind on October 30. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY “The acquisition of MCM represents a defining milestone in Broadwind’s transformation into a leading solutions provider to the power generation and critical infrastructure markets, stated Eric Blashford, President and CEO of Broadwind. “Over the past several years, we’ve deliberately reshaped our business by divesting non-core assets, exiting underperforming business lines, and strengthening our balance sheet, while accelerating our strategic shift toward higher-value, more durable revenue streams supported by differentiated capabilities, defensible competitive advantages, and compelling long-term growth fundamentals.” “MCM expands our domestic manufacturing capacity and skilled workforce in support of the electrical transmission and distribution market, an industry benefiting from record investment in grid modernization, capacity expansion, hardening, and resilience,” continued Blashford. “Growing domestic electricity demand from data centers is a key contributor to load growth, reinforcing the need for sustained investment in transmission infrastructure. With approximately 95% of its trailing-twelve-month revenue through June 30, 2026, generated by T&D component manufacturing, MCM provides Broadwind with an immediate and compelling entry into this growing market. MCM’s seasoned team of precision manufacturing professionals, 30-year history of value creation, deep technical expertise, longstanding customer relationships, fully domestic production base, and proven record of profitable growth position the business as a strong strategic fit for Broadwind.” “MCM is immediately accretive to Broadwind, adds a higher-margin revenue stream to our portfolio, and enhances the quality of our earnings within our targeted vertical markets,” continued Blashford. “MCM introduces an entirely complementary customer base, providing meaningful diversification in new markets, while the company’s sole-source and preferred supplier positions underscore the strength of its customer relationships as an incumbent supplier. At a strategic level, MCM aligns with the key priorities we’ve outlined in our acquisition strategy, as it provides exposure to attractive end markets, an accretive margin profile, consistent profitability, and a fully domestic operating footprint. This acquisition exemplifies the lower-risk, value-creating bolt-on acquisitions we expect to define our growth strategy, over time.” “We are excited to join the Broadwind team and begin this next chapter of growth and transformation,” stated Wes Jones, Founder and CEO of MCM. “Together, we will offer our customers greater scale and expanded capabilities while preserving the differentiated value proposition that has defined MCM for three decades, including a commitment to exceptional quality, deep technical expertise, and responsive, on-demand service. Broadwind shares our commitment to operational excellence and customer service, and its platform will enable us to serve our customers even more effectively. I am proud of what our team has built and confident in what we will accomplish together.” TRANSACTION DETAILS On a trailing twelve-month basis through June 30, 2026, MCM generated total revenue of approximately $16.5 million and adjusted EBITDA of approximately $3.7 million, or ~23% of revenue. The transaction price, excluding any financial incentives that may be earned by MCM in the future upon achieving specified performance milestones, is 4.8x trailing-twelve months adjusted EBITDA as of June 30, 2026. The acquisition of MCM is expected to be accretive to Broadwind on both a margin rate and diluted earnings per share basis upon the closing of the transaction. As of June 30, 2026, the combined Broadwind and MCM businesses generated pro forma trailing twelve-month revenue of $90 million and adjusted EBITDA of $3.6 million, representing 4.0% of revenue. Pro forma net debt to trailing twelve-month adjusted EBITDA for the combined businesses was approximately 3.7x as of June 30, 2026. COMPELLING TRANSACTION RATIONALE ● Provides strategic entry into high-growth power and critical infrastructure markets. MCM positions Broadwind to capitalize on a multi-year investment cycle in domestic grid modernization, capacity expansion, hardening, and resilience, driven by rising electricity demand and the need for a more reliable transmission network. ● Capitalizes on a structural, multi-year investment cycle in transmission infrastructure. The U.S. transmission network spans more than 600,000 miles and continues to require substantial investment to support rising load growth, reliability, and resilience. Industry estimates indicate that data centers will be among the largest drivers of U.S. electricity-demand growth. ● Adds a higher-value revenue stream within targeted vertical markets. MCM delivered a four-year revenue CAGR of 25% through year-end 2025, reflective of increasing customer demand and recent capacity investments that position the business to convert a growing backlog. For the trailing twelve months ended June 30, 2026, MCM generated an adjusted EBITDA margin of 23%, well above Broadwind’s comparable margin. With approximately 95% of its trailing-twelve-month revenue derived from the domestic T&D market, MCM meaningfully accelerates Broadwind’s revenue mix shift toward power and critical infrastructure, directly advancing the Company’s stated strategic priorities. 2 ● Expands Broadwind ’ s domestic manufacturing footprint and skilled workforce. The acquisition adds approximately 50 skilled, non-union employees and MCM’s 37,000-square-foot design, engineering, and manufacturing facility in Northport, Alabama, to Broadwind’s operating platform. As of September 30, the addition of MCM increases Broadwind’s total employee base by more than 20%. ● Adds longstanding customer relationships supported by sole-source and preferred supplier positions. MCM introduces an entirely complementary customer base, meaningfully expanding and diversifying the combined company’s commercial relationships. MCM differentiates itself through short lead times, daily delivery capabilities, and the ability to consistently meet demanding utility-grade specifications. These capabilities have earned MCM sole-source or preferred supplier status across many key accounts, with average tenure among its top ten customer relationships exceeding 20 years. ● Provides a clear pathway to reduce net leverage. At closing, Broadwind expects pro forma net debt to trailing-twelve-month adjusted EBITDA, based on results through June 30, 2026, to be approximately 3.7x. The Company plans to reduce net leverage to 2.0x or less by year-end 2028 through a combination of earnings growth and debt repayment. Northland Capital Markets acted as the advisor to Broadwind in the transaction.
