II, a Cayman Islands exempted company (the “ Predecessor ”), NT1 Pty Ltd, an Australian proprietary limited company Australian Company Number 664 042 746 (the “ Company ”), as well as such other persons who are contemplated to later join this Agreement as the “Purchaser” and “Merger Sub” herein. The Predecessor, Company, Purchaser and Merger Sub are sometimes referred to herein individually as a “Party” and, collectively, as the “Parties.” RECITALS: A. The Company and its Subsidiaries (together, the “ Company Group ”) is an Australian mineral exploration company engaged in the acquisition, holding and exploration of mineral properties and related exploration assets focused on critical minerals, including rare earth elements, niobium and IOCG mineral systems, in the West Arunta region of Western Australia and the Northern Territory, Australia (the “ Business ”). B. Predecessor is a blank check company formed for the sole purpose of entering into a share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, recapitalization, reorganization or other similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities. C. Purchaser is to be a newly incorporated Cayman Islands exempted company and a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. D. Merger Sub is to be a Cayman Islands exempted company incorporated as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Purchaser for the purpose of consummating the SPAC Merger (as defined below). E. The parties intend that the Purchaser shall acquire all of the issued and outstanding Company Shares from the shareholders of the Company (the “Company Shareholders”) in exchange for the issuance of Purchaser Shares to the Company Shareholders, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth herein (the “ Acquisition Transfer and Exchange ”). F. After consummation of the Acquisition Transfer and Exchange, the Parties desire to merge Merger Sub with and into Predecessor with Predecessor continuing as the surviving company (the “ SPAC Merger ”), so that following the SPAC Merger, (i) Predecessor becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Purchaser (the Predecessor is hereinafter referred to for the periods from and after the SPAC Merger Effective Time (as defined below) as the “ SPAC Merger Surviving Company ”), (ii) the securityholders of Predecessor receive Purchaser Securities in exchange for their shares in the Predecessor (the “ Predecessor Securities ”), and (iii) Predecessor will, by operation of law, accede to all of the contracts, acquire all of the assets and assume all of the liabilities of Merger Sub. G. For U.S. federal income tax purposes, the Parties hereto intend that the Acquisition Transfer and Exchange and the SPAC Merger will qualify as a “transfer to a corporation controlled by the transferor” within the meaning of Section 351 of the Code, or that the SPAC Merger and Acquisition Contribution and Exchange will separately qualify as “reorganizations” within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Code. 5 H. The Board of Directors of the Company has determined that this Agreement, the Acquisition Transfer and Exchange, the SPAC Merger and the other transactions contemplated by this Agreement and the Additional Agreements (collectively, the “ Transactions ”) are fair and advisable to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its shareholders. I. The Board of Directors of Predecessor has determined that this Agreement and the Transactions are fair and advisable to, and in the best interests of Predecessor and its shareholders. J. Simultaneously with the execution of this Agreement, the Company has delivered the Company Written Consent to Predecessor evidencing the approval of this Agreement and the Transactions.
