The Parties desire to merge Merger Sub with and into Predecessor with Predecessor continuing as the surviving corporation (the “ Initial Merger ”), so that following the Initial Merger, (i) Predecessor becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary of Purchaser, (ii) the securityholders of Predecessor receive securities in Purchaser in exchange for their securities in Predecessor, and (iii) Predecessor will, by operation of law, accede to all of the contracts, acquire all of the assets and assume all of the liabilities of Merger Sub. E. Promptly following the Closing, Predecessor shall be liquidated and all of its assets shall be transferred to Purchaser. The Parties acknowledge that the Liquidation is expected to be a taxable transaction for Tax purposes. F. Simultaneously with the Initial Merger, Purchaser wishes to acquire the Company by issuing Purchaser Shares to the Shareholder in exchange for the transfer by the Shareholder of all of the Company Shares owned by the Shareholder (the “ Acquisition Transfer and Exchange ”). G.
