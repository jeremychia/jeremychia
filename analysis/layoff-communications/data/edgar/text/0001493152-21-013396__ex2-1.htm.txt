WHEREAS, the Seller Owner is the sole shareholder of the Seller and owns all of the issued and outstanding Equity Securities of the Seller, the Seller is the sole shareholder of SRS and owns all of the issued and outstanding Equity Securities of SRS, and for convenient reference, Seller and SRS are together referred to in this Agreement as the “ Company ”;
