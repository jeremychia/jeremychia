As previously disclosed on April 30, 2026, Stardust Power Inc. (the “Company”) received a letter from The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) on April 24, 2026, notifying the Company that it no longer satisfied the minimum $35,000,000 market value of listed securities requirement set forth in Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(b)(2), and the Company has until October 21, 2026 to regain compliance with that requirement. Further, on September 11, 2026, the Company received a written notice from the Nasdaq Listing Qualifications Department (the “Staff”) notifying the Company that, based upon the closing bid price of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Common Stock”), for the last 30 consecutive business days, the Company no longer satisfied the requirement of Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) that listed securities maintain a minimum bid price of $1.00 per share (the “Minimum Bid Price Requirement”). In accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(A), the Company was provided a compliance period of 180 calendar days, or until March 10, 2027, to regain compliance with the Minimum Bid Price Requirement. On October 15, 2026, the Company received a subsequent written notice (the “Staff Determination”) from the Staff indicating that it has determined that, as of October 14, 2026, the Company’s Common Stock had a closing bid price of $0.10 or less for ten consecutive trading days, triggering application of Nasdaq Listing Rule 5810(c)(3)(A)(iii), pursuant to which, if during any compliance period specified in Rule 5810(c)(3)(A), a company’s security has a closing bid price of $0.10 or less for ten consecutive trading days, the Listing Qualifications Department shall issue a Staff Delisting Determination under Rule 5810 with respect to that security. As a result, the Staff indicated that it has determined to suspend trading in the Company’s Common Stock and warrants and delist the Company’s securities at the opening of business on October 23, 2026, and that a Form 25-NSE will be filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission to remove the Company’s securities from listing and registration on The Nasdaq Stock Market. The Company may request a hearing before the Nasdaq Hearings Panel (the “Panel”) to appeal the Staff Determination, pursuant to the procedures set forth in the Nasdaq Listing Rule 5800 Series. A hearing request must be received no later than 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time on October 23, 2026, and, pursuant to Nasdaq Listing Rule 5815(a)(3), a non-refundable hearing fee of $20,000 must be submitted within seven calendar days of the date of the Staff Determination. Pursuant to Nasdaq Listing Rule 5815(a)(1)(B)(ii)(e), a timely request for a hearing will not stay the trading suspension of the Company’s securities. The Company is considering all available options; however, there can be no assurance that the Company would be successful in any appeal or that it will be able to regain compliance with Nasdaq’s listing requirements within the timeframe that may be provided by the Panel, or at all. On October 14, 2026, Mr. Udaychandra Devasper notified the Company of his decision to resign as the Company’s Chief Financial Officer. Mr. Devasper’s resignation is anticipated to be effective as of October 23, 2026. Item 8.01 Other Events. The Board of Directors of the Company (the “Board”) and management are evaluating whether to request a hearing before the Panel, which will depend on, among other things, the Company’s ability to present an acceptable compliance plan, the costs of an appeal and the Company’s available resources. Because a hearing request will not stay the trading suspension, the Company may seek to have its securities quoted on an over-the-counter market. The Board and management will also consider other strategic alternatives, including a sale, a merger or other business combination. There can be no assurance that any of these measures or alternatives will be adopted or completed, or that the Company will be able to obtain financing on acceptable terms, or at all.
