(the “Company”) ( OTCID:AITX ), a developer and operator of AI-driven security and productivity solutions for enterprise clients, today described four operating milestones it intends to use to measure progress toward breakeven. CEO/CTO and founder Steve Reinharz discusses the milestones in a video update available at www.youtu.be/NSPCFNvr4jk . Each milestone applies a stricter test than the one before it. All four are internal management measures, not measures under generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”); see “Important Information Regarding Management Measures” below. We are focused on milestones one and two, and as time goes on, I expect to be able to forecast three and four.” Starting Point For its fiscal second quarter ended August 31, 2026, the Company reported revenue of $1,985,604, up 8% sequentially and 5% year over year. Revenue from customers other than its largest grew 61% year over year, and that customer’s share of revenue fell to 18% from 46% a year earlier. Recurring device rental revenue represented 89% of total revenue. Two Levers: Revenue and Cost The Company’s projection for Milestone 1 assumes revenue growth of approximately 2.7% per month, the pace implied by its second-quarter sequential growth of 8%. “We have two basic levers: increase revenue and decrease cost,” said Reinharz. “Our revenue assumption is based on what the business actually did last quarter.” On cost, the Company expanded its August target of approximately $200,000 in monthly cash spend reductions to more than $350,000 per month from February 2027, measured against its August 2026 run-rate, as announced October 20, 2026. The program includes a reduction in force of approximately 7 positions in August 2026, as previously announced, and 17 positions in October 2026. It also includes reductions in marketing, promotion, cloud and other operating costs. The Company’s sales force was not affected. The program also includes salary deferrals, which reduce cash spend faster than they reduce total expenses. Deferred amounts remain obligations of the Company. This is why Milestone 1 measures cash spend and Milestone 2 measures expenses as incurred. “These were hard decisions, and we don’t take them lightly,” added Reinharz. “We tried to cut costs without significantly affecting our major initiatives or our existing revenue streams.” In August, the Company expected to reach Milestone 1 in December 2026. As announced October 20, 2026 , the expanded program phases in through year-end, and the Company now expects to reach it around January 2027. Gross Margin Second-quarter gross margin was 67% excluding a one-time, non-cash inventory write-down of $270,987 related to the discontinued residential camera line, and 54% as reported. Management’s objective is to return gross margin to the 75%–85% range, a level the Company has reached before: gross margin was approximately 83% in its fiscal fourth quarter ended February 28, 2026, as derived from its annual and nine-month results. The hardware price increases effective September 22, 2026 further support that objective. Debt Service Certain of the Company’s monthly-repayment loans, including merchant cash advances, are scheduled to be fully repaid beginning in April 2027, which would reduce monthly cash debt service and support progress toward Milestones 3 and 4. This is one data point. The Company’s other debt obligations, including those described in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ended August 31, 2026, could change the timing of those milestones. Inventory Financing The Company currently pays cash for the inventory it uses to build devices because it has not obtained inventory financing on acceptable terms. The Company expects inventory needs to grow with ROAMEO ™, RAIA™ and its existing product lineup. As it reaches Milestones 1 and 2, the Company hopes to become eligible for non-dilutive financing, such as secured, lower-cost inventory financing, rather than the financing it relies on today. “What we’re hoping happens as we hit milestones one and two is that new, non-dilutive ways to finance inventory become options for us,” concluded Reinharz. “That’s a milestone I can’t wait to get to.” The Company intends to report progress against these milestones in future updates. Important Information Regarding Management Measures The four milestones are management estimates and internal measures, not results achieved, guarantees of future performance or projections of profitability under GAAP. They differ from GAAP net income (loss) and from net cash provided by or used in operating activities, and should not be considered substitutes for those measures. The Company cannot provide a quantitative reconciliation of these forward-looking measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures without unreasonable effort, because it cannot reliably estimate future working capital changes, non-cash items and other reconciling items. Revenue recognized in a month does not necessarily equal cash collected in that month. Milestone 1 compares recognized revenue with cash operating expenditures. Salary deferrals and deferred compensation reduce cash expenditures but remain obligations of the Company, and Milestones 1 and 2 exclude them. Milestones 2 through 4 exclude depreciation and other non-cash items. Collection timing, deferred obligations, debt service, inventory purchases and other cash requirements may require financing even if a milestone is reached. Reaching any milestone would not, by itself, establish GAAP profitability, positive net cash provided by operating activities, sufficient liquidity to meet the Company’s obligations or an end to its need for outside financing. The milestones are not defined under GAAP or any other accounting standard. Management calculates them from internal records, and they have not been audited or reviewed by the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm.
