PROPOSAL 1 FIRST SHARE CONSOLIDATION Purpose of First Share Consolidation The Company’s Class A Ordinary Shares are listed on The Nasdaq Capital Market under the trading symbol of “FCHL.” In order for the Class A Ordinary Shares to continue to be listed on The Nasdaq Capital Market, the Company must satisfy various listing standards established by Nasdaq. Among others, Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) requires that listed shares maintain a minimum bid price of US$1.00 per share (the “ Bid Price Rule ”). To enhance the Company’s ability to maintain compliance with the Bid Price Rule, the Board believes that it is in the best interest of the Company and its shareholders to effect the Share Consolidations at the applicable ratios described below. The Company is also considering capital-raising opportunities that may involve the issuance of additional Class A Ordinary Shares or securities convertible into or exercisable for Class A Ordinary Shares. If undertaken, these financings could dilute existing shareholders’ ownership interests and place downward pressure on the market price of the Class A Ordinary Shares. In anticipation of these potential effects, the Company is proposing the Share Consolidations with the additional objective of increasing the per-share trading price of the Class A Ordinary Shares and better positioning the Company to pursue future financing opportunities. The Board believes that a higher per-share trading price may enhance the marketability of the Class A Ordinary Shares and provide greater flexibility in structuring potential capital-raising transactions. However, the Share Consolidations would not eliminate or reduce the ownership dilution resulting from any subsequent issuance of shares, and there can be no assurance that they will achieve or sustain a higher trading price or improve the Company’s ability to raise capital. The Board believes that maintaining the listing of the Class A Ordinary Shares on The Nasdaq Capital Market and preserving the Company’s ability to pursue future financing opportunities are important to the Company’s business and future prospects. Delisting could decrease liquidity and increase trading price volatility, reduce current or future analyst coverage and diminish institutional investor interest, potentially making future capital-raising transactions more difficult or costly. In addition, delisting could cause a loss of confidence among corporate partners, customers and employees, which could harm the Company’s business and future prospects. Accordingly, the Board believes that the Share Consolidations are in the best interests of the Company and its shareholders as a means of supporting continued compliance with the Bid Price Rule and better positioning the Company for potential capital-raising transactions, including in anticipation of their potentially dilutive effects. First Consolidation The First Share Consolidation will consolidate every two (2) issued and unissued shares of each class with a par value of US$0.00225 into one (1) share of the corresponding class with a par value of US$0.0045. The First Share Consolidation will apply equally to the Class A Ordinary Shares, Class B Ordinary Shares and preferred shares. The Consolidated Class A Shares, Consolidated Class B Shares and Consolidated Preferred Shares will rank pari passu in all respects with the corresponding existing shares and will have the same rights and be subject to the same restrictions (save as to par value) as set out in the third amended and restated memorandum and articles of association of the Company. Conditions to the First Share Consolidation If approved by the Members, the First Share Consolidation will take effect as soon as practicable following the Meeting, from the opening of trading on the Market Effective Date stated in the Company Event Notification Form submitted to Nasdaq (the “First Consolidation Effective Time”), subject to the Nasdaq Notification Condition having been satisfied. The Nasdaq Notification Condition requires that (i) the Company has submitted a completed Company Event Notification Form to Nasdaq and (ii) the Company has made the public announcement required by Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(b)(4). Under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(e)(7), the Company must notify Nasdaq of the First Share Consolidation by filing a completed Company Event Notification Form, together with a draft of the public disclosure required by Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(b)(4), no later than 12:00 p.m. ET ten (10) calendar days before the proposed Market Effective Date. Under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(b)(4), the Company must publicly announce the First Share Consolidation no later than 12:00 p.m. ET at least two (2) business days before the Market Effective Date. In evaluating whether or not to conduct the Share Consolidations, the Board considered various negative factors associated with such corporate action. These factors include: the negative perception of a share consolidation held by some investors, analysts and other stock market participants; the fact that the share prices of some companies and the Company that have been effected by share consolidation have subsequently declined back to pre-consolidation levels; the adverse effect on liquidity that might be caused by a reduced number of shares outstanding; and the costs associated with implementing the Share Consolidations. The Board considered these factors, and the potential harm of being delisted from The Nasdaq Capital Market. The Board determined that continued listing on The Nasdaq Capital Market is in the best interest of the Company and its shareholders, and that the Share Consolidations are probably necessary to maintain the listing of the Company’s Class A Ordinary Shares on The Nasdaq Capital Market. In addition, there can be no assurance that, after the Share Consolidations, the Company will be able to maintain the listing of the Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company on The Nasdaq Capital Market. The Nasdaq Capital Market maintains several other continued listing requirements currently applicable to the listing of the Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company. Shareholders should recognize that if the Share Consolidations are effected, they will own a smaller number of Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company than they currently own. While the Company expects that the Share Consolidations will result in an increase in the market price of the relevant shares of the Company, they may not increase the market price of the relevant shares of the Company in proportion to the reduction in the number of Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company outstanding or result in a permanent increase in the market price (which depends on many factors, including but not limited to our performance, prospects and other factors that may be unrelated to the number of shares outstanding). If the Share Consolidations are effected and the market price of the Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company declines, the percentage decline as an absolute number and as a percentage of the Company’s overall market capitalization may be greater than would occur in the absence of the Share Consolidations. Furthermore, the liquidity of the Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company could be adversely affected by the reduced number of shares that would be outstanding after the Share Consolidations. Accordingly, the Share Consolidations may not achieve the desired results that have been outlined above. Fractional Shares No fractional shares will be issued in connection with either the First Share Consolidation or the Second Share Consolidation. If a shareholder would otherwise be entitled to receive a fractional share upon either consolidation, the number of shares to be received by that shareholder will be rounded up to the next whole number. Any additional fraction of a share issued as a result of such rounding up will be credited as fully paid out of the Company’s share premium account or other reserves. Effects of the Share Consolidations Authorized Shares and Unissued Shares The Share Consolidations will apply to both the issued and unissued shares of each class, and the authorized share capital of the Company will remain US$23,000,000. Following the First Share Consolidation, the authorized share capital will change from 10,177,777,777.78 class A ordinary shares, 22,222,222.22 class B ordinary shares and 22,222,222.22 preferred shares, each of par value US$0.00225, to 5,088,888,888.89 class A ordinary shares, 11,111,111.11 class B ordinary shares and 11,111,111.11 preferred shares, each of par value US$0.0045. Following the Second Share Consolidation, the authorized share capital will change to 50,888,888.8889 class A ordinary shares, 111,111.1111 class B ordinary shares and 111,111.1111 preferred shares, each of par value US$0.45. Issued and Outstanding Shares The First Share Consolidation will reduce the number of issued and outstanding shares of each class at a ratio of two (2) to one (1), accompanied by a corresponding increase in the par value of the shares from US$0.00225 to US$0.0045. Subject to the Fundraising Condition, the Second Share Consolidation will then reduce the number of issued and outstanding shares of each class at a ratio of one hundred (100) to one (1), accompanied by a corresponding increase in the par value of the shares from US$0.0045 to US$0.45. Taken together, the two consolidations will result in an aggregate 200-to-1 reduction in the number of shares, subject to the treatment of fractional shares. The consolidated shares of each class will rank pari passu in all respects with each other and will have the same rights and be subject to the same restrictions (save as to par value) as set out in the applicable memorandum and articles of association. Each shareholder’s proportionate ownership of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company immediately following each consolidation would remain the same, with the exception of adjustments related to the treatment of fractional shares (see above). Proportionate adjustments will be made based on the applicable ratio of each Share Consolidation to the per share exercise price and the number of shares issuable upon the exercise or conversion of all outstanding options, warrants, convertible or exchangeable securities entitling the holders to purchase, exchange for, or convert into, our ordinary shares. This will result in approximately the same aggregate price being required to be paid under such options, warrants, convertible or exchangeable securities upon exercise, and approximately the same value of the Class A Ordinary Shares of the Company being delivered upon such exercise, exchange or conversion, immediately following the applicable Share Consolidation as was the case immediately preceding that Share Consolidation.
