The Original Report otherwise remains unchanged. Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. As previously announced, on December 13, 2023, the Company committed to a workforce reduction and other cost saving initiatives as part of the Company’s efforts to reduce operating expenses. At the time of the Original Report, the Company estimated that it would incur one-time severance related payments of approximately $10 million. In addition to these charges, the Company has now determined an additional non-cash benefit related to stock-based compensation expense. Specifically, the Company expects to recognize a reversal of stock-based compensation expense of approximately $5.9 million, largely attributed to the modification of stock awards, in the quarter ended December 31, 2023.
