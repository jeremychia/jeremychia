By presenting revenue, adjusted EBITDA margin and adjusted net income margin net of TAC, we believe that investors and analysts are able to obtain a clearer picture of our business without the impact of the revenues we share with our partners. ​ SBC ​ SBC is a significant expense item, and an important part of our compensation and incentive programs.
