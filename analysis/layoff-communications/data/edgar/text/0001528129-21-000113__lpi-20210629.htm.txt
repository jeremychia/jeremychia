On June 29, 2021 (the “Effective Date”), Laredo Petroleum, Inc. (“Laredo” or the “Company”) committed to a company-wide reorganization effort (the “Plan”) that includes a workforce reduction of 14 individuals, or approximately 5% of the workforce. The reduction in workforce was communicated to employees on the Effective Date and implemented immediately, subject to certain administrative procedures. The Company put the Plan in place in order to reduce costs and better position the Company for the future. In connection with the Plan, the Company estimates that it will incur an aggregate of approximately $9.8 million of one-time charges in the second quarter of 2021 comprising of compensation, tax, professional, outplacement and insurance related expenses.
