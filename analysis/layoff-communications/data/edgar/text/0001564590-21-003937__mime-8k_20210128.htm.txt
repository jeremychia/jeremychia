Item 2.05 Costs Associated With Exit or Disposal Activities. On January 28, 2021, the Board of Directors of the Company approved a restructuring plan designed to align the Company’s resources with its strategy. The restructuring plan, which includes a reduction of the Company’s workforce by approximately 4%, will permit the Company to increase investment in strategic growth areas. The Company currently estimates that it will recognize pre-tax charges to its GAAP financial results of approximately $3.7 million, consisting of severance and other one-time termination benefits, and other restructuring related costs. These charges are primarily cash-based and are expected to be recognized in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2021. The actions associated with the restructuring plan are expected to be completed by the end of the first quarter of fiscal 2022. Item 9.01 Financial Statements and Exhibits. (d) Exhibits .
