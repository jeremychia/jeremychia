Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities On November 6, 2026, GrowGeneration Corp. (the "Company") committed to a strategic restructuring plan (the "2026 Restructuring Plan") focused on long-term profitability in its Gardening and Cultivation segment as part of its accelerated, strategic transformation into a primarily commercial, proprietary-brand-driven business. The 2026 Restructuring Plan primarily includes reductions in cost structure by closing and consolidating four to five underperforming retail locations, workforce reductions, and other operational improvements. The Company expects that the restructuring activities associated with the 2026 Restructuring Plan will be substantially completed by the end of the first quarter of fiscal 2027. The Company is not currently able to estimate the amount or range of amounts it will incur in connection with the 2026 Restructuring Plan, required by paragraphs (b), (c) and (d) of Item 2.05 of Form 8-K, including charges related to the consolidation or elimination of real estate leases, employee termination benefits, and other associated costs. The Company will file an amendment to this report after it makes a determination of such estimates or range of estimates, if any.
