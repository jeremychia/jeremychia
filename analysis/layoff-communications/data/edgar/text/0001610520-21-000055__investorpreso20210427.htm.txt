Slide 10 – Global Wealth Management As just mentioned, we have introduced net new fee-generating assets, a new performance measure for GWM this quarter. Compared with net new money, net new fee-generating assets exclude flows related to assets that, from trading or new issuance, predominantly generate transaction-based revenues in the form of commissions and transaction spreads. This new KPI captures net flows related to mandates, investment funds, hedge funds and private markets investments, and includes dividend and interest payments into mandates.
