F. Acquiror desires to purchase from NewCo and the Seller Guarantors and the Sellers desire to cause NewCo to sell to Acquiror, all of the issued and outstanding Company Stock, all upon the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement (the “Stock Purchase”).
