Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On November 2, 2021, the Board of Directors of Zillow Group made the determination to wind down Zillow Offers operations. This decision was made in light of home pricing unpredictability, capacity constraints and other operational challenges faced by Zillow Offers that were exacerbated by an unprecedented housing market, a global pandemic and a difficult labor and supply chain environment. Zillow Group expects to continue to complete the purchase of homes currently under contract and to renovate and sell properties currently in inventory through 2022. The wind down is expected to take several quarters and result in approximately a 25% reduction of the Company’s workforce. In connection with the preparation of financial statements for the quarterly period ended September, 30, 2021, Zillow Group recorded a $304.4 million write-down of inventory associated with Zillow Offers as a result of purchasing homes during the third quarter at higher prices than Zillow Group’s current estimates of future selling prices after selling costs. Zillow Group expects to record additional charges of approximately $240 million to $265 million associated with homes that Zillow Group was under contract to purchase at September 30, 2021, which may be canceled without significant penalty, within cost of revenue in its consolidated statements of operations during the three months ending December 31, 2021. Zillow Group expects to record additional pre-tax charges of approximately $175 million to $230 million related to the wind down of Zillow Offers operations, primarily in the fourth quarter of 2021 and the first half of 2022. Of the charges that will be recorded as impairment and restructuring costs in Zillow Group’s consolidated statements of operations, an estimated $110 million to $150 million relate to employee termination costs, an estimated $15 million to $20 million relate to charges associated with long-lived assets pertaining to Zillow Offers, an estimated $5 million to $10 million relate to charges associated with termination of contractual obligations, and the remaining amounts primarily relate to approximately $40 million to $50 million of charges associated with winding down financing facilities related to Zillow Offers operations, which will be recorded as interest expense in Zillow Group’s consolidated statements of operations. Zillow Group plans to fund the cash costs through existing cash and investment balances. As a result of the decision to wind down Zillow Offers operations, Zillow Group plans to report Zillow Offers as a discontinued operation beginning with the period during which disposition of the business is complete. Our evaluation of various alternative courses of action that we may take related to the wind down is in progress and we may incur additional costs as a result of such actions. All estimated amounts are subject to change until finalized.
