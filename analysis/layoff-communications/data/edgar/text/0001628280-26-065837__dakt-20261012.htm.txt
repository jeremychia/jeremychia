On October 12, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “ Board ”) of Daktronics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “ Company ”), approved certain manufacturing network optimization initiatives (the “ Optimization Initiatives ”). The Optimization Initiatives are intended to improve factory utilization, increase manufacturing flexibility, and reduce structural costs across the Company’s global manufacturing network. As part of the Optimization Initiatives, the Company intends to exit the highly customized portions of the international transportation business, which serves rail, bus, and roadway customers outside of North America. As such, the Board gave approval for the Company to begin a phased-exit of operations at its Ennistymon, Ireland manufacturing facility (the “ Irish Facility ”) and for Daktronics Ireland Co. Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, to enter into a plan of termination with the majority of its Irish employees (collectively the “ Irish Phase-Out ”). The Irish Phase-Out is expected to be substantially complete in the first half of fiscal 2028. On October 14, 2026 the Company issued a press release regarding the Optimization Initiatives. A copy of this release is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. The Irish Phase-Out, including the Company’s obligations under the plan of termination, is estimated to result in cumulative cash and non-cash charges of approximately $13.5 million, comprised of: $11.7 million related to severance, stay bonuses, and other termination benefits; $0.8 million related to the phased-exit of operations at the Irish Facility, legal costs, and equipment transfer costs; and $1.0 million of asset impairments associated with property, plant, and equipment at the Irish Facility. Approximately $12.3 million of the Irish Phase-Out costs are expected to be recorded during fiscal 2027, with the remaining charges in the first half of fiscal 2028. Reductions in workforce are subject to local regulatory requirements. Future cash payments related to these charges are anticipated to be $12.5 million, primarily related to the payment of severance and related benefits, contract terminations, equipment dismantlement and relocation and inventory transfers, and costs to exit operations at the Irish Facility. The actions associated with these charges are expected to be substantially complete by mid-fiscal 2028. Future actions by the Company; changes in circumstances from current assumptions, including any site disposition gains or losses; or fluctuations in currency exchange rates may cause actual results and future cash payments to differ. Other than the costs outlined above with respect to the Irish Phase-Out, the Company’s exit from the highly customized portions of the international transportation business is not expected to have a material effect on the Company’s consolidated financial results. Item 2.06 Material Impairments. The information set forth under Item 2.05 of this Current Report on Form 8-K is incorporated herein by reference. Item 8.01 Other Events. On September 2, 2026 the Company filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the fiscal quarter ended August 1, 2026 (the " Form 10-Q "). The Company has determined that certain disclosure contained in the section of the Form 10-Q entitled " Liquidity and Capital Resources " regarding the Company's expected capital expenditures for fiscal 2027 contained an inadvertent clerical error. Specifically, page 29 the Form 10-Q stated that the Company projected total capital expenditures of approximately $13.2 million for fiscal 2027. The Company’s intended projection of capital expenditures for fiscal 2027 at the time of filing was approximately $23.4 million. The Company is furnishing this Item 8.01 on Current Report on Form 8-K solely to correct and update the previously disclosed estimate. The correction does not affect the Company's previously reported consolidated financial statements or related financial statement disclosures contained in the Form 10-Q. Except as described above, the Company has not identified any other inaccuracies in the Form 10-Q arising from this matter. Item 9.01 Financial Statements and Exhibits. (d) Exhibits.
