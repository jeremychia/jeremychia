Telix Pharmaceuticals Notice of General Meeting Invitation from the Chair Dear Shareholder On behalf of the Board of Directors, I am pleased to invite you to attend a General Meeting (Meeting) of Telix Pharmaceuticals Limited (Telix), relating to the proposed transaction of Telix and ITM Isotope Technologies Munich SE (ITM), a global leader in radioisotope production and radiopharmaceutical development. As announced on September 21, 2026, Telix has entered into a strategic agreement (the Transaction) to acquire 100% of the shares in ITM for total upfront consideration valued at US$1.65 billion on a cash-free/debt-free basis (as further described in the Explanatory Notes below) and additional contingent consideration of up to US$700 million upon achievement of future regulatory approvals and commercial sales milestones for ITM-11, ITM’s lead therapy candidate for the treatment of neuroendocrine tumors. The Transaction has been unanimously approved by Telix’s Board of Directors and, as of the date of this letter, has been agreed to by shareholders holding over 93% of ITM’s shares1. The Meeting is being held to seek Shareholder approval for certain matters related to the Transaction and will be convened at 10:00 a.m. (Melbourne time) on Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at The Events Centre, Level 5, Tower 2/727 Collins Street, Melbourne, Victoria 3008 (Venue) and via Telix’s share registry’s Virtual Meeting Online Platform at meetings.openbriefing.com/ TLXMeeting2026. The Board believes this Transaction is a defining step in Telix's evolution. It brings together two established leaders in radiopharmaceuticals with deep scientific expertise, a pipeline of novel candidates and the scale and infrastructure to compete and lead over the long term. The Transaction builds on many years of partnership between Telix and ITM, giving us a strong understanding of ITM's capabilities, operations and culture, and providing a solid foundation for integration and future value creation. The terms of the proposed Transaction are set out in the Explanatory Notes attached to this Notice of Meeting and further detailed in the investor presentation included as Appendix A (Investor Presentation).2 This Notice of Meeting should be read in its entirety in conjunction with the accompanying Explanatory Notes and Investor Presentation. A Glossary of defined terms used in this Notice of Meeting is included after the Explanatory Notes. Strategic rationale Against a backdrop of rapid industry growth and consolidation, the Board believes that the next generation of radiopharmaceutical leaders will require integrated capabilities across discovery and development of both diagnostic and therapeutic agents, isotope production, manufacturing, supply chain and commercialization. This Transaction is designed to position Telix at the forefront of this industry evolution by bringing together three highly complementary growth engines: • A leading commercial precision medicine business with global reach and proven scale; • One of the industry's most extensive late-stage therapeutic radiopharmaceutical pipelines; and • A world-class radioisotope manufacturing and supply platform. The Transaction will further strengthen Telix’s leadership as a vertically integrated radiopharmaceutical company with the capabilities required to develop and deliver innovative treatments to patients globally. The addition of ITM’s commercially validated isotope production and distribution business adds a diversified revenue stream underpinned by strong historic growth and positive outlook, while securing supply chain control and infrastructure to support future therapeutic launches using a variety of isotopes. ITM’s late-stage compound, ITM-11 (177Lu-edotreotide), is complementary to Telix and has the potential to accelerate Telix’s entry into the commercial therapeutic market for neuroendocrine tumors, a commercially validated and clinically significant market for targeted radionuclide therapy. ITM-11, which is a differentiated somatostatin receptor (SSTR)- targeted treatment of gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (GEP-NETs), has completed a successful Phase 3 trial (COMPETE, NCT03049189) and fully enrolled a second indication expansion Phase 3 study (COMPOSE, NCT04919226). The combined organization is expected to generate unaudited pro forma revenue and other income exceeding US$1.3 billion in 2026, based on management estimates. ITM's profitable, cash-generative isotope manufacturing business is expected to contribute continued growth. Along with cost savings already underway and targeted post-closing synergies, ITM is expected to contribute positively to Group EBITDA3 from 2027 onwards.
