Item 2.05. Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On November 4, 2026, the board of directors of Eos Energy Enterprises, Inc.(“we”, “the Company” or “Eos”) adopted a plan that is intended to better align cost structure and support gross margin improvement to the Company’s target financial model, while also aligning manufacturing capabilities with current and anticipated business needs and the Company's strategic priorities. As part of this plan, the Company is consolidating the manufacturing facilities located in Turtle Creek, Pennsylvania and Thorn Hill, Pennsylvania. We expect the actions defined under this plan will be largely completed by the end of June 2027 with the production line anticipated to be operational within the first quarter 2027. The plan is expected to result in the Company recording cash restructuring charges in the aggregate amount of approximately $16 to $18 million on a GAAP basis, estimated to be comprised primarily of costs relating to moving and clean up.
