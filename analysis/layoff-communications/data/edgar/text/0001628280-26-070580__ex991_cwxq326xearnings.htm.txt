Third Quarter 2026 Highlights: • Reported sales of $953 million, up 10%, operating income of $188 million , operating margin of 19.7%, and diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $3.77; • Adjusted operating income of $189 million, up 11%; • Adjusted operating margin of 19.9%, up 30 basis points; • Adjusted diluted EPS of $3.94, up 16%; • Free cash flow (FCF) of $157 million, generating 108% FCF conversion; • Total share repurchases of $352 million; • New orders of $1.1 billion, up 15%, reflected a 1.1x book-to-bill; and • Backlog of $4.6 billion, up 12% year-to-date. Raised Full-Year 2026 Adjusted Financial Outlook: • Sales increased to new range of 8% to 10% growth (previously 8% to 9%), reflecting continued strong growth in the Aerospace & Defense (A&D) and Commercial end markets; • Operating income increased to new range of 12% to 14% growth (previously 11% to 13%); • Operating margin increased to new range of 19.2 % to 19.4 % , now up 60 to 80 basis points compared with the prior year (previously 19.1 % to 19.3 %, up 50 to 70 basis points) ; • Diluted EPS increased to new range of $15.40 to $15.60, now up 16% to 18% (previously $15.10 to $15.40, up 14% to 16%); and • FCF increased by $ 10 million to new range of $595 t o $615 million, which continues to reflect greater than 105% FCF conversion. "Curtiss-Wright delivered a strong third quarter, highlighted by double-digit growth in both revenue and operating income, and mid-teens growth in adjusted diluted EPS, as we continue to execute on the strength of our product portfolio,” said Lynn M. Bamford, Chair and CEO of Curtiss-Wright Corporation. "Orders increased 15% year-over-year, primarily driven by continued strong demand in defense electronics and growth across all of our A&D markets, reinforcing our confidence in our ability to deliver consistent and durable future growth. Based on our strong year-to-date performance, we have raised our full-year guidance for all major financial metrics. We now anticipate sales growth of 8% to 10%, along with improved profitability across all three segments, as our Pivot to Growth Strategy continues to build momentum." "We also remain committed to deploying our strong balance sheet to support disciplined and strategic capital allocation. We are on track to deliver near-record share repurchases in 2026 and continue to drive internal investments to support our future growth, which underscores our confidence in the business and our commitment to delivering consistent and superior results for our shareholders."
