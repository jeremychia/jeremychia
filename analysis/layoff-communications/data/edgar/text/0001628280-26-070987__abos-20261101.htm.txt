As previously disclosed in its Current Report on Form 8-K filed on November 10, 2023, Acumen Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a Loan and Security Agreement, dated November 10, 2023 (the “Loan Agreement”), with K2 HealthVentures LLC (the “Lender”). The Loan Agreement provided for a term loan facility (the “Term Loan”) in an aggregate principal amount of $50.0 million, of which the Company borrowed $30.0 million in the first tranche on November 10, 2023. The principal amount of the Term Loan outstanding under the Loan Agreement bore interest per annum at the greater of (i) 9.65% or (ii) the sum of the prime rate last quoted in The Wall Street Journal plus 1.15% for such interest period, and was scheduled to mature on November 1, 2027. The Loan Agreement provided for a final payment fee of an additional $1.6 million (the “Final Payment Fee”) due upon repayment of the Term Loan. On November 3, 2026, the Company provided notice of its intention to voluntarily prepay in full, using cash on hand, all outstanding obligations under the Loan Agreement in an aggregate amount of approximately $23.3 million (the “Payoff Amount”). The Payoff Amount of $23.3 million consisted of outstanding principal, accrued and unpaid interest, the Final Payment Fee and certain Lender’s expenses. On November 5, 2026, upon receipt by the Lender of the Payoff Amount, the Loan Agreement and certain other loan documents executed in connection therewith (other than those provisions that by their terms survive termination) were terminated, all commitments of the Lender thereunder were terminated, the Lender’s conversion right under the Loan Agreement terminated, and all liens and security interests securing the Company’s obligations under the Loan Agreement were released. The foregoing description of the Loan Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Loan Agreement, a copy of which was filed as Exhibit 10.15 to the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2023, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on March 26, 2024. Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. As previously disclosed in its Current Report on Form 8-K filed on October 30, 2026, the Company announced that it plans to cease development of sabirnetug, including the open-label extension portion of its ALTITUDE-AD trial, and to evaluate its strategic options, including with respect to its Enhanced Brain Delivery™ (EBD ® ) program. In connection with these decisions, on November 1, 2026, the board of directors of the Company (the “Board”) approved certain steps to implement cash conservation measures, including a reduction of the Company’s workforce by 52 full-time employees, or approximately 90% of the Company’s current employee base (the “Restructuring”). The Company initiated the Restructuring on November 2, 2026, with the majority of impacted positions ending in November 2026 and a small number of positions maintained into early 2027. As a result of the Restructuring, the Company estimates that it will incur approximately $7.75 million in costs resulting from cash expenditures consisting of one-time severance payments and related expenses. The Company expects to record a significant portion of these charges in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027. The Restructuring is expected to be substantially complete by early 2027. The estimates of costs that the Company expects to incur and the timing thereof are subject to a number of assumptions and actual results may differ. The Company anticipates that it will incur additional costs in connection with its evaluation of strategic options, including the retention of outplacement and advisory services and related matters, which the Company is unable to estimate in good faith at this time. In accordance with paragraph (d) of Item 2.05 of Form 8-K, the Company will file an amendment to this Current Report on Form 8-K once it makes a determination of such estimates or range of estimates. The Company may also incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated in connection with the Restructuring.
