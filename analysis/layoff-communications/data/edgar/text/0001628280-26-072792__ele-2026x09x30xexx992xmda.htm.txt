Elemental's royalty and mineral property portfolio consists of approximately 20 producing assets and more than 260 royalties across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and Australia. The Company's common shares are listed on the Nasdaq Exchange ("NASDAQ") and on the Toronto Stock Exchange ("TSX") under the ticker symbol "ELE". Strategy Elemental’s strategy is to pursue disciplined growth through the acquisition and financing of high-quality royalty and streaming interests, with the objective of building a diversified portfolio that generates sustainable cash flow and provides long-term growth potential. The Company focuses on acquiring royalty and stream interests, pursuing strategic investments and financing opportunities, and maintaining balanced exposure to precious and base metals, with an emphasis on precious metals. The key components of Elemental’s business strategy are summarized as follows: Royalty and Streaming Acquisitions and Financing Elemental seeks to acquire and finance royalty and streaming interests across a spectrum of asset stages, ranging from producing operations to advanced development projects. The Company targets opportunities in both the precious metals, and base metals. Through disciplined capital allocation and transaction structuring, Elemental aims to build a portfolio that delivers near-term cash flow, long-term growth potential and exposure to commodity price upside. Royalty Generation Royalty generation has historically been a complementary component of Elemental’s broader acquisition, financing, and investment strategy. Through these activities, the Company has leveraged in-country geological expertise to originate mineral projects, establish partnerships, and generate royalty interests with long-term upside potential. As part of its strategy to sharpen its focus on its core royalty and streaming business, the Company is actively pursuing the divestiture of its royalty generation business. This initiative is expected to streamline operations, reduce ongoing expenditures, and allow the Company to concentrate its resources on acquiring and managing royalty and stream interests. Highlights Q3 2026 continued with strong performance across Elemental’s diversified portfolio, supported by favourable commodity prices and contributions from key producing assets, including Caserones, Timok, Karlawinda, Bonikro, and Leeville. The quarter continued to further demonstrate the advantages of the Company's expanded scale and diversification following the merger with EMX Royalty Corporation in late 2025. A key highlight of the quarter was the successful completion of the acquisition of Vizsla Royalties Corp. ("Vizsla"), adding a significant royalty interest over the high-grade Panuco silver-gold project in Mexico. The acquisition further strengthens Elemental's portfolio, increases its silver exposure and adds a cornerstone development-stage asset with meaningful long-term growth potential. During the quarter, Elemental also announced the acquisition of a portfolio of five streams and royalties from Orion Mine Finance for approximately $290 million, further expanding its portfolio of cash-generating assets. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed the acquisition of four of the five assets, with the acquisition of the remaining asset expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. The Company also increased its revolving credit facility to $250 million, plus a $50 million accordion, providing additional financial flexibility to support its growth strategy. In addition to these developments, Elemental announced plans to divest its royalty generation business to streamline operations and focus on its core royalty and streaming portfolio. Such divestment is expected to significantly reduce general and administrative costs. Elemental continued to return capital to shareholders through its normal course issuer bid and the declaration of its third quarterly dividend of $0.03 per share. Supported by its expanded portfolio, disciplined capital allocation and continued growth, Elemental remains well positioned to deliver long-term shareholder value. Portfolio Growth Elemental continues to advance a disciplined growth strategy focused on building a globally diversified portfolio of high-quality royalty and streaming interests, with a primary emphasis on gold and precious metals. The portfolio combines cash-generating, producing assets, including cornerstone royalties such as Karlawinda, with a material growth pipeline through exposure to development-stage assets such as Laverton and Dugbe. This precious metals focus is complemented by selective exposure to large-scale base metals operations including Caserones and Timok, which provide diversification across commodities, jurisdictions, and operators. Together these assets underpin a growing and diversified revenue base, while offering substantial long-term upside through production growth, exploration success and mine life extensions. During the third quarter, Elemental completed the acquisition of Vizsla representing a significant advancement of this precious metals strategy. The transaction added a substantial royalty interest over the high-grade Panuco silver-gold project in Mexico, further increasing the scale and quality of Elemental’s portfolio and enhancing its exposure to silver. Panuco represents a cornerstone development-stage asset with the potential to generate meaningful future royalty revenue as the project advances towards production, while also providing substantial exploration and mine life extension optionality. In September 2026, Elemental announced the acquisition of a portfolio of five precious metals streams and royalties from Orion Mine Finance for total consideration of $290.0 million. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed the acquisition of four of the five assets, with the remaining asset expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. The acquisition further expands the Company's portfolio of cash-generating assets, increases its precious metals exposure and provides additional long-term growth potential. Near to medium-term portfolio growth is expected to be supported by continued development, optimization, and exploration activities at several key assets. In addition, recent exploration success in the broader Timok district, including the Malka Golaja discovery, highlights the prospectivity of the regional land package and reinforces the long-term optionality of Elemental’s royalty interest. Vizsla Royalties Acquisition On September 14, 2026, the Company completed its previously announced acquisition of all issued and outstanding common shares of Vizsla Royalties Corp. by way of a court-approved plan of arrangement. Under the terms of the transaction, the Company paid aggregate cash consideration of approximately C$82 million and issued 8,105,440 common shares to former Vizsla shareholders, representing approximately 11.2% of the Company's issued and outstanding common shares immediately following closing. To partially fund the cash consideration, the Company drew $30.0 million from its revolving credit facility in connection with the acquisition. The royalties are uncapped and have no buyback or step-down provisions, providing significant long-term exposure to future production and exploration potential. Risk and Capital Management: Financial Instruments Fair Value of Financial Instruments Financial instruments measured at fair value are classified into one of three levels in the fair value hierarchy based on the degree to which the inputs used to determine the fair value are observable. The three levels of the fair value hierarchy are: Level 1 – Unadjusted quoted prices at the measurement date for identical assets or liabilities in active markets. Level 2 – Observable inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1, such as quoted prices for similar assets and liabilities in active markets; quoted prices for identical or similar assets and liabilities in markets that are not active; or other inputs that are observable or can be corroborated by observable market data. Level 3 – Unobservable inputs which are supported by little or no market activity.
