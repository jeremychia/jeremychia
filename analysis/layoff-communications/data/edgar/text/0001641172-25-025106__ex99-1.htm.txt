Beneficial Owner If, on the Record Date, your Ordinary Shares were held in an account at a brokerage firm or at a bank or other nominee holder, you are considered the beneficial owner of Ordinary Shares held “in street name,” and these proxy materials are being forwarded to you by your broker or nominee who is considered the shareholder of record for purposes of voting at the Meeting.
