Operating earnings per share is defined as "Earnings per common share - diluted" excluding the after-tax impact of significant items. Operating EBIT is defined as earnings (i.e., "Income before income taxes") before interest, excluding the impact of significant items. Operating EBIT margin is defined as Operating EBIT as a percentage of net sales. Operating EBITDA is defined as earnings (i.e., "Income before income taxes") before interest, depreciation and amortization, excluding the impact of significant items. Free cash flow is defined as "Cash provided by (used for) operating activities - continuing operations," less capital expenditures. Under this definition, free cash flow represents the cash generated by the Company from operations after investing in its asset base. Free cash flow, combined with cash balances and other sources of liquidity, represent the cash available to fund obligations and provide returns to shareholders. Free cash flow is an integral financial measure used in the Company's financial planning process. Cash flow conversion is defined as "Cash provided by (used for) operating activities - continuing operations," divided by Operating EBITDA.
