On September 12, 2024 , at approximately 11:45 a.m. Eastern Time, Dow Inc. (“Dow”) will participate in the 12 th Annual Morgan Stanley Laguna Conference (“Conference”). During the Conference, Dow will provide an update on the macroeconomic environment, industry dynamics and Dow’s earnings guidance for the third quarter of 2024. Dow issued a press release and presentation relating to the update, attached hereto as Exhibits 99.1 and 99.2, respectively, and incorporated herein by reference.
