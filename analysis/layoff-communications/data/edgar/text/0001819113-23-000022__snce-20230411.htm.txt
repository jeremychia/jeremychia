Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. On April 11, 2023, the Company committed to and commenced a cost reduction program. The cost reduction program included a reduction in force affecting approximately 140 employees (representing approximately 30% of total employees prior to these actions). The Company’s Board of Directors approved the program on March 30, 2023 and the majority of the affected employees were informed of the reduction in force on April 11, 2023. The Company expects the majority of the reduction in force to be completed by the end of the second quarter of 2023. The Company expects that the cost reduction program will generate annual gross cash savings of approximately $24 million; however the Company expects to only recognize a range of $8 million to $10 million of such savings incremental to previously issued 2023 financial guidance. Total cash expenditures for the cost reduction program are estimated to be approximately $3.7 million, substantially all of which are expected to be related to employee severance costs. The Company expects to recognize most of these pre-tax reduction in force charges in the second quarter of 2023. The Company’s estimates are subject to a number of assumptions, and actual results may differ. The Company may also incur additional costs not currently contemplated due to events that may occur as a result of, or that are associated with, the cost reduction program.
