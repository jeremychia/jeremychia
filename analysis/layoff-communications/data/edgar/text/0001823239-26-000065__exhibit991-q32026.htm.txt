(Maravai) (NASDAQ: MRVI), a global provider of life science reagents and services to researchers and biotech innovators, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, together with other business updates. Key Financial Results: • Quarterly revenue of $45.9 million, Net loss of $(24.0) million, and Adjusted EBITDA of $5.8 million; • Full year 2026 revenue guidance range increased to $212.0 million to $216.0 million; Adjusted EBITDA guidance raised to $40.0 million to $42.0 million. “Our third- quarter results build on the momentum we established in the first half of the year,” said Bernd Brust, CEO of Maravai LifeSciences. “TriLink revenue grew 12% year-over-year led by 28% revenue growth for our mRNA products, while Cygnus delivered its sixth consecutive quarter of year-over-year growth, with revenue up 8%.” Brust continued, “Four Discovery mRNA customers transitioned to clinical-stage GMP this quarter, reinforcing our belief that today’s Discovery success will create tomorrow’s GMP opportunity. Based on our third-quarter performance and our expectations for the fourth quarter, we are raising our full-year revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance. We remain focused on driving sustained, profitable growth and creating long-term value for our shareholders.” Revenue for the Third Quarter 2026 Three Months Ended September 30, (Dollars in 000’s) 2026 2025 Year-over-Year % Change TriLink $ 28,350 $ 25,353 11.8 % Cygnus 17,501 16,277 7.5 % Total Revenue $ 45,851 $ 41,630 10.1 % Revenue for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Nine Months Ended September 30, (Dollars in 000’s) 2026 2025 Year-over-Year % Change TriLink $ 110,495 $ 85,188 29.7 % Cygnus 52,635 50,689 3.8 % Total Revenue $ 163,130 $ 135,877 20.1 % Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results by Reporting Segment Revenue for the third quarter was $45.9 million, an increase of 10.1% compared to the prior year period, driven by the following: • TriLink revenue increased 11.8% year-over-year, primarily driven by increased demand for Discovery mRNA and GMP consumables, as well as strength from our CDMO business. • Cygnus revenue increased 7.5% year-over-year, driven by increased demand for Host Cell Protein (HCP) and ELISA kits. Net loss and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) were $(24.0) million and $5.8 million, respectively, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net loss and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) of $(45.1) million and $(10.8) million, respectively, for the third quarter of 2025. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results by Reporting Segment Revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased 20.1% compared to the prior year period, driven by the following: • TriLink revenue increased 29.7% year-over-year, primarily driven by $14.3 million of high-volume CleanCap orders for commercial phase COVID vaccine programs in Q1 2026. Excluding COVID CleanCap revenue, TriLink base revenue grew 12.9% year-over-year with increased demand for Discovery mRNA and GMP consumables. • Cygnus revenue increased 3.8% year-over-year, driven by demand for HCP and ELISA kits. Net loss and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) were $(52.0) million and $34.8 million, respectively, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net loss and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) of $(167.7) million and $(31.7) million, respectively, for the same period in the prior year. Updated Financial Guidance for Full Year 2026 Maravai’s financial guidance for the full year 2026 is based on expectations for its existing business and does not include the financial impact of potential new acquisitions, if any, or items that have not yet been identified or quantified. This guidance is also subject to a number of risks, uncertainties and other factors, including those identified in “Forward-looking Statements” below. Revenue for the full year 2026 is now expected to be in the range of $212.0 million to $216.0 million, up from the prior range of $205.0 million to $215.0 million. Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) is now expected to be in the range of $40.0 million to $42.0 million, up from the prior range of $33.0 million to $35.0 million. Non-GAAP Financial Information This press release contains financial measures that have not been calculated in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the U.S. (GAAP). These non-GAAP measures include: Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted fully diluted Earnings Per Share (EPS). Maravai defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, certain non-cash items and other adjustments that we do not consider representative of our ongoing operating performance from period to period. Maravai defines Adjusted Net Income (Loss) as tax-effected earnings before the adjustments described above, and the tax effects of those adjustments. Maravai defines Adjusted fully diluted EPS as Adjusted Net Income (Loss) divided by the diluted weighted average number of shares of Class A common stock outstanding for the applicable period, which assumes the proforma exchange of all outstanding units of Maravai Topco Holdings, LLC (paired with shares of Class B common stock) for shares of Class A common stock. These non-GAAP measures are supplemental measures of operating performance that are not prepared in accordance with GAAP and do not represent, and should not be considered as, an alternative to net loss, as determined in accordance with GAAP. Management uses these non-GAAP measures to understand and evaluate Maravai’s core operating performance and trends, and to develop short-term and long-term operating plans. Management believes the measures facilitate comparison of Maravai’s operating performance on a consistent basis between periods and, when viewed in combination with its results prepared in accordance with GAAP, help provide a broader picture of factors and trends affecting Maravai’s results of operations. These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider them in isolation, or as a substitute for analysis of Maravai’s results as reported under GAAP. Because of these limitations, they should not be considered as a replacement for net loss, as determined by GAAP, or as a measure of Maravai’s profitability. Management compensates for these limitations by relying primarily on Maravai’s GAAP results and using non-GAAP measures only for supplemental purposes. The non-GAAP financial measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.
