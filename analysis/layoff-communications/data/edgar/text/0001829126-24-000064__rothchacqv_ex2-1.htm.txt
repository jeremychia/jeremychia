Roth is a blank check company formed for the purpose of effecting a merger, share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities; Merger Sub is a wholly owned direct subsidiary of Roth and was formed for the sole purpose of entering into a merger.
