shares with registration number 435166 (“ PubCo ”), CSLM Digital Asset Acquisition Corp III, Ltd, a Cayman Islands exempted company limited by shares with registration number 412434 (“ Acquiror ”), RECITALS WHEREAS , Acquiror is a blank check company incorporated as a Cayman Islands exempted company limited by shares and incorporated for the purpose of effecting a merger, share exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, recapitalization, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses; WHEREAS , Acquiror has established PubCo in the Cayman Islands, as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Acquiror, for the purpose of effectuating the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (inclusive of the Reincorporation Merger), with the objective of acting as the publicly traded holding company for its Subsidiaries; WHEREAS , Acquiror will establish a Cayman Islands exempted company limited by shares (“ Merger Sub ”), as a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of Acquiror, for the purpose of effectuating the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (inclusive of the Acquisition Merger); WHEREAS , the Parties hereto desire and intend to effect a business combination transaction whereby (a) at least one (1) Business Day prior to the Reincorporation Merger Closing Date (as defined below), the Company will (i) re-domicile from Gibraltar to the Cayman Islands, and (ii) transfer by way of continuation as an exempted company limited by shares in the Cayman Islands in accordance with the Companies Act 2014 of Gibraltar and Companies (Re-Domiciliation) Regulations 1996 of Gibraltar (the “ Gibraltar Companies Act ”) and the Companies Act (2026 Revision) of the Cayman Islands (the “ Cayman Companies Act ”) (as applicable) (the “ Re-domiciliation and Continuation ”), (b) at least one (1) Business Day after the consummation of the Re-domiciliation and Continuation, Acquiror will merge with and into PubCo (the “ Reincorporation Merger ”), with PubCo being the surviving entity (for the periods from and after the Reincorporation Merger Effective Time (as defined below), PubCo may also be referred to as the “ Surviving Acquiror ”), and Merger Sub becoming a wholly-owned subsidiary of PubCo, and (c) at least one (1) Business Day after the completion of both the Re-domiciliation and Continuation and the Reincorporation Merger and within three (3) Business Days after the completion of the Reincorporation Merger, Merger Sub will merge with and into the Company (the “ Acquisition Merger ” and, together with the Reincorporation Merger, the “ Mergers ”), with the Company being the surviving entity and becoming a wholly-owned Subsidiary of PubCo (for the periods from and after the Acquisition Merger Effective Time (as defined below), the Company may also be referred to as the “ Surviving Company ”), with each of the Mergers to occur upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement and in accordance with the Cayman Companies Act; WHEREAS , upon the Reincorporation Merger Effective Time, (i) all of the issued and outstanding securities of Acquiror immediately prior to the Reincorporation Merger Effective Time shall no longer be issued and outstanding and shall automatically be cancelled, in exchange for the right of the holders thereof to receive certain securities of PubCo as determined in accordance with this Agreement, and (ii) each share of PubCo issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the Reincorporation Merger Effective Time shall no longer be issued and outstanding and shall automatically be cancelled, in each case, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement and in accordance with the provisions of the Cayman Companies Act and other applicable laws; WHEREAS , upon the Acquisition Merger Effective Time, (i) each Company Exchanging Share shall automatically be cancelled and converted into, and shall thereafter represent the right of each holder of the Company Exchanging Shares to receive, such number of newly issued PubCo Ordinary Shares as determined in accordance with this Agreement, and (ii) each share of Merger Sub issued and outstanding as of immediately prior to the Acquisition Merger Effective Time shall automatically be converted into one (1) Surviving Company Share and accordingly, the Surviving Company shall become a wholly-owned subsidiary of PubCo;
