Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities On August 11, 2022, the Company committed to a second workforce reduction plan (the “August Plan”) in addition to the initial workforce reduction plan disclosed on April 18, 2022. The August Plan is part of an extensive review to manage the Company’s cash consumption in alignment with market conditions, as previously disclosed. The focus of the August Plan is on streamlining the Company’s title and general and administrative operations. The August Plan includes the elimination of approximately 220 positions across the Company, or approximately 11% of the Company’s current workforce. The eliminated positions include approximately 140 current employees and approximately 80 vacancies. The Company estimates that it will incur approximately $4.0 million in charges in connection with the August Plan, consisting of cash expenditures for severance payments, employee benefits, payroll taxes and related facilitation costs. The Company expects that execution of the August Plan, including cash payments, will be substantially complete in the third quarter of 2022. The eliminated positions represent annualized compensation expense of approximately $27.3 million. The Company may incur other charges or cash expenditures not currently contemplated due to unanticipated events that may occur as a result of or in connection with the implementation of the August Plan. The Company intends to exclude the charges associated with the August Plan from its non-GAAP financial measures, including non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP operating expenses, non-GAAP loss from operations, and non-GAAP net loss.
