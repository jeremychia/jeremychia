(iii) Performance Participation. The Performance Earn-Out for each Earn-Out Year shall equal the Applicable Percentage of Gross Profit in excess of the applicable Baseline Carve-Out Amount. (v) Payment Mix. Any Performance Earn-Out payment earned for an Earn-Out Year shall be paid fifty percent (50%) in cash and fifty percent (50%) in shares of Purchaser Common Stock (the “Earn-Out Shares”) (or such other equity securities issued in substitution or conversion thereof), subject to applicable securities laws. (vi) Calculation and Payment.
