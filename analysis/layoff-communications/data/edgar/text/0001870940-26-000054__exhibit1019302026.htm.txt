RECITALS The Borrowers have asked the Lenders to extend credit to the Borrowers consisting of (a) an initial term loan in the aggregate principal amount of $39,000,000 and (b) a revolving credit facility in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $5,000,000 at any time outstanding. The Lenders are severally, and not jointly, willing to extend such credit to the Borrowers subject to the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth.
