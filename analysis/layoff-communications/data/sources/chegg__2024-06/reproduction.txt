 Chegg layoffs to impact hundreds of employees

(KRON) — Bay Area tech company Chegg, Inc. announced plans to lay off 23% of its global workforce this week. In a letter to shareholders, Chegg President and CEO Nathan Schultz announced the sweeping job cuts as part of restructuring effort.
Chegg, which provides AI-driven study assistance for students, is based in Santa Clara. The layoffs will impact around 440 workers, nearly a quarter of the company’s workforce.

“I am committed to our Student First mission and confident that we can achieve remarkable results serving learners around the world,” wrote Schultz in the letter. “Today, as we enter a new chapter for Chegg, we executed a major step in my plans to refocus the company through a restructuring effort, which included a reduction of our global workforce by 23%.”
Schultz went on to tell shareholders that Chegg had “reorganized our teams, streamlined our decision-making processes, and are actively simplifying our systems and platforms.” The company, Schultz said, could save between $40 and $50 million in 2025 as a result of the job cuts.
Chegg stock
Chegg stock shot up earlier this week following news of the layoffs. It has since dropped back down to where it was late last week.
