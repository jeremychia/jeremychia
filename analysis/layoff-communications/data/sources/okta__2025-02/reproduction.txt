Okta lays off 180 employees — nearly one year after last workforce reduction Mary Ann Azevedo 9:51 AM PST · February 4, 2025 U.S. access and identity management giant Okta laid off 180 employees on Tuesday, the company confirmed to TechCrunch — just over one year after it let go of 400 workers.

The company separately conducted a workforce reduction in February of 2023 that affected 300 employees.

While Okta did not share how many employees it has after its latest round of layoffs, doing the math, it had around 5,300 employees at this time in 2024. According to an SEC filing, Okta’s new cuts affected 3% of its workforce.

In a written statement, a company spokesperson said: “Okta employees were notified today that we are eliminating approximately 180 roles to reallocate resources to new growth areas. We thank our outgoing colleagues for all they contributed to Okta and are committed to providing support and resources to help through this transition.”

In December, the company reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of $665 million, up 14% from the prior year third quarter.
