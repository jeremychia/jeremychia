## What to watch for when applying

**1. The "internship is his most recent role" problem is his single biggest risk.** Every ATS and every human screener reads top-to-bottom. The resume opens with Deezer (an internship, Aug 2024–Jan 2025) before Swiggy (a senior title with real scope). That ordering silently downgrades him from "6-year commercial analyst" to "recent grad with an internship." He needs to control this narrative explicitly, not hope the reader does the math themselves.

**2. He's claiming tools he can't back up.** dbt, Airflow, Amplitude sit in the skills line with zero supporting bullets. adjoe/DISH won't probe this (Tableau/SQL shops), but Voodoo explicitly requires "solid experience with dbt" — that's a first-screen question he can't dodge. Before applying to Voodoo specifically, he should decide: either find a real story (thesis work, Deezer, a personal project) or quietly drop dbt from that application's skill list. Claiming it and then failing the follow-up question is worse than not claiming it.

**3. Visa/relocation posture is currently invisible.** Three of four roles are in Germany; one requires no relocation (Voodoo, Paris). Nothing on the resume states his work authorization. Recruiters filter this early, especially at companies less generous than adjoe (which explicitly offers visa support). He should have one line ready — e.g. "EU work authorization via France; open to relocation" or the true equivalent — for cover letters and first calls, since it's not really a resume-body fact.

**4. Fashion/marketplace domain gap at Tradebyte is real, not a framing problem.** No resume edit fixes "deep understanding of fashion brand monetization." If he applies there, the interview will test genuine domain knowledge (GMV, sell-through, margin) that he'll need to have read up on beforehand — this one needs prep, not polish.

**5. Tableau vs. Power BI.** Three of four JDs name Tableau specifically ("advanced Tableau" at DISH). His evidenced dashboard work is Power BI. Not disqualifying, but he should be ready to say plainly "my dashboard depth is in Power BI, concepts transfer, here's how fast I'd ramp" rather than let the resume imply Tableau fluency it can't back.

## Resume edits to accentuate his strengths

Working from his actual bullets — reordering and adding a summary, not inventing facts.

**Add a 3-line summary at the top**, before Experience. The single highest-leverage fix — it's currently missing entirely, so nothing frames the 9M EUR / 21% / 28% numbers before the reader hits them cold in bullet 6.

> 6+ years driving commercial analytics impact — 9M EUR annual savings and 3.2X signup growth at Axis Bank, 21% WoW order lift at Swiggy. MSc Big Data & Business Analytics (ESCP Paris/Berlin) sharpening ML and AI-driven analysis on top of hands-on SQL, Python, and dashboard delivery at scale.

This does three things: states "6+ years" before any date confuses the reader, leads with his two best numbers, and reframes the MSc as an addition to seniority rather than a reset to junior.

**Neutralise the Deezer-first ordering.** Keep strict reverse-chronological order (standard and ATS-safe), but ensure the summary line has already told the reader "6+ years" so Deezer reads as "recent EU-market experience," not "his career starting point."

**Pull his best quantified lines into stronger position within each role.** Axis Bank leads with the 9M EUR / 3.2X line — good, keep. Swiggy leads with the 21% WoW lift — good, keep. For EXL, the 23M EUR/month compliance-flagging line is currently second; it's a stronger opener than the topic-segmentation bullet for adjoe/DISH (both heavily "commercial impact" cultures) — swap those two.

**Tier the skills list instead of a flat line.** A flat `SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, dbt, Amplitude, Airflow...` lets a reader weight dbt/Airflow equal to SQL/Python. Split:

> **Core:** SQL, Python, Power BI, R
> **Working knowledge:** Tableau, dbt, Airflow, Amplitude, n8n

Honest, still ATS-parseable (keywords present), and pre-empts the "claimed dbt and couldn't answer" failure mode — a recruiter reads "working knowledge" as "ask a light question, not a deep one."

**Per-role bullet reordering:**
- **Voodoo:** front-load the Swiggy "instrumented app events, created fact tables, validated data pipelines" bullet — currently bullet 2 of 2, should be bullet 1; it's his closest analog to their "SDK diagnostic events / Ad Experience datasets" ask.
