## Problem
about 44k invoice lines a month carry VAT country `GB` instead of `UK`. finance files and reviews `GB` as a separate country from `UK`, and it slips past checks written against `UK`. three places in billing take a country code without converting it from ISO:

| path | lines (august 2026) | cause |
