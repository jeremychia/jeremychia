- **IC3, craft mastery:** tackles most challenges independently from start to finish while accounting for the full extent of the business context of the work
- **IC4, technical lead:** fully responsible for identifying, prioritising, and systematically resolving area / domain level analytics engineering challenges
- **IC4 note:** IC4 is a fundamentally different role for which a business need must exist before a promotion can be considered. Not everyone is expected to be able to grow to meet the expectations of an IC4 role

## Scope & Complexity

### Theory & techniques

- **IC3:** I can independently design and implement complex data architectures at scale in a reasonable timeframe (including e.g. complex data models, kappa vs lambda architectures, orchestration, and integration across internal & external systems). I keep a strong emphasis on data quality, privacy, performance, and costs. I have sufficiently mastered these techniques to explain them to anyone.
- **IC4:** I know the best practices, theory, and techniques for designing and implementing data architectures. I enable Analytics Engineers in the area / domain to design and implement the most complex data architectures

### Engineering

- **IC3:** I consistently write code whose structure and purpose is immediately obvious to anyone and whose quality is an inspiration for those I work with. I have a strong intuition on implementation and computational quirks and how they can be addressed in my areas of expertise. For example, I can use a query plan to optimise a query, including explaining the different join algorithms, and understanding how underlying data is stored.
- **IC4:** I proactively set and guard best-practices within the department and enable the Analytics Engineers in the area / domain to write high quality code

### Quality standards and procedures (e.g. documentation, reproducibility, maintainability, privacy)

- **IC3:** I help others meet our quality standards
- **IC4:** I proactively identify opportunities to improve quality standards and procedures and enable the Analytics Engineers in my scope to meet our standards

### Data mastery

- **IC3:** I fully understand every aspect of the data within my area throughout the entire lifecycle: from source data and ingestion through transformation (including CI/CD), to actual business usage (e.g., nuances of key business metrics). I maintain an up-to-date knowledge of Vinted’s data stack and architecture.
- **IC4:** I fully understand every aspect of Vinted's data stack and architecture

### Impact measurement

- **IC3:** I can fully own the metrics reflecting the accuracy, performance, and impact of my work, and proactively propose new metrics. I ensure that metrics are defined such that their implications are obvious to all key stakeholders
- **IC4:** I enable the Analytics Engineers in area / domain to fully own the metrics reflecting the accuracy, performance, and impact of their work, and to proactively propose new metrics. I enable them to arrive at metrics that are defined such that their implications are obvious to all key stakeholders

### Structuring

- **IC3:** I holistically consider how the various pieces of complex problems related to the topics that I work on could be best tackled in an iterative and systematic way
- **IC4:** I enable the Analytics Engineers in the area / domain to holistically consider how the various pieces of complex problems related to the topics that they work on could be best tackled in an iterative and systematic way

### Strategy & roadmap

- **IC3:** I proactively bring in valuable input for the strategy and roadmap of my team / area and facilitate discussions related to analytics engineering
- **IC4:** I am a strategic expert on my area / domain and a proactive contributor to its strategy with respect to analytics engineering in the domain

### Scope of ownership

- **IC3:** I can independently resolve complex new problems from start to finish. From engaging stakeholders and clarifying data needs, to delivering solutions that are reliable, performant and easy to use – I make sure it happens without requiring support even when no established approach exists.
- **IC4:** I own the long-term quality of Analytics Engineers output and their literacy within the area / domain. I serve as advisor, supporter, and collaborator for the Analytics Engineering Staff (IC5)

### Technical and scientific debt

- **IC3:** I proactively identify technical and scientific debt related to my area of expertise that has been incurred by my team
- **IC4:** I ensure the Analytics Engineers in the area / domain systematically make a conscious decision whether to pay down or incur debt related to their area of expertise

## Decision Making & Judgement

### Business understanding

- **IC3:** I understand the business dynamics of my area / domain well enough to anticipate on future changes in data users' needs—and act accordingly
- **IC4:** I am the expert within Vinted on the business dynamics of my team's scope to the extent that they affect our current and future data needs. I have a good understanding of the workings of Vinted and can effortlessly switch between a helicopter view and the problems at hand within the context of my work

### Prioritisation

- **IC3:** I independently set my priorities while also accounting for the area / domain objectives. I systematically consider the possibility of cutting scope in light of the opportunity cost of my time investment
- **IC4:** I prioritise the most impactful and systemic problems in my scope to improve the long-term quality of Analytics Engineering there

### Time management

- **IC3:** I have a strong understanding of the uncertainty involved in the delivery time of my commitments and proactively manage the expectations of those that may be affected by it
- **IC4:** I convey accurate expectations about delivery times of tools/processes/techniques related to my assigned scope

## Alignment & Collaboration

### Collaboration

- **IC3:** I am able to proactively drive cross-functional collaboration within the scope of my work
- **IC4:** I consistently drive cross-department and cross-domain collaborations when and where it leads to improving quality of delivery of Analytics Engineers in my scope

### Communication

- **IC3:** I clearly and persuasively communicate the most complex aspects of my work to any stakeholder in a way that makes their implications obvious (e.g. how the assumptions made in the processing of data may affect the conclusions that a business owner could draw from them)
- **IC4:** I am a skilled communicator who creates minimal additional communication overhead in my scope. I effectively initiate and lead discussions around complex projects and influence decision-making at the company-level, while integrating diverse perspectives

### Challenging

- **IC3:** I systematically encourage people to challenge my ideas, and create a safe environment for them to do so *(same as the level below)*
- **IC4:** I serve as a role model for others in terms of ability to encourage people to challenge my ideas. I actively yet constructively create a safe environment for other people to challenge each other's ideas

## Scaling Capabilities

### Knowledge leadership

- **IC3:** I proactively initiate knowledge sharing within my area / domain and with the relevant guild(s)
- **IC4:** I proactively create an environment of excellence in knowledge leadership, where people feel inspired to aim higher in their areas of interest and expertise and feel safe to share their knowledge within the area / domain. Above all, I lead by example

### Helping others

- **IC3:** I pull my weight when it comes to tasks that are not directly related to my deliverables, but are important to the capability function (e.g. recruitment, answering questions of colleagues on Slack, etc.) to the best of my current capabilities *(same as the level below)*
- **IC4:** *same as IC3*

### Feedback

- **IC3:** I systematically give constructive feedback
- **IC4:** I stimulate a feedback culture within my scope and the wider organization

### Learning

- **IC3:** I am self-aware of my long-term career path and steer my personal development accordingly. To ensure continued learning, I create a circle of peers to learn from around me and consistently get out of my comfort zone
- **IC4:** I stimulate a culture of continuous learning and growth throughout the capability function and the wider organization. I proactively identify opportunities for Analytics Engineers within my scope to grow

### Attitude

- **IC3:** I keep a constructive attitude in more difficult times. I feel responsible for the mood within my primary team—and act accordingly
- **IC4:** My attitude is an inspiration for others. I feel responsible for the mood within my scope—and act accordingly

### Caring

- **IC3:** I truly care for my colleagues. I speak out and look out for their wellbeing. I systematically consider the impact that my actions have on others on a personal level. I help build a diverse company *(same as the level below)*
- **IC4:** *same as IC3*
