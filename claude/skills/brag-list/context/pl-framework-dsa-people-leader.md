## Area of ownership

- **PL3, team lead:** Team—comfortably leads a single Team with scope of limited complexity. May have a significant hands-on component*. May need limited help to define and evolve team structure over time
- **PL4, manager:** Team or Area / Domain—comfortably leads a large Team with a more complex scope and possibly 1-2 subteams or a relatively small Area / Domain. May do some hands-on work*. Defines and evolves team structure over time, can execute structural changes independently
- **PL5, director:** Area / Domain or Function—co-accountable for smooth running of a large Area / Domain or Function. Leads multiple teams. No hands-on component. Comfortably adapts structure to changing business needs and environment

## Scope & Complexity

### Accountability for planning timespan

- **PL3:** Operational: Within my area of ownership I focus on day to day activities (3-6 months)
- **PL4:** Tactical: Within my area of ownership I focus on turning the strategy into actionable plans (<12 months)

### Vision & strategy

- **PL3:** My area of ownership has an ambituous yet realistic vision, strategy, and roadmap that contributes to Vinted's mission.
I contribute to the vision, strategy, and roadmap of my value creation organization
- **PL4:** I contribute to the vision, strategy, and roadmap of the Capability Function. I co-create the vision, strategy, and roadmap of my value creation organization

### Theory & techniques

- **PL3:** I have sufficient mastery of the theory & techniques that are used throughout my area of ownership or know who can provide that knowledge to support my reports in their day to day work. I can help get my reports unblocked in any situation - either by helping myself or by pointing this person into the right direction
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### DSA quality standards

- **PL3:** The deliverables within my assigned scope meet DSA's high quality standards, while always balancing effort and impact

When the opportunity arises, I proactively contribute to improving tools, processes or quality standards within the Area / Domain or Capability Function. Before I do, I am able to manage potential conflicting priorities and bring clarity to my team
- **PL4:** I drive the implementation of the improvements of tools, processes or quality standards within the Function or Capability Function when the opportunity arises and my priorities allow it. I am constantly researching industry best-practices and quality standards within my assigned scope

### Leading projects

- **PL3:** I work on projects/problems of diverse scope and can advise my team members on difficult matters. I may lead projects across Functions. I resolve most complex problems and issues in efficient ways within my area of ownership within the set timelines while maintaining our high quality standards
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

## Decision Making & Judgement

### Decision making

- **PL3:** I am comfortable making decisions within my area of ownership and may contribute to decision making beyond my scope. I clearly lay out arguments, counterarguments, and final rationale behind decisions
- **PL4:** I consistently zoom out to consider the wider implications.

### Risk identification & balancing

- **PL3:** I have a good understanding of the risk-reward trade-offs within my area of ownership and make conscious decisions based on that
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### Business understanding

- **PL3:** I can be an equal sparring partner with anyone on the business / product dynamics that are relevant to my area of ownership. I have a good understanding of Vinted ways of working (business model, long term strategy and plans etc.)
- **PL4:** I am one of the experts on the business / product dynamics related to my area of ownership within the company. I can effortlessly switch between a helicopter view and the problems at hand

### Prioritisation & time management

- **PL3:** I understand the importance and impact of delegation, involvement and prioritization. I make sure people in my area of ownership work on the right things for the right amount of time to achieve maximum impact while keeping a healthy work life balance
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

## Alignment & Collaboration

### Communication

- **PL3:** When circumstances change and it impacts project's timelines and commitments - I proactively act to bring clarity to relevant stakeholders on what they can actually expect. I am able to influence decisions related to my area of ownership. I maintain consistent, transparent, and empathetic communication with my direct reports.
- **PL4:** I communicate complex ideas skillfully and with nuance, adapting style to different audiences. 

### Collaboration

- **PL3:** I create an alignment within my area of ownership and relevant stakeholders. I involve the right stakeholders at the right time to prioritize and deliver results. I am seen as a constructive and productive colleague that always keeps the company goals in mind.  I establish clear processes to route and prioritise stakeholder requests, keeping in mind my team’s focus and wellbeing.

- **PL4:** I drive cross-function and cross-domain collaboration within the company. If applicable, my EPDA or other relevant peers see me as an equal sparring partner and contributor. I promote a culture of collaborative working within teams

### Procedures

- **PL3:** I continuously evaluate and suggest creative ways to improve tools and processes for collaboration within my area of ownership and with all relevant stakeholders
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### Challenging

- **PL3:** I take pause and critically evaluate any information, plan, decision taken and encourage others to do the same. I encourage others to challenge my ideas, and create a safe environment for people to do so
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

## Scaling Capabilities

### Team design

- **PL3:** With some support I ensure team design and hiring is optimal to reach the desired results on time. I consider diversity and inclusion, and team resilience when building my team. I ensure successful onboarding of new team members
- **PL4:** I am able to comfortably adapt the structure and composition of my area of ownership in face of changing business needs. I participate in cross-function hiring, share best practices and seek for improvement.  I anticipate how organisational design will affect the team’s wellbeing and resilience.


### Team performance

- **PL3:** I ensure my team’s performance is high and sustainable in the long run. Each member of my team understands their role and how they contribute to our team’s success. I notice underperformance of my team members on time and take needed measures to address that. I balance peak-demand intensity with periods of stability to ensure the long-term wellbeing of my team.

- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### Feedback

- **PL3:** I stimulate a feedback culture within my area of ownership by encouraging my team members to continuously gather feedback. I lead by example: provide regular, direct, and impactful feedback and seek out feedback from other team members to grow
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### Learning

- **PL3:** I am self-aware of my long-term career path and steer my personal development accordingly. I continuously get out of my comfort zone to ensure continued learning
- **PL4:** I find ways to spread learnings across the wider organization. I share best practices by building a network and interacting with similar managers outside of my organization

### People growth

- **PL3:** I contribute to the career development of my team members and support them with creating individual growth plans. I know what motivates them and where possible, I take individual needs/strengths into account when planning their work
- **PL4:** I proactively mentor my team members and spot career opportunities. I am able to adapt to the different seniority of my team members (situational leadership)

### Attitude

- **PL3:** I lead by example in following Vinted's vision and living our values and behaviors in every day work. I keep a constructive attitude even at difficult times. I am responsible for my team health's and act accordingly. I openly share my failures in order to create a culture of openness and innovation. My attitude is an inspiration for other people around me
- **PL4:** *same as PL3*

### Caring

- **PL3:** I am aware of the wellbeing situation in my team. I look out for the wellbeing of people within team and take action as needed, keeping in mind that different team members have different needs. I systematically consider the impact that my actions have on others on a personal level.
- **PL4:** I am aware of the wellbeing situation in my scope. I look out for the wellbeing of people within my scope and take action as needed, keeping in mind that different people have different needs. I see to it that the processes and policies that allow people to grow, be engaged and resilient, and feel included are followed in my scope.

### External engagement

- **PL3:** I understand the importance of employer brand.
I participate in external engagement / community building events
- **PL4:** I might participate in raising's Vinted's profile by engaging with external communities
