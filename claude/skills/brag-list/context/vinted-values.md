# Vinted values

Source: [Vinted Values](https://vinted.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/EKP/pages/29905420289/Vinted+Values) (V-Know space, Confluence page 29905420289).

## We aim high

Our mission is to make second-hand first choice worldwide, and "good enough" won't get us there.

- We know the price for high standards, and we choose not to compromise.
- We make smart decisions and take calculated risks based on our members' input, analytics and expertise.
- We take more time to analyse our failures than to acknowledge things that go well.

**Tag it when:** a problem was found before anyone reported it, a check was raised above what was asked, a root cause was chased instead of patched, or a decision was backed by data.

## We take ownership

Our sense of ownership gives us freedom while keeping us accountable for achieving our goals. We learn through our mistakes and are responsible for our outcomes.

- We choose to trust ourselves and each other.
- We don't micromanage, but help people claim ownership.
- We take ownership of our tasks, which makes us invested in our team's results and Vinted's success.

**Tag it when:** an incident was led end to end, a fix was carried into another team's code, or a mistake was owned and cleaned up.

## We co-create

We don't achieve results in isolation. We share accountability and empower everyone's unique skills and abilities.

- We communicate transparently and flatly, no matter the position or the team.
- We challenge each other, we share best practices, and we run with each other's ideas.
- We collaborate to solve urgent problems, but wider issues can take a lot of time and persuasion to resolve.
- We care to ask for the buy-in of everyone affected.

**Tag it when:** an RFC was put up for review, another team was warned about an upstream change, a practice was shared with the guild, or a peer's PR was reviewed.

## We care

When we can be fully ourselves, we work better.

- We are real.
- We take the time to build connections with each other.
- We care about each other through the good times and the bad.
- We're continually building an environment where we can be real and vulnerable.

**Tag it when:** a team event, a volunteering day, onboarding help or a well-being suggestion.

## We grow

We grow both personally and professionally. To achieve our mission, we must innovate: that means making mistakes, learning, and growing.

- We operate in the unknown, where change is inevitable.
- We adapt to the dynamic pace and changing priorities.
- We learn by experimenting and reflecting on our mistakes.
- We support each other by constantly giving and seeking honest and balanced feedback.
- We believe in limitless possibilities to learn and grow.

**Tag it when:** a new technique was learned and applied, a retro was written, feedback was given or asked for, or a hiring or community event was run.
