### Seniority Signals
The language is consistently **autonomous and directive**:
- "Set technical direction"
- "Define and evolve"
- "Lead the definition and implementation"
- "Drive adoption"
- "Mentor"

This is a **principal IC role**, not a manager position. They want someone who drives change through influence and expertise, not through authority.

**Resume framing:** Outcomes over tasks. "Designed layered dimensional models" beats "worked on modeling". "Established a risk-based control framework" beats "helped implement controls". Lead with decisions and design choices, not execution tasks.

### Loss Aversion Triggers
Multiple governance and reliability signals:
- "high-quality semantic assets"
- "long-term robustness"
- "data privacy, security, and compliance knowledge"
- Implicit: they've had data quality, compliance, or reliability incidents

**Resume framing:** Frame achievements as **risk reduction and reliability wins**. €1.6m recovery and 90% risk reduction are loss-aversion narratives—exactly what this hiring manager fears losing.
