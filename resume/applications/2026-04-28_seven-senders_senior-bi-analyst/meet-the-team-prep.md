**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Format:** Cultural fit — this is not a technical interview. They are deciding whether they want to work with you.

---

## What this meeting is

This is a culture-and-vibe round, not a skills assessment. The panel has already decided you can do the job. They are asking:

1. Would I enjoy working with this person?
2. Does this person understand what we do and care about it?
3. Will they contribute to the team dynamic, not just the work?

Your job: be genuinely curious, warm, and specific. Avoid monologuing. Ask each person something real. Make them feel you've thought about them individually.

## How to run the 45 minutes

**First 5 minutes:** Let them set the tone. Introductions will happen naturally. Be warm, make eye contact, use names immediately.

**Middle 30 minutes:** This will likely be a group conversation with some rotation. Don't try to have a deep 1:1 with each person — that's impossible in 45 minutes. Ask one good question per person when the opportunity is natural. Listen generously. Follow threads — if someone says something interesting, stay on it instead of moving to the next scripted question.

**Last 10 minutes:** They will likely ask if you have questions. If you haven't reached Dominik yet, use your question for him now. Close with energy — you want to be here.

---

## Messages to land (woven in naturally, not announced)

1. **You know their stack cold.** Snowflake, dbt, Looker — say it with familiarity, not formality. Reference the ELT approach if it comes up naturally.
2. **You've worked with data engineers, not above them.** Frame your Vinted experience as collaborative — you built things that made the DE team's life easier, you reviewed code with them, you didn't create upstream chaos.
3. **You're looking for a place to stay.** You are not in a job search — you are evaluating this team as the right place to invest the next chapter. That energy is different from desperation.
4. **You find the problem interesting.** Cross-border shipping at parcel granularity, carrier cost variance, delivery performance — this is genuinely interesting data. Let that show.

---

## Things to avoid

- Don't sell yourself. The interview is over — this is a relationship-building meeting.
- Don't over-talk. Two minutes is the ceiling for any single answer.
- Don't ask salary/start date questions. Wrong room.
- Don't ask the same question to every person — it reads as scripted.
- Don't ignore the quieter members to focus on Dominik or Mia.
