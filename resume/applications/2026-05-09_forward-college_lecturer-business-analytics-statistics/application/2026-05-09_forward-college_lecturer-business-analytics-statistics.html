Teaching Philosophy

The real obstacle in analytics education is rarely mathematical: students freeze not because they misunderstand a confidence interval or decision tree, but because they do not yet trust themselves to act on uncertain, quantified information. The goal of every course is a specific transformation — from 'I do not know how to approach this' to 'I know how to approach this' — and staying connected with students long enough to see that confidence carry forward. Courses are structured around frameworks and concepts rather than tools, so the foundation outlasts the next wave of software change. Direct instruction for deterministic content; inquiry-based seminars for applied, judgement-heavy questions where reasonable people working from the same data can legitimately reach different recommendations.
