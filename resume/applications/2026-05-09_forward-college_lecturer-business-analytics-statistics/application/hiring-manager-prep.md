## 7. Interview mechanics

- **This is a culture interview, not a technical screen.** Marco is a literature scholar. Do not volunteer regression notation, dbt, or BigQuery. Speak about teaching, students, and learning.
- **Match his register.** His email was warm, thoughtful, slightly formal. Reflect that back — not stiff, but considered.
- **Use "I" not "we"** — they are assessing individual ownership of the teaching relationship.
- **Concrete over general** — every time you are tempted to say "I believe in active learning," replace with a specific student moment or design decision.
- **Pause before answering** — 2–3 seconds signals confidence and prevents over-talking. This is a 15-minute call; density matters.
- **Acknowledge the gap honestly if it comes up.** Your cover letter already flagged that formal statistics teaching is shallower than your practitioner grounding. Do not backpedal from this — it signals self-awareness, which Marco will read positively.
- **Do not ask about salary** — that is covered in the JD (EUR 6.5–7.5k). Ask about the role, the students, and the institution.
