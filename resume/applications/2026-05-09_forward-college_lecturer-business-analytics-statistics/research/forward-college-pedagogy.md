# Forward College: Pedagogy & Student Experience

## The Core Premise

Forward College was founded in 2021 by Boris Walbaum, Céline Boisson, and Jeffrey Sampson on a single conviction: a prestigious diploma is necessary but not sufficient. The world is changing too fast for academic knowledge alone. Students need to graduate as better experts *and* better learners, better collaborators, and better citizens. ([University World News, Jan 2026](https://www.universityworldnews.com/post.php?story=20260106100036879))

Their answer is not a bolt-on careers programme. The pedagogy, the living arrangements, the three-city structure, and the personal development curriculum are all one integrated system — designed to develop four forms of intelligence simultaneously. ([forward-college.eu/our-vision](https://forward-college.eu/our-vision/))

- **Cognitive**: Critical thinking, problem-solving, technical knowledge
- **Social**: Understanding others, collaboration, intercultural communication
- **Emotional**: Self-awareness, resilience, stress management
- **Practical**: Adaptability, entrepreneurship, ethical conduct

## The Teaching Model

### No lectures. Active learning by design.

Forward College explicitly rejects the passive lecture format. Instead, every session is designed to make students do the intellectual work. ([forward-college.eu/learning-forward](https://forward-college.eu/learning-forward/))

- **Flipped classroom**: Students explore topics individually before class. Seminars are reserved for debate, simulation, and application — not content delivery.
- **Seminar-based**: Small groups of ~15 students (within cohorts of 150) tackle complex questions, simulate real-life scenarios, and challenge each other's reasoning.
- **Weekly one-on-one tutorials**: Oxbridge-style. 60–90 minutes. Individualised feedback, structured around that week's work. Designed to build self-evaluation and intellectual confidence progressively across three years.
- **Peer learning**: Group and team projects are built into the curriculum. The multicultural composition of cohorts — 40+ nationalities — is treated as a learning resource, not just a feature.

The underlying research base is explicit: the college draws from *"contemporary and research-proven teaching & learning practices across education, pedagogy, cognitive sciences and neurosciences."* ([forward-college.eu/learning-forward](https://forward-college.eu/learning-forward/))

### What a professor actually said

One faculty member, quoted in University World News (Jan 2026), described their relationship with students simply:

> "I know all the names, all the voices."

That's the benchmark. Every tutor is expected to know each student's intellectual personality — their strengths, their blindspots, how they argue, what they find hard. The institutional design makes this possible: 15 students per class, not 150.

A student reviewing the programme on EDUopinions confirmed this lived experience:

> "Professors that actually know us, are excited to teach and interact with us, and will always take the time to help you."

## The Three-City Structure (and Why It's Pedagogical)

Students move through three cities as a cohort — together, not individually. ([forward-college.eu/overview](https://forward-college.eu/overview/))

| Year | City | Developmental Focus |
|------|------|---------------------|
| 1 | Lisbon | Belonging, social intelligence, foundation |
| 2 | Paris | Resilience, emotional intelligence, stretch |
| 3 | Berlin | Independence, leadership, professional exposure |

This is not a study-abroad programme. The cities are chosen deliberately and sequenced intentionally:

- **Lisbon**: Warm, safe, high quality of life. Designed to ease transition to independent living while building cohort bonds. Students live communally. One student called it *"one of the most beautiful and welcoming cities"* they'd had the pleasure to live in. ([forward-college.eu/co-living](https://forward-college.eu/co-living-in-3-countries/))
- **Paris**: Demanding city, prestigious campus at Cité Internationale alongside 10,000+ international students. Requires adaptation and resilience — the difficulty is part of the design.
- **Berlin**: Creative, entrepreneurial, startup-dense. Students live independently and engage directly with the professional world through speakers, networking events, and companies. The city matches the final-year focus on leadership and professional formation.

The college is direct about what this achieves: *"Adaptability and intercultural communication are not personality traits; they are deep skills built through repeated exposure to the unfamiliar."* ([forward-college.eu/overview](https://forward-college.eu/overview/))

Moving together as a cohort means every new city is entered with established friendships. As the college frames it: the experience is *individual in how much you grow, but collective in how you live it.* ([forward-college.eu/co-living](https://forward-college.eu/co-living-in-3-countries/))

Zsanett (Class of 2025, now at HEC Paris) described exactly this:

> "I grew so much as a person through working on international projects with my peers, living independently in a foreign country and learning to juggle studies, work and free time." ([forward-college.eu/testimonials](https://forward-college.eu/testimonials/))

## The Personal Development Programme

This runs in parallel with academic study across all three years — structured, assessed, and coached. ([forward-college.eu/certificates/personal-development](https://forward-college.eu/certificates/personal-development/))

### Year 1 — Social Intelligence
- MBTI profiling to understand personality and working styles
- Networking and teamwork skills
- Cohabitation in intercultural environments
- Group dynamics, giving and receiving feedback
- Understanding how cultural background shapes social interaction

### Year 2 — Emotional Intelligence
- Self-awareness and mindfulness
- Regulating motivation
- Building courage and resilience
- Stress management and personal balance
- Wise decision-making

### Year 3 — Leadership
- Decision-making and strategic thinking
- Designing your post-Forward trajectory
- Negotiation and influence
- Accountability and autonomy

Each module follows a four-week cycle: define personal objectives → group workshops and practical activities → weekly habit-building → review session. Students complete annual 360-degree assessments gathering feedback from peers, teachers, and colleagues, plus two 45-minute coaching sessions per year to interpret results and build individualised growth plans. ([forward-college.eu/certificates/personal-development](https://forward-college.eu/certificates/personal-development/))

Myriam (Class of 2024, now at Sciences Po reading MSc International Security) described what this structure gave her:

> "Forward provided me with a space where I could safely experiment, make mistakes, and push myself beyond my academic limits." ([forward-college.eu/testimonials](https://forward-college.eu/testimonials/))

## What Relationship Means Here

The founders deliberately limit growth to preserve human interaction. Transparent communication structures mean students can access leadership directly. The institutional design is anti-scale by choice. ([University World News, Jan 2026](https://www.universityworldnews.com/post.php?story=20260106100036879))

One faculty member's framing, reported in University World News, captures the institutional bet:

> "Human connection is what universities can offer that AI cannot."

This is not a marketing line — it's a structural commitment. The co-living model, the small cohorts, the one-on-one tutorials, the named coaching — all of it exists to make relationships the engine of learning. As the college puts it on their overview page: *"Relationships are the necessary fuel for deep learning."* ([forward-college.eu/overview](https://forward-college.eu/overview/))

Yohana (Class of 2024, now in HR Learning at L'Oréal) put it plainly:

> "I knew I wouldn't be treated merely as a number and would be given the opportunity to shape my bachelor's degree based on my own interests." ([forward-college.eu/testimonials](https://forward-college.eu/testimonials/))

## What Students Say About the Academic Rigour

Forward College is not soft. The degrees are designed and examined by LSE (#3 in Europe) or King's College London (#6 in Europe) and awarded by the University of London. ([forward-college.eu/overview](https://forward-college.eu/overview/))

One student on EDUopinions was explicit:

> "Courses are designed and examined by LSE/King's College — difficult exams, lots of readings, writings and exercises." ([eduopinions.com/forward-college](https://www.eduopinions.com/universities/universities-in-france/forward-college/))

The same student acknowledged the flipped classroom in practice: preparation before sessions is real work, not optional. Seminars assume it.

Daniel (Class of 2024, now Junior Consultant at EY) on the payoff:

> "Forward gave me the skills and international experience to start my career at Ernst & Young, while feeling my degree was truly valued." ([forward-college.eu/testimonials](https://forward-college.eu/testimonials/))

Carolina (Class of 2025, now at the UN) on why she chose it:

> "I wanted to push myself within an environment of curious, passionate, and ambitious people from all over the world." ([forward-college.eu/testimonials](https://forward-college.eu/testimonials/))

## The Experience in One Sentence

Forward College is designed to be a three-year personal transformation — where the pedagogy, the living, the cities, and the cohort are all one curriculum — producing graduates who are analytically rigorous, emotionally intelligent, culturally fluent, and genuinely ready to lead.
