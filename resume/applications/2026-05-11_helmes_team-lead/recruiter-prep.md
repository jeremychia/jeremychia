## 1. What the recruiter is screening for

### Viability checklist

- **3+ years project management in software development** — Partially covered. 11 client projects at LucaNet (finance software), full roadmap ownership at Tourlane. Not traditional software dev PM. Flag proactively; reframe as delivery management across technical programmes.
- **Team management / motivating people** — Covered: 30 interns over 3 years (Soap Cycling), 3 team members onboarded (Tourlane), cross-functional workshop facilitation (Vinted).
- **Financial and budget management at unit level** — Covered: financial statement preparation and audit coordination (Soap Cycling board), billing controls recovering €1.6m (Vinted), project billing at LucaNet.
- **Scrum process management** — Gap. Not evidenced in resume. If asked, acknowledge it and note that all roles have operated in iterative delivery environments; no formal Scrum certification.
- **Estonian language (very good, written and spoken)** — Hard gap. Not covered. Acknowledge immediately; do not let it surface as a surprise.
- **Good communication / primary client contact** — Covered: client enablement at 4.5/5 satisfaction (LucaNet), cross-functional alignment (Vinted), conference speaking (Forward Data, Data Maker's Fest).

### Soft skill and culture fit signals

The JD uses language like "caring work environment", "freedom, trust, and responsibility", and "real impact" — this is a team that values autonomous operators who take genuine ownership, not people who need managing. The "inspire and develop team members" phrasing signals they want a leader who invests in people intrinsically, not as a checkbox. Mirror this by anchoring on outcomes and specific team development moments — not process adherence. Tone on the call: grounded and direct; avoid enthusiasm signalling.

### Likely hard question

**"This role requires very good Estonian — how do you see that working?"**

*Suggested response frame:* Name the gap directly: "I don't speak Estonian and that's a genuine constraint. What I'd want to understand is how much of the team and client interaction is Estonian-only versus English-workable — because that determines whether there's a realistic path here." This shows self-awareness and turns it into a practical conversation rather than an apology.

## 4. Company research findings

### Business model and unit economics

Helmes is an IT services and consulting firm operating on a project-based revenue model — custom software development, digital transformation, BI&Analytics (QLIK, Jedox, Databricks), AI, and legacy modernisation. Revenue comes from client contracts, typically multi-year. Their proprietary methodology, "The Helmes Way," claims 150% efficiency versus alternatives — a bold differentiation that underpins pricing power and client retention. Some relationships span 30+ years ([source](https://www.helmes.com/about/)). The Team Lead role is structurally a profit-centre owner: responsible for billing, reporting, client satisfaction, and — importantly — new opportunity development. This means team leads directly affect revenue, not just delivery.

### Competitive position

Helmes is an established Estonian IT services firm competing primarily with Nortal (Estonian origin, digital government and enterprise transformation), Tietoevry (Finnish, strong Baltic presence), and CGI (global, Baltic operations). Helmes differentiates through: the "Helmes Way" methodology and efficiency claim; long-term client relationships built on trust rather than price; and a strong domestic employer brand ("Dream Employer 2025" in Estonia, NPS 60+). They appear to position as a quality/retention play rather than cost-competitive — which means the team lead role is less about throughput and more about sustained client value. Recent expansion into Poland (Feb 2025) and Lithuania (TeleSoftas acquisition, Sep 2025) signals challenger ambition in the broader CEE market.

### Data stack and engineering culture

Helmes offers BI&Analytics as a service line using QLIK, Jedox, and Databricks across major cloud providers. In February 2026 they partnered with [Agile Data Engine](https://www.helmes.com/blog/) to enhance enterprise-scale data product delivery. This is service-line context, not internal stack. Engineering culture signals: ISO 27001/9001/14001 certified, emphasising process rigour and security-by-design. The "Helmes Way" methodology includes systematic gap analysis and business case documentation before development begins — suggesting they value structured delivery over speed.

### Data team size and structure

1,500 employees across 20 offices. The Team Lead role manages a software development team — size not specified. IT services firms typically structure teams as 5–15 developers per lead. Helmes's careers page emphasises "full autonomy to think, act, and take responsibility for their business" at team level — suggesting teams operate as semi-autonomous units with P&L accountability at the team lead level.

### Recent news and growth signals

- **February 2025:** Opened Warsaw office — entering Polish market ([blog](https://www.helmes.com/blog/))
- **September 2025:** TeleSoftas (Lithuania) rebranded as Helmes — Baltic market consolidation
- **February 2026:** Partnership with Agile Data Engine for data products
- **February 2025:** Named "Dream Employer 2025" in Estonia — strong domestic talent brand

Active geographic expansion: three new markets or acquisitions in 12 months. This puts pressure on team leads to operate with lower central support — autonomy is real, not rhetorical.

### Role-specific insight

The Team Lead role at Helmes is explicitly a hybrid: delivery manager + account manager + P&L owner. The JD lists "manage finances (billing, reporting)" and "seek new opportunities" in the same breath as team management. In IT services firms, this means team leads are expected to own client budgets, spot upsell opportunities, and contribute to sales pipeline — not just manage sprints. This is closer to a Partner track in consulting than a traditional engineering team lead.

### Suggested opening hook

> "Helmes expanded into three new markets in 12 months. That kind of pace puts real pressure on team leads to operate independently — which is the kind of autonomy I've built a career around."

## 6. Questions to ask

Pick 1–2 for the call; have the others ready if the conversation opens up.

### Impact / success definition

> "The JD mentions monitoring performance and continuously improving work methods. What does that actually look like in the first six months — is there an existing framework the team runs, or is the Team Lead expected to define what 'improved' means?"

### Team problem

> "You absorbed TeleSoftas in Lithuania in September 2025 and opened Warsaw in February. How does a Team Lead in Tallinn interact with teams across these new geographies — and what's the hardest coordination challenge that came with the expansion?"

### Business / strategy

> "The Helmes Way claims 150% efficiency versus alternatives. How does a Team Lead actually protect and demonstrate that claim to a client mid-project — is it measured per engagement, and how does it feed into billing conversations?"

### Ways of working

> "The JD mentions both developing client relationships and supporting sales and client acquisition. In practice, how does that split work — is the Team Lead leading the commercial conversation with existing clients, or are there dedicated sales people you hand off to?"

*Why specific questions work:* Generic questions ("what does success look like?") are forgettable. Questions that reference something real signal preparation, business understanding, and that you're already thinking about the role — not just trying to get it.

## 7. Call mechanics

- **Stand up** if it is a phone call — opens your chest, makes your voice more energetic.
- **Wait 2 seconds** after finishing a story — prevents rambling, gives recruiter time to finish notes.
- **Use "I" not "we"** — recruiters assess your individual contribution.
- **Salary / notice period** — state your range confidently. If pushed early: "I'm flexible depending on the full package — can you share the budgeted range for the role?"
