**Repetition = fear/desire:** "Data modeling" appears 3+ times (responsibilities + requirements + preferred). "Business requirements/logic/KPIs" appears 3+ times. **Culture signals:** No personality language. Very structured, requirements-driven JD. German-company directness — they value someone who works independently, doesn't need hand-holding, and communicates clearly across technical/non-technical audiences.

**Absence signals:** No mentorship, no team leadership, no stakeholder management titles — pure IC. No mention of Python/Spark/ML — this is SQL + dbt analytics engineering, not data science or data platform engineering.
