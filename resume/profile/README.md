# Profile

A repository of personal content used as raw material for resumes, cover letters, recruiter call prep, and interviews. Files here are meant to be picked from selectively — not used wholesale.

---

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [narrative.md](narrative.md) | Career arc in plain English — the "tell me about yourself" backbone |
| [star-stories.md](star-stories.md) | Pre-written STAR stories organised by theme, reusable across interviews |
| [motivations.md](motivations.md) | What energises you, what drains you, what you're optimising for in a next role |
| [values.md](values.md) | Non-negotiables and strong preferences — culture, management, work arrangement |
| [personality.md](personality.md) | Assessment results + your own commentary on how they land in practice |
| [working-style.md](working-style.md) | How you work best — team size, management style, ambiguity, feedback |
| [logistics.md](logistics.md) | Notice period, location, salary range, visa situation |

---

## What to write yourself

Everything here needs to come from you — no tool can infer the "why" behind your moves or what you genuinely care about. The highest-leverage files to fill in first:

1. **`narrative.md`** — the one story that connects every role
2. **`star-stories.md`** — real lived stories, organised by theme; these make recruiter prep far richer than anything derived from resume bullets
