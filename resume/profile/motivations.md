# Motivations

## What energises me
- Complex problems → elegant solutions
- Mastery and continuous learning; diving deep, building expertise
- Measurable, merit-based impact; tangible outcomes tied to effort
- Autonomy within clear guardrails
- Competent, direct collaborators who value logic over sentiment
- Fixing broken systems and inefficient processes
- Helping others improve through reliability and clear feedback — not emotional expression
- Growing practitioners: teaching frameworks + reasoning, not just tools; staying connected to see long-term impact
- Enabling impact at scale through others' capability, not just my own output

## What drains me
- Bureaucracy, ambiguous requirements, slow decisions
- People who take criticism personally
- Shallow work or constant task-switching without mastery
- Emotional labour over directness
- Vague or moving goalposts
- Extended high-stimulation social environments

## What I'm optimising for in my next role
1. **Autonomy** — clear goals, trust to execute without micromanagement
2. **Technical excellence** — hard problems, deepening expertise
3. **Clear communication norms** — directness valued over soft skills
4. **Impact at scale** — measurable outcomes; understand where team adds value in the org and how to amplify it
5. **Stability** — predictable direction, time to build mastery
6. **Competent collaborators** — ownership, high bar
7. **Growing people** — if in leadership: set others up by understanding their strengths and motivations

## Leadership style (if applicable)
- Set crisp expectations; make trade-offs and scope explicit
- Advocate fiercely for team accomplishments — make sure good work gets recognised
- Stay hands-on in domain; lead through technical judgment, not pure delegation
- Match people to problems based on what actually motivates them (mastery / impact / autonomy)
- Protect focus time; actively block endless meetings, vague roadmaps, politics
- Create psychological safety through directness — model that disagreement is okay
- **Not:** motivational speaker, conflict smoother, culture-through-outings builder
- **Watch for:** directness without context reads as harsh; lack of check-ins reads as indifference — be explicit it means trust
