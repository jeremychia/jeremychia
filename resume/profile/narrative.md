# Narrative

## Career arc

- Drive to understand systems deeply enough to improve them; gravitates to hard problems with direct impact
- Consistent thread: technical depth over breadth, clarity over consensus, merit over politics; judged on results not relationships
- Finance data specialisation: cost modelling, controls, reconciliation — built at scale, set standards, became go-to resource
- Cross-functional bridge at Vinted: combined deep expertise with genuine curiosity about how Finance, FinOps, Product, and Data Infra teams think — explained the *why*, not just the *what*
- Teaching crystallised this: impact grows when helping others think clearly about problems, not just solving them myself; stays connected across years to see how people apply what they learned
- Next: deep expertise in a high-impact domain, autonomy to shape solutions, collaborators who value truth and competence

## Why each move

| From → To | Why |
|---|---|
| Keppel Finance Analyst → LucaNet Consultant | From operational reporting into designing consolidation/budgeting systems across 11 listed companies. Wanted harder problems and broader credibility. |
| LucaNet Consultant → Master's + Tourlane | Hit ceiling with vendor tooling. Rebuilt foundations (SQL, Python, cloud). Tourlane: first role building systems from scratch — true technical depth. |
| Tourlane Data Analyst → Vinted Senior Analytics Engineer | Specialised into finance. Combined high-stakes system-building, measurable impact (€1.6m recovered through controls design), and expert recognition. Autonomy to set standards. |

## The one thread

Energised by solving hard problems well. Structure life to maximise time on actual work, minimise politics and consensus-building.
