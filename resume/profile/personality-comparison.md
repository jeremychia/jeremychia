# Personality Comparison: Insights Discovery 2024 vs 2026

**Headline:** your profile moved from a Red-led "Directing Reformer" to a Blue-led "Reforming Observer". In plain terms, the 2024 report describes someone who drives and directs. The 2026 report describes someone who analyses, reflects and keeps to himself. Underneath, the numbers changed less than the text suggests. Earth Green fell by about 0.7 points and Cool Blue rose by about 0.7. The other two colours barely moved.

**Two things to know before reading the text:**

- **The four colours always add up to about 13.7.** Insights uses forced-choice answers. A rise in one colour has to come out of another. "Blue up, Green down" is one shift, not two.
- **The less-conscious chart is a mirror of the conscious one.** Each less-conscious score equals 6 minus the opposite colour's conscious score. For example, 2026 less-conscious Red is 6 − 2.99 (conscious Green) = 3.01. This holds for all eight scores. So the less-conscious change is not a second, separate finding.

## 3. Possible causes

Ordered from most to least likely to explain the change. Several can be true at once.

### 3.1 Measurement: small changes look large

- **Forced-choice scoring.** Insights is ipsative, meaning scores are ranked against each other, not measured on their own. A small change in a few answers moves two colours at once (Hicks, 1970).
- **Category boundaries.** Your Red and Blue scores were close in both years (4.53 vs 4.27; 4.73 vs 4.93). A small swap in which one leads changes the type name and almost all the generated text. Jungian type tools are known for this. As many as half of people get a different Myers-Briggs type when they retake it ([Pittenger, 1993](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543063004467)).
- **Generated text.** The report is assembled from a large statement library. A different wheel position pulls different statements, so the prose changes more than the scores do.
- **Barnum effect.** Both reports will feel accurate, because general statements tend to feel personal ([Forer, 1949](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0059240)). This makes it hard to judge which report is "right" by reading it.

### 3.2 Situation: you took the tests in different roles

- **September 2024:** about a year into the shipping finance role. The [narrative](narrative.md) says you set standards and became the go-to resource there.
- **October 2026:** two months into the Finance DSA role, which started on 3 August 2026.

Several theories predict the shift from this alone:

- **Trait activation theory.** A situation brings out the traits it rewards ([Tett & Burnett, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.88.3.500)). An established expert is rewarded for directing. A newcomer is rewarded for learning the system.
- **Newcomer socialisation.** New joiners seek information, ask questions and hold back judgement until they understand the setting ([Morrison, 1993](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.78.2.173); [Bauer et al., 2007](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.92.3.707)). The 2026 text says almost this word for word: "learns through exploration, discussion and by asking searching questions".
- **Whole trait theory.** Personality is a range of behaviours, not a fixed point. A questionnaire mostly samples your recent behaviour ([Fleeson, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.80.6.1011)). Your recent behaviour has been a newcomer's.
- **Reference-group effect.** People rate themselves against the people around them ([Heine et al., 2002](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.82.6.903)). A new team gives you a new comparison group.

### 3.3 Life context: the transition between roles

Your resume ends the shipping finance role in March 2026. The new role started in August 2026. Career transitions use up time and energy.

- **Conservation of resources theory.** When resources are stretched, people protect what they have. They withdraw, narrow their focus and avoid new demands ([Hobfoll, 1989](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.44.3.513)). This matches the 2026 lines on withdrawal, boundaries and strain under changing expectations.
- **Fewer established relationships.** Earth Green covers harmony and support. That behaviour needs people you already know. Two months in, there are fewer of those relationships to draw on.

### 3.4 Deliberate change after the 2024 report

Your [personality notes](personality.md) list work on the 2024 weaknesses: "stepping on toes at scale" and "need to bring people along, not steamroll". If you consciously held back the directing style, Red-led behaviour would fade in your own answers.

- **Self-verification theory.** People answer in line with the self-image they hold now (Swann, 1983). Two years of acting on the 2024 report can change that self-image.

This explains the drop in "directing" language. It does not explain why Green fell rather than rose.

### 3.5 Real, long-term development

- **Maturity principle.** Between 20 and 40, most people become more conscientious and more agreeable ([Roberts, Walton & Viechtbauer, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.1.1)). Insights colours map only loosely to these traits. Blue rising fits more conscientiousness. Green falling goes against more agreeableness.
- **Social investment principle.** Committing to a new adult role changes personality over time (Roberts, Wood & Smith, 2005). Two months is too short for this to show.

So long-term development explains half of the change at best.

### 3.6 Jung's own framing

Insights maps Fiery Red to Jung's extraverted thinking and Cool Blue to introverted thinking. Your lead function moved from the outward-facing version to the inward-facing version. Jung called the face we adapt for the outside world the "persona". In 2024 your conscious and less-conscious positions differed. In 2026 they are the same. Insights reads that as less adapting to your setting. Because of the mirror arithmetic in section 1, treat this as a weak signal.

Your ISTP result in April 2026 also leads with introverted thinking. It agrees with the 2026 Insights result, and it was taken in the same transition period.
