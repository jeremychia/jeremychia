# Values

## Non-negotiables
- Truth and competence over politics — optics-first culture = exit
- Clear expectations and authority structure
- Merit-based advancement — results, not tenure or relationships
- Autonomy to do the work right — no forced half-measures or ignoring obvious problems
- Environment where people can be themselves; politics not treated as inevitable
- Kindness, fairness, meaningful contribution; space for downtime and reflection
- Investing in practitioners — code review, teaching the *why*, raising standards; valued as contribution, not side activity
- Impact at scale — help others develop judgment so impact compounds; not just solving for self

## Strong preferences

| Preference | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Remote / hybrid / in-person | Flexible | Remote or hybrid preferred |
| Company size | Mid-to-large (100+) | Scale for interesting problems |
| Industry | Tech-forward or data-heavy | Engineering and data matter strategically |
| Management style | Trust-based, direct | Autonomy, logic-valued, no constant reassurance |
| Team culture | Intellectually rigorous, low-drama | Quality-focused, head-on problem solving |

## Green flags
- Technical depth valued; celebrates shipping good solutions
- Leadership reasons through trade-offs openly instead of deciding by consensus
- "Here's the outcome, figure out how" autonomy + clear goals
- Growth path to recognised expertise or people leadership at scale
- High bar for quality; leadership distinguishes reversible vs. irreversible decisions
- Free-flowing feedback; disagreement welcomed; directness valued
- Actual measurable impact — not theatre or make-work
- Servant leadership modeled — leaders invest in reports' craft and career growth

## Red flags
- Managers who can't handle disagreement
- Decision-by-committee; constant scope re-negotiation
- Quantity over quality; technical debt as normal
- Heavy process/politics; managing up as core skill
- Feedback-avoidance; everyone supportive instead of honest
- Unclear growth path or static role
- Vague expectations ("passionate about X"; undefined success metrics)
